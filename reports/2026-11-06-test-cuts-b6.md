in ui/app: task-list, task-modal, task-props, decide, meetings, automations, bot-page, org-rules, redesign-org, ask-panel, thread-view,

tools) draws one markup at both widths, so one pass is enough. Where a script has `if (phone)` / `!phone` assertions it keeps both.
