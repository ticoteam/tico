Model picker, scores

Each Claude model and effort with its Artificial Analysis index, cost and time per task, shown where the choice is made. A proposal sample: nothing here is in the app.

The popover Proposed

Today's rows, plus three small numbers on each effort and a dot. The dot marks an option that no other model matches on index, cost and minutes at once. 8 of the 20 options have it. An option is tested against other models from every provider; a model's own efforts never rule each other out, as cost, time and index all rise with effort.

Model picks the family, and the chart joins that family's five efforts. The ring is the chosen option; filled marks are the dotted ones. The line is cost and index only, so a filled mark under it holds its place on minutes. Point at a mark, or tab to it, for its name and numbers.

Where the data and the app differ To settle

  • The app lists Fable 5.1. Artificial Analysis scores Fable 5.2 only, so the sample shows 5.2 under that name. No 5.1 numbers are made up.
  • The app's Default effort has no score. It stays in the list with "No score".
  • Artificial Analysis prints "Default Fallback" after the effort on Opus, Sonnet and Fable. Its meaning is not confirmed. The picker drops it; the rows below keep each name as printed.
  • Cost and minutes are those of Artificial Analysis's benchmark run, not what a task costs or takes in Tico.

Where each number comes from

One file, claude-pareto.json. Each figure in the sample is one of these fields.

  • Index: claude[].intelligence_index, shown to one decimal.
  • Cost: claude[].cost_per_task_usd, shown to the cent.
  • Min: claude[].minutes_per_task, shown to one decimal.
  • The dot, and a filled mark on the chart: claude[].on_3d_frontier. The rule is method_3d.
  • The chart line: frontier_cross_model, 20 points on cost and index, all providers. One of them costs $0 and sits on the left edge.
  • Option names: claude[].family and claude[].effort.
  • The other model in "Which one": all_points_with_cost[], the same three fields, by name.
  • Date and source: meta.fetched (2026-11-13) and meta.source, the Artificial Analysis models leaderboard.
