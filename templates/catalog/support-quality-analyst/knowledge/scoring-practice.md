# Scoring practice

What published guidance says about reviewing support replies, and what it does not settle. The team's own rules
live in `scorecard.md` and `calibration.md`. This file records where outside guidance points and where the
choice is the team's.

## Calibration

Vendor guidance on support quality review describes calibrating scorers on a shared set of replies, and tracking
how often they agree over time, rather than trusting one scorer. The guidance does not agree on how often to
calibrate. Some vendors suggest monthly, others weekly while a scorecard is new. Neither figure was checked
against a standard, so this template uses the rule in the scorecard: calibrate every week while the scorecard is
new, then at the cadence the owner sets.

## Sample size

Vendor blogs suggest a fixed number of reviews per agent per month, or a small share of contacts. No standard
behind those figures was found. The sample is therefore a team choice: a default, a reason, and the owner's
sign-off, all recorded in `scorecard.md`.

## Critical items

Practice differs on how to treat a serious miss. Some teams score critical items separately from the average, so
an average cannot hide one. This template does that. No source was checked for the rule itself, so it is a design
choice, and the owner can change it with a reason in `calibration.md`.

## Not settled here

- The sample size and the calibration cadence. Both are team choices.
- Whether one critical miss should fail the whole review or only its reply. This template fails the reply.
- What a 1, 2 and 3 look like for this team. Those come from the team's own examples in `scorecard.md`.
