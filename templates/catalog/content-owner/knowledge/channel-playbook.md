# Content playbook

How {{company_name}} runs its blog and social posts, and what the Content Owner may do by itself. This is the starter:
the Content Owner fills in the blanks at setup and publishes it as the page "Content playbook". To change a line, tell
the Content Owner on a task; it changes this file and publishes the page again.

## The basics

- **Channel tag:** `channel:content`. Every content task carries it.
- **KPI:** signups through a page or post. Read weekly from product analytics or the signup records, which must show the
  page or post each signup came through. It sits under the North Star.
- **Tools:** the public web. The site's repository or CMS and the team's social accounts when connected. Read is enough
  to start.
- **Who puts pieces live:** (filled at setup: the person who presses publish). Until a Tool lets the Content Owner
  publish, this person publishes each piece a person OK'd, and each next piece of a kept series.
- **What must never appear in public, and pieces that must never change:** (filled at setup).

## What Watch checks

Weekly, Mondays.

- Each kept series: the last piece went out on its date, and the next piece is ready for its date.
- Signups per series: signups that came through its pieces, last 7 days, against its range.
- The published pieces of each kept series still load, and their links work.
- The KPI: signups through a page or post, for last week.

## Where ideas come from

- Questions customers ask on calls, in tickets and in sales chats, kept in `knowledge/questions.md`. A question asked
  again and again is worth a piece.
- Pages and posts that bring signups: more like them. Pieces with readers but no signups: a better next step.
- What competitors publish that buyers read and we have no answer to (`tico market delta`).
- Kept series that were retired: what to try instead.
- An idea that moves another channel's number goes to that channel's Owner.

## Upkeep the Content Owner does by itself

No person's OK needed. Done within granted Tools, or filed with the exact change for the person who puts pieces live.

- Publish the next piece of a kept series that a person already OK'd as a series, in the voice and plan the person
  approved. Or file it, ready to post, for the person who publishes.
- Fix a broken link, or a wrong date or figure, in a published piece, with its new source.
- Keep `knowledge/series.md` and `knowledge/questions.md` current: each series, its schedule and what went out; each
  question, where it was asked and when.

## Writing for other Owners

Other Owners file writing sub-tasks to the Content Owner: the SEO Owner's bet that needs new pages, say. The sub-task
is the Content Owner's. It writes the piece in the team's voice, checks every claim, and hands the draft back on the
sub-task. The bet, its Go-live page and its number stay with the Owner who filed it.

## What needs a person

- A new series, a new kind of piece, or a one-off piece: that is a bet. A person OKs its Go-live page first.
- A change to a kept series: its topic, its voice, its schedule or where it goes out. That is an idea, not upkeep.
- Anything on the "never" list above.
- Anything sent outside the company: an interview request, a reply to a reader, a pitch to another site.

## Never

- Invent a number, a customer quote or a result.
- Use a customer's words without their agreement.
- Promise signups, traffic or reach.
- Write a number without its dated source.
