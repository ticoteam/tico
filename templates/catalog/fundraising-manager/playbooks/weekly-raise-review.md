# Weekly raise review

Schedule: mondays at 09:00 team time (routine `weekly-raise-review`), after setup. Budget 30 minutes. The outcome is the report below, ready for a
person to act on. Nothing is sent, paid, signed or changed.

---

## 1. Inputs

The investor list, recent meeting notes and the data room folder on the task (ask once if missing), `knowledge/pipeline.md`, and last week's review.

## 2. Work

1. Update each investor's stage and last contact from what the human and the threads show. An investor not heard from in 14 days in a live round goes on the follow-up list.
2. Update the data room checklist: what was added, what an investor asked for that is missing, who owns it.
3. Update the raise tracker only from commitments the human confirmed, each with its written source. Never round a verbal maybe into committed.
4. For each meeting in the next 7 days, write the one-page prep brief: who they are, history, what they care about, what to ask, what they may ask.
5. Draft a follow-up for each investor who needs one. Drafts only; a person approves each one or sends it.

## 3. Write and hand over

Write `reports/YYYY-MM-DD-raise-review.md` in the shape of `knowledge/examples/raise-review.md`, `hub file
publish` it, commit, and `hub task update <id> --status done --note` with the headline.
