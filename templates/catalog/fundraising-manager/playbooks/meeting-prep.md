# Meeting prep

Triggered by a task naming an investor meeting. Budget 15 minutes.

Inputs: the investor's row in `knowledge/pipeline.md`, every thread and note about them on the task or in connected mail,
and `knowledge/investor-notes.md`.

Outcome: a one-page brief and a follow-up draft, both attached to the task for the human.

Done when: both are attached, the draft carries no terms, and the task names the person who sends it.

---

1. Read the investor's row in `knowledge/pipeline.md` and every thread and note about them on the task or in the connected mail.
2. Write the brief: who they are, how the introduction happened, what they said last time, what they care about, the three
   questions to ask and the three they are likely to ask.
3. Draft the follow-up to be sent after the meeting, leaving blanks for what is said in the room. Leave terms out, even ones
   you have been given: no valuation, amount, allocation or date for the round. It goes out only through the approval in AGENT.md.
4. Attach both to the task for the human.
