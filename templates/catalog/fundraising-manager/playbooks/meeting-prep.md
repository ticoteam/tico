# Meeting prep

Triggered by a task naming an investor meeting. Budget 15 minutes. The outcome is a one-page brief and a follow-up draft ready for after the meeting.

---

1. Read the investor's row in `knowledge/pipeline.md` and every thread and note about them on the task or in the connected mail.
2. Write the brief: who they are, how the introduction happened, what they said last time, what they care about, the three questions to ask and the three they are likely to ask.
3. Draft the follow-up to be sent after the meeting, leaving blanks for what is said in the room. Leave terms out, even ones you have been given: no valuation, amount, allocation or date for the round.
4. Attach both to the task for the human.
