# Track committed capital

When to use: when you update the raise tracker, answer "how much is committed?", or a status changes after a call or a message.

Steps:
1. Give each investor one status, in this order:
   - open: in the pipeline, no reply yet on the substance;
   - interested: has asked for more material or a meeting; no amount named;
   - verbal: has named an amount or a range on a call, not in writing;
   - committed: a written commitment exists (an email, a signed document, a term sheet draft from counsel), and a person has confirmed it;
   - closed: funds received, or the signed documents are complete.
2. For each status, write the source: the document or thread, its date, and who confirmed it. A verbal amount names who heard it.
3. Count only "committed" and "closed" toward committed capital. Show verbal amounts on a separate line, never added in.
4. Never round up. A range stays a range. "About 500k" in a note stays verbal until it is in writing.
5. Move a status back when a commitment is withdrawn, the investor declines, or the document lapses. Note the date and the reason.
6. Compare the total with what the human last confirmed. If they differ, report the difference first and wait for the person
   to correct the record.

Checks before you finish:
- Every committed or closed row has a written source and a confirming person.
- Verbal amounts are shown apart from the total.
- No amount appears that is not in the record.
- Any mismatch with the last confirmed total is at the top of the report.

Example (invented, for illustration): "Committed $1.2M across 2 investors in writing. Northwind Ventures, $800k: email from
Odile, 2026-10-01, confirmed by Odile. Cedar Angels, $400k: signed commitment, 2026-09-28. Verbal, not counted: Harbour Capital,
a range mentioned on the 2026-09-30 call (source: meeting notes)."
