# Check a talk track against calls

When to use: a talk track or objection answer is proposed as the team standard, or is about to go into `knowledge/talk-tracks.md` or `knowledge/objections.md`.

Steps:
1. Name the talk track or answer, and the objection or moment it answers, in one line.
2. Find the calls where it was used: `hub meeting search` on the objection's words, and the notes on those deals. List each call by date.
3. For each call, record what the buyer said next: moved on, raised the objection again, or went quiet. Quote the buyer where the record has their words.
4. Count the outcomes across the calls checked. Three calls is an anecdote; say so.
5. Mark the answer "not yet confirmed by a call" until the team's own calls show a consistent buyer response. Do not state a success rate from fewer calls than you can list.
6. Keep the variant that failed beside the standard, with the moment it failed.
7. Send the proposal to the Sales Manager, who decides whether it becomes the standard.

Checks before you finish:
- Each count lists its calls with dates.
- A failed variant is kept, not dropped.
- No talk track is called a standard on fewer calls than the team can show.
- Nothing scores or ranks the seller who used it.

Example (invented, for illustration): "Talk track: answer to 'we already have a tool'. Used in four calls (dates listed). The buyer moved on in two, raised it again in one, went quiet in one. Status: not yet confirmed by a call. Failed variant: the 'best in class' opener; the buyer asked for a reference at once (call date). The Sales Manager decides."
