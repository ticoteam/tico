# Check a review request for compliance

When to use: before any review invitation, reminder or request batch is drafted for sending, and when a sales or
success note proposes asking a customer for a review.

Steps:
1. Confirm the list rule. Every customer at the milestone in `knowledge/surfaces.md` is in the batch, and nobody
   is added or left out because of how they seem to feel. Write the milestone and the date it was checked.
2. Confirm the reward rule. Any reward is the same for every reviewer, paid by the platform's program, and not tied
   to a rating or a wording. A reward for a review that is negative or positive is banned.
3. Confirm the disclosure. A paid or incentivised review carries the platform's label. The invitation says it will.
4. Check the wording. No suggested content, no mention of stars or ratings, no keywords or service names to include,
   and no request to change or remove an earlier review.
5. Check the platform's current policy page for each surface and write its date beside the surface in
   `knowledge/surfaces.md`. If the page cannot be read, the surface is "blocked", and nothing is sent to it.
6. Check the team's own staff. No staff member is given a number of reviews to collect.
7. Mark each line in the batch: compliant, changed (with the new wording) or stopped (with the rule it breaks).

Checks before you finish:
- Every customer in the batch meets the same milestone, and the list source is named.
- The reward, if any, is the platform program's, and the same for every reviewer.
- No wording suggests what to write.
- Each surface has a policy date no older than the last batch, or it is blocked.
- Stopped items are listed with their rule, so the next batch does not repeat them.

Example (invented, for illustration): "Customers at the 90-day milestone: 14 (list from the success team, 2026-10-30).
Reward: the platform's gift card, same for all (program page checked 2026-10-30). Wording change: 'please mention the
setup speed' removed; replaced with the standard neutral invitation. Stopped: 2 accounts added by a sales note that
asked only for 'happy' customers, breaks the milestone rule."
