# Test an ad claim before launch

When to use: before any new ad variant, headline, landing-page block or public copy goes live, and before a
review proposes copy that makes a number, a guarantee or a comparison.

Steps:
1. List every claim in the copy, one per line: each number, each comparison, each "only", "free", "guaranteed",
   "best", each testimonial, each named company or product.
2. For each claim, write its basis: the dated source, the baseline and period for a saving, the rating's source,
   the terms for a guarantee. A claim with no basis is removed or rewritten to what can be shown.
3. Check the wording against the platform's ad policy (for example, a guarantee needs its terms on the page; a
   competitor's name is not used without a permitted reason).
4. Classify the ad: housing, credit, employment, health, political or general. A restricted category changes the
   targeting, and sometimes the wording. Write the category before the targeting.
5. For a testimonial, check consent and that the quote is verbatim with its date. A composite or edited quote is
   not a testimonial.
6. Mark each line: supported (with its source), changed (with the new wording), or removed.
7. Record the approved copy and its sources in `knowledge/changes.md` before it is published.

Checks before you finish:
- No claim is left without a basis, and every basis has a date.
- Savings name their baseline and period. Percentages name what they are a percentage of.
- Guarantees link to their terms, or they are gone.
- The category is written, and the targeting matches it.
- Each removed claim is listed, so nobody restores it from an old draft.

Example (invented, for illustration): "Ad line 'Save 30% on fees': no baseline in the source. Changed to 'Fees of 3.9% of rent, against 8% to 10% for a typical manager (pricing page, 2026-10-02; competitor rates from their public pages, 2026-10-05)' with both sources listed. The line 'Guaranteed rent in 30 days' is removed: no terms exist to link."
