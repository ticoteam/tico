# Renewal and notice practice

Reference for the duties register and the renewal check. This is general practice, not law, and no vendor
has to follow it. Read the contract in front of you before you act on any figure here.

## What this template assumes

- The date that matters is the notice deadline, not the renewal date. Many contracts let the team cancel or
  change terms only up to a notice date set before renewal.
- Each contract has one named owner, and the register states the cost of missing its notice deadline.
- The first check comes well before the notice deadline. Practice notes often suggest about 90 days, but they
  disagree and none gives a universal figure. Use the lead time in `knowledge/rhythm.md` when it is longer.
- A missed notice deadline usually means automatic renewal, often on new terms or at a new price. Check the
  automatic-renewal and price-change clauses.
- A deadline recorded from memory, or from a vendor email that is not the contract, is a marked gap until the
  contract confirms it.
