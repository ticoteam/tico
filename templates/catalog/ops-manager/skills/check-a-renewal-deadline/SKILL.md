# Check a renewal deadline

## When to use

Read when a contract, licence, insurance policy, domain or subscription is added to the register, when a renewal date
appears in a task or an email, and when a renewal comes into its lead time on the weekly page.

## Steps

1. Find the document itself: the contract, the policy schedule, the renewal notice or the vendor's account terms.
   Note where you read it and the date you read it.
2. Find the notice clause: how long before renewal, or by which date, the team must act, and how notice has to be
   sent. Record the notice deadline as a date. Work it out from the renewal date only when the document gives the
   rule, and show both in the row.
3. Find the automatic-renewal clause: does it renew by itself, for how long, and at what price? Record any
   price-change rule the document states.
4. If the clause or the date is missing, write "not found in the document read" and mark the gap. Do not fill it in
   from memory, from another vendor's terms, or from a summary you cannot trace to the contract.
5. Name one owner. With no owner, "needs an owner" goes to the top of the weekly page.
6. Set the first check. It is no later than the lead time in `knowledge/rhythm.md`, and always well before the notice
   deadline. `knowledge/renewal-practice.md` has the background.
7. Add the row to `knowledge/duties.md`: what, owner, notice deadline, renewal date, the source and the date read.
8. When the lead window opens, say so on the weekly page with the cost of missing the deadline, if the document states
   one. Route the decision to the owner. A contract already in place goes to `vendor-manager` for review.

## Checks before you finish

- The notice deadline is a date, with its clause and the date read beside it.
- The automatic-renewal outcome is stated, not assumed.
- Each gap is marked "not found" and named on the page.
- Nothing was signed, renewed or cancelled. Only the owner decides.

## Example

Invented example. A cleaning contract renews on 2027-04-30 (a Friday). Clause 9, read 2026-11-01, asks for notice
60 days before renewal, so the notice deadline is 2027-03-01 (a Monday). Clause 9.2 renews the contract for a year
unless notice is given. Owner: Sam Reyes. The register's lead time is 90 days, so the first check is 2026-12-01.
The row reads: "Cleaning contract, Sam Reyes, notice by 2027-03-01 (clause 9, read 2026-11-01)."
