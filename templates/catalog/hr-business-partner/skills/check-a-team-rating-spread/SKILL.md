# Check a team's rating spread

When to use: when manager ratings are in, for the team-level part of the calibration sheet and the calibration flags in the weekly
tracker.

Steps:
1. Take the grid the HR owner attached: reference, team, level, rating, and whether the review cites evidence (yes, partly, no).
   Keep only those fields. Do not keep names, pay or notes.
2. Count each rating point by team and by level, and for the whole company.
3. Merge any team or level cell with fewer than five reviews into "other" for the team-level view. Never show a distribution for a
   group under five.
4. For each team, compare its spread with the company's. A team far from the whole is a question for its manager, written as a
   question, not a correction.
5. Flag a team where every review sits on one point of the scale. Flag a rating at either end of the scale with no evidence cited.
6. Flag wording that does not match the rating: two reviews with the same rating described very differently, or different ratings
   described in the same words. Refer to the review by reference; do not quote it.
7. Order the flags by how many reviews they cover, largest first. Keep at most ten.
8. Write the result in the team section of the calibration sheet, using team names and references only.

Checks before you finish:
- No cell shows fewer than five reviews.
- Every flag names a team or level and the number of reviews it covers.
- No flag says a rating is right or wrong.
- The grid is deleted locally once the sheet is attached.

Example (invented, for illustration): "34 reviews on a 1-to-4 scale: 2 at 1, 8 at 2, 14 at 3, 10 at 4. Team C (8 reviews): all 8 at
4, with no evidence cited in 3 of them. Flag: team C at one point with partial evidence. Question for its manager: how do the 4
ratings read against the definition on page one of the sheet? Team D (4 reviews) merged into other."
