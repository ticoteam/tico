# Write a conversation note (SBI)

When to use: when a manager asks for help preparing a conversation about performance, expectations or a missed goal. Not for conduct,
complaints, health, leave or a possible dismissal: those go to the employee relations owner untouched (see AGENT.md).

Steps:
1. Confirm the topic is performance or an expectation. If it touches conduct, a complaint, health, leave or a dismissal, stop and
   route it as AGENT.md says. Write nothing more about it.
2. Gather the facts from the manager's own words on the task and any dated examples: what happened, when, where.
3. Write the Situation: when and where, in one or two sentences, without describing the person.
4. Write the Behavior: what was done or not done, in terms a witness could check. Leave out trait words ("careless", "negative").
5. Write the Impact: the effect on the work, the team, a customer or a deadline, as the manager saw it, with its source.
6. Write the expectation: the standard the role or goal sets, referred to by its document or goal reference.
7. Add the support offered (a plan, a meeting, a tool, training) and the follow-up date. A note with no follow-up date is not
   finished.
8. Point to the policy page the manager should read first (`hub doc ask`). Do not say what should be decided, what the outcome
   should be, or what the person will receive.
9. List the open questions the manager should answer before the meeting, as a short list.

Checks before you finish:
- Each of the four parts is present, and each is a fact or an expectation, not a judgment.
- The note has no outcome, no rating and no suggested consequence.
- A follow-up date is set.
- The person is referred to by the task's reference, unless the manager's own name is needed.

Example (invented, for illustration): "Situation: the release review on Tuesday 22 September. Behavior: the release notes went out
two hours after the agreed time, and checklist items 3 and 5 were blank. Impact: support answered three customer questions
without the notes. Expectation: notes ready 24 hours before a release, as the release process page sets out. Support offered: a
shared template and a 15-minute check on Monday. Follow-up: 6 October. Read first: the release process page."
