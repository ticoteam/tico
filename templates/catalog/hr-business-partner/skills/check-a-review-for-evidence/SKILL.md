# Check a review for evidence

When to use: for each review attached to a calibration or pack task, before the review counts as checked in a calibration sheet
or a pack.

Steps:
1. Find the review by its reference on the task. Take the scale from `knowledge/rating-scale.md`. If the scale has no written
   meaning for the rating given, record "no definition" for that item and move on.
2. For each rated item, list the examples the review cites: date, source (goal, shipped work, customer note, one-to-one note) and
   what happened, in one line.
3. Mark each item: "evidenced" (at least one dated example that matches the item), "partly" (an example that is undated or does not
   match the item), or "no evidence" (none cited).
4. Check the wording. Flag descriptions of personality or style ("abrasive", "not a culture fit", "a natural leader") with no
   example. Flag comparisons with named colleagues.
5. Check for content that is not about work: health, family, leave, a protected characteristic, a complaint or a conduct matter.
   Do not read further, summarise or quote it. Name the reference and the kind of content, and send the review to the employee
   relations owner in `state.md` the same day, untouched.
6. Write one line per review: reference, items evidenced, partly and with no evidence, wording flags, and any employee relations
   handover. Do not say whether a rating is right or wrong.
7. Attach the list to the task. Keep review text out of the page and out of `reports/`.

Checks before you finish:
- Every item has one of the three marks and, for "evidenced", a named dated example.
- No rating is called too high or too low, and no outcome is suggested.
- Employee relations content is named by kind and handed over, not quoted.
- Each line uses the review's reference, not the person's name.

Example (invented, for illustration): "Review R-118, team B, level 3. Items: delivery evidenced (two dated examples: goal G-22 and a
shipped release, July); communication partly (one example, undated); collaboration no evidence. Wording: 'difficult in meetings'
has no example, flagged. No employee relations content. Attached to T-520."
