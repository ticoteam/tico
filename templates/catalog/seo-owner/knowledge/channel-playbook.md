# SEO playbook

How {{company_name}} runs SEO, and what the SEO Owner may do by itself. This is the starter: the SEO Owner fills in the
blanks at setup and publishes it as the page "SEO playbook". To change a line, tell the SEO Owner on a task; it
changes this file and publishes the page again.

## The basics

- **Channel tag:** `channel:seo`. Every SEO task carries it.
- **KPI:** organic clicks, brand terms left out. Read weekly in Search Console. It sits under the North Star.
- **Tools:** the public web. Search Console and the site's repository or CMS when connected. Read is enough to start.
- **Who puts changes live:** (filled at setup: the person who edits the site). Until a Tool lets the SEO Owner change
  the site, this person applies each change a person OK'd.
- **Pages that must never change:** (filled at setup).

## What Watch checks

Weekly, Mondays.

- Each watched page set: organic clicks and average rank for its pages, last 7 days against its range.
- The tracked buyer questions in `knowledge/prompts.md`, asked in the AI assistants the team uses: is
  {{company_name}} named, is one of our pages cited.
- The pages in each watched set still load, can be indexed, and keep their title and description.
- The KPI: organic clicks, brand terms left out, for last week.

## Where ideas come from

- Search Console: pages and questions with many views but few clicks, and pages that fell.
- Buyer questions where an AI assistant names a competitor and not us.
- What competitors rank for or are cited for that we have no page on (`tico market delta`).
- Pages in `knowledge/pages.md` that do not answer their buyer question.
- Watched page sets that were retired: what to try instead.

## Upkeep the SEO Owner does by itself

No person's OK needed. Done within granted Tools, or filed with the exact change for the person who puts changes live.

- Fix a broken link, a missing or duplicate title or description, or a page that stopped loading, in a watched page set.
- Put back a title, description or paragraph a person OK'd that was changed or lost.
- Update a date or a figure on a watched page when its source changed, with the new source.
- Keep `knowledge/prompts.md` and `knowledge/pages.md` current. A change to the tracked questions is a proposal, never
  an edit, so the trend keeps its meaning.

## What needs a person

- Any new page, new copy or new section: that is a bet. A person OKs its Go-live page first.
- Anything on the "never change" list above.
- Anything sent or submitted outside the company: a link request, a listing, a directory.

## Never

- Buy, request or swap links.
- Promise a rank or a mention in an AI answer.
- Write a number without its dated source.
