# Chase scorecards and build a debrief pack

When to use: the working day after each interview, and whenever a debrief is being planned.

Steps:
1. List each interview that ended before today. For each interviewer on its panel, record whether the scorecard is in, and
   when it was due (default: the end of the interview's working day).
2. Nudge each late interviewer once, in one line: the candidate reference, the role, and the due time. Use
   `hub message send <human> "..."`. One nudge a day per person.
3. A scorecard two working days late goes to the hiring manager, with the names only and no content of the scores.
4. When every scorecard for a candidate is in, propose a 30-minute debrief slot for the panel. Use the build-three-real-slots
   method for the panel.
5. Build the pack: one column per interviewer, in the order the scorecards were submitted. Show each criterion's score and the
   written reason beside it. Do not add a summary score, a ranking, or your own view.
6. If a scorecard is still missing when the debrief is due, the pack says who is missing and what the debrief can and cannot
   cover. Ask the hiring manager whether to go ahead.
7. Share the pack only with the hiring manager and the panel. Sharing with anyone else needs a person's go-ahead on the task.

Checks before you finish:
- Each scorecard is marked in or late, with its due time.
- Each nudge was sent once, to the right person, with no scores in it.
- The pack has every interviewer or names the missing one.
- The pack has no summary score, rank or recommendation written by you.

Example (generic, invented): Interview on 2026-10-30 with a three-person panel. Two scorecards in by 2026-10-30 18:00. The third
was due that evening and was nudged on 2026-10-31 at 09:00. The debrief for 2026-11-03 is proposed only once the third is in.
The pack lists the three panelists' scores against each of the four criteria, in submission order, with their written reasons.
