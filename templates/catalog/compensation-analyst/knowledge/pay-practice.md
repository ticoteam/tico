# Pay practice

Standing reference for pay transparency, bands and pay equity checks. The team's own philosophy and bands live in
`philosophy.md` and `bands.md`; this file holds general practice, the law as it stood when read, and the sources. Where the
two disagree, the team's own signed policy wins, and the law wins over both.

## Pay transparency (changed recently)

- **European Union.** Directive (EU) 2023/970 on pay transparency had a transposition deadline of 7 June 2026. The European
  Commission confirmed in December 2025 that the date would not move (law-firm summaries, read 2026-11-01). Several member
  states had not finished their national laws on that date. Check the national law of each country where people work.
- **Joint pay assessment (EU, Article 10).** Commentary read 2026-11-01 describes the duty this way: if an unexplained gap of
  5 percent or more in pay between women and men exists in any category of workers, and it is not fixed within six months,
  the employer must carry out a joint pay assessment with worker representatives. The official text was not read. Confirm
  the wording and the category rules before a decision depends on them.
- **United States, job postings.** Federal law does not require a pay range in a posting. State and some city laws do, and
  many require a good-faith range for the job. Trackers in 2026 disagree on the count (summaries read 2026-11-01 give 13 to 18
  states plus the District of Columbia), so use the current list from each state's labour agency. Some laws also cover
  internal openings. Many count a remote role as covered when the job can be done from that state.

## Bands

- A band is set for a role family and a level, not for a person. Its minimum, midpoint and maximum come from the philosophy:
  the market median for pay at market, a higher point for roles the team leads on.
- The **compa-ratio** is the proposed pay divided by the midpoint. Read it with the band's spread and the peers' positions,
  never alone.
- A band needs a source and a date. Fewer than three data points, or a source older than a year, makes it provisional.
- Public ranges are a source of last resort for a band. They show what employers advertise, not what they pay.

## Pay equity checks

- Report only groups of five or more people. Combine smaller groups, and do not stack filters that shrink a group below five.
  Five is a common convention in employee surveys (vendor practice, read 2026-11-01); the team's own threshold may be higher.
- Compare like with like: the same job family and level first, then location and tenure. Say which factors were held constant.
- A performance rating as a control can carry the bias the check is looking for. Leave it out unless the owner decides
  otherwise and writes down why.
- Call the part a listed factor cannot explain "unexplained", and state its size, the method and the group sizes. It is a
  question for a human, not a finding of discrimination.
