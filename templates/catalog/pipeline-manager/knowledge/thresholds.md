# Thresholds

Starting defaults until setup replaces them. A card past a threshold is stuck and is named in the weekly report.

| Stage kind | Stuck after |
|---|---|
| Idea, targets or on-ice stage | 7 days |
| Working stage (doing, running, talking) | 3 days |
| Review stage (waiting for a reviewer or a decision) | 2 days |
| Waiting for room (full next stage) | 2 days |

Time on hold (a hold to a date) does not count toward a threshold. A card with no owner is stuck from the day it enters
the stage.

## Sources
- Starting defaults, chosen for a team that reviews its pipeline weekly. Replaced at setup.
