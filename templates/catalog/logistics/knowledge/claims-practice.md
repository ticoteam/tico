# Claims and carrier deadlines

How a lost or damaged shipment claim is prepared and when it must be filed. The team's own carrier terms in
`knowledge/carriers.md` override anything general here.

## Deadlines

- Each carrier sets its own claim window in its terms. The window can depend on the carrier, the service, and the country
  of shipment. It can run from the ship date, the delivery date, or the date a loss is reported.
- Secondary guides for the major national carriers, read 2026-11-01, give windows from 21 days to nine months, counted
  from different dates for different services. The guides disagree with each other, and none was checked against the
  carrier's own terms. Do not quote a window from them.
- Read the window from the carrier's terms for the service in use, and record the date you read it. If the terms cannot be
  found, mark the claim "deadline not found" and ask the carrier in writing.

## Evidence carriers usually ask for

No primary source was read for this list. It is general practice; check it against each carrier's claim form.

- The order value or commercial invoice.
- Proof of shipment: the collection or acceptance scan, with its time.
- The last scan, with its time.
- For damage: photographs of the goods and the packaging, taken on receipt, with the date.

## Practice

- File early, once the evidence is complete. The 7-day margin in the weekly report is the team's own, not a carrier term.
- A settlement offer is a decision for the owner named in `knowledge/carriers.md`.
