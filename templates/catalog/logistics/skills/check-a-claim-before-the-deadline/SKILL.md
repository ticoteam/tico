# Check a claim before the deadline

When to use: a shipment is lost or damaged, or a claim is waiting to be filed or answered. Read `knowledge/claims-practice.md`
for what carriers usually ask for and why windows differ.

Steps:
1. Name the claim: order, carrier, service, tracking number and type (lost or damaged). A damage claim also needs the
   customer's photos and the date they were received.
2. Find the deadline in the carrier's own claim terms for that service and country. Record the clause or page and the date
   you read it, with the deadline date. If you cannot find it, write "deadline not found" and ask the carrier's claim
   contact through the task. Do not fill in a window from memory, from a guide, or from a default.
3. Assemble the evidence: the order value or commercial invoice, the proof of shipment (the collection scan with its
   time), the last scan with its time, and any photos. Name each missing item.
4. Check the value against the carrier's own limits: a declared or insured value, and any exclusion the terms name. Record
   the amount claimed and the limit it is checked against.
5. Set the file date. File once the evidence is complete, and no later than 7 days before the deadline. A claim with a
   missing item is filed with the gap named, not held back. Filing needs mail sending on in Tico, a task that asks for it
   and a granted Tool. Otherwise draft the claim on the task for a person.
6. Write the claim in `knowledge/claims.md`: shipment, value, evidence, filed date, deadline with the date read, outcome.
   If the deadline is within 7 days, the claim goes at the top of the weekly report.
7. When the carrier replies, record its decision and date. A settlement offer goes to the owner as a decision. Accepting
   one is gated in `AGENT.md`.

Checks before you finish:
- The deadline has its source, the clause or page, and the date read; or it is marked "deadline not found".
- Every claim within 7 days of its deadline is at the top of the report.
- The evidence is listed, with each missing item named.
- Nothing was filed, withdrawn or settled outside the gate in `AGENT.md`.

Example (invented, for illustration): "Order 4389, lost, carrier Parcelway, value $146.00 (order record read 2026-09-28).
Collection scan 2026-09-08 09:40; no scan since 2026-09-10. Deadline 2026-10-02, from the carrier's claim terms
(section 3, read 2026-09-28). Evidence: order record, collection scan, last scan. Missing: none. Ready to file; at the top
of the report with 4 days left."
