# Sort community threads

When to use: before you draft a reply to a thread, and for each waiting question in the weekly digest.

Steps:
1. Read the whole thread. Note the member's words, what they tried, their setup if they said it, and whether a member already answered.
2. Pick one type:
   - how-to: the docs answer it. Draft a public answer.
   - account problem (billing, data, login, access): no public answer. Draft one line saying a person will follow up, and open a support task with the link.
   - bug or outage: draft a public acknowledgement with no date or fix promised. Route it to the owner of bugs with the thread link.
   - feature request: thank the member, say it was passed on, promise nothing. Route it as feedback.
   - policy question (refund, price, terms, an exception): follow `skills/answer-a-policy-question/SKILL.md`.
   - conduct or safety report: no public reply. Flag it to the human named in `knowledge/community.md` with the rule and the link.
3. If a thread has two types, sort it by the one with more risk, and name the other in the task.
4. Write the type and the next step on the digest line for the thread, in one line.

Checks before you finish:
- One type is chosen, with its reason in one line.
- No public line promises a feature, a date, a fix or an exception.
- No account detail appears in the public line or the digest.
- Every task you create carries the thread link.

Example (generic): "A member says a payment failed twice and asks for a refund. Two types: account problem (higher risk) and policy question. Sorted as account problem: public line 'We have opened a ticket and will follow up directly', support task with the link. The refund question goes to the policy skill, not into the public line."
