# Explain a variance

## When to use
When a balance or category moved past the review limit in `knowledge/close-practice.md`, against the prior month, the same month last year, or the budget where the team keeps one.

## Steps
1. Pick the line: account or category, period, amount, and percent change against the prior month and the same month last year.
2. Apply the limit. A movement needs an explanation when it is over the percent limit and over the amount limit, both. The house defaults are in `knowledge/close-practice.md`; the Head of Finance can change them in the checklist.
3. Find the drivers. Split the movement into the entries, invoices, payroll runs, or accruals behind it. Group by counterparty and by document. Usually three drivers explain most of the movement.
4. Match each driver to a document: an invoice, a contract, a payroll register, a bank line. Record the document's name and date.
5. Classify each driver: timing (reverses next period), one-off (will not recur), or run-rate (will recur, so the forecast needs changing). Say which.
6. Write one or two sentences per driver. Then the residual: the part of the movement no document covers. The residual is an open item for an owner, with its amount.
7. If a driver looks like an error and not a real change, say so and run `skills/review-the-books-for-unusual-entries/SKILL.md` on it.

## Checks before you finish
- Explained amounts plus the residual equal the total movement.
- Every driver names a document and a date. None says "probably".
- Each driver has its classification.
- Nothing is reclassified or adjusted by you. Proposed changes go to the accountant.

## Example (invented)
Software expenses, October 218,400 against September 158,250: +60,150 (+38%). Over both limits (10% and 5,000).

- Two annual renewals expensed in October: 41,000 (invoice 2026-10-02) and 14,500 (invoice 2026-10-09). Timing. Proposed to the accountant: move the unexpired part to prepaid expenses.
- Residual: 4,650. Unexplained. Owner: payables.

Total explained: 55,500. Residual 4,650. Check: 55,500 + 4,650 = 60,150.
