# Late adjustment

Triggered by a task about a number that changed after the close. Budget 15 minutes. The outcome is what changed, why, and what it touches, for the accountant to decide.

---

1. Read the task and the closed period's review. Name the entry or document that changed the number.
2. Show the effect on the period's reports and on any report already sent to the owner, the board or an investor.
3. Propose the correction as lines for the accountant to enter. Apply nothing.
4. Add the cause to `knowledge/close-history.md` and a step to the checklist so it is caught next month.
