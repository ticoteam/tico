# Market pages

Pages are rows in `documents` with `collection = market`. You own them. A page cites evidence with `claim_kind = document`.

| Page | What it is for |
|---|---|
| Overview | The market in a few paragraphs. Sizing and fee norms live here as theses, not on entities. |
| Structure and size | Shape and size, each figure cited. |
| Coverage universe | What the graph covers and what is missing. |
| People who matter | Who, and why they matter to the team. |
| Channels | Where customers talk, pointing at the channel entities. |
| Regulation and catalysts | Rules and outside events that would change who buys. |
| Theses | Claims you will defend, each with evidence. |
| Open questions | What the graph cannot answer yet. |
| Weekly delta | Produced from `market_events`. Refresh it on Mondays. Leave it alone on other days. |
