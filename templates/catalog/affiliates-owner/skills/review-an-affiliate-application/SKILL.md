# Review an affiliate application

When to use: for each new application on a task, before any decision is proposed or any reply is drafted.

Steps:
1. Read the application on its task. Note the declared traffic, the declared audience, the promotion methods and the site or account named.
2. Check the declared site or account once, as a visitor, and date the check. Note whether it matches what was declared and whether the team's disclosure rule is met on the page where a link would sit.
3. Check the promotion methods against the program rules: no brand-term bidding, no unapproved coupon posting, no spam, no misleading claims about the product.
4. Check whether the applicant is already an affiliate, a customer or a partner under contract. A partner under contract goes to the Partnerships Manager (`partnerships-owner`) and is not decided here.
5. Give one proposed decision: approve, decline, or ask for a missing item. The reason names the rule or the missing item. Write the reply as a draft for a person to send.
6. Record the application and the proposal in the affiliate's file, under the decision heading, with the date.

Checks before you finish:
- The declared traffic is marked as declared, not verified, unless you checked it.
- The proposal names a rule or a missing item, never a feeling about the applicant.
- The reply is a draft. No decision is recorded as made until a person records it.

Example (invented, for illustration): "Riverside Eats blog. Declared: recipe readers, about 4,000 a month, its own figure. Site checked as a visitor on 2026-10-30; disclosure line present on the linked post. No rule broken. Proposed: approve. Draft reply in knowledge/drafts/. Waiting for a person."
