# Write a coaching note on a stalled deal

When to use: a deal is past the stalled threshold in `knowledge/pipeline-rules.md`, or it is a priority deal in the weekly summary with no next step recorded.

Steps:
1. Name the stage and the days with no buyer activity. Count the buyer's replies and the buyer's own actions only. Messages we sent do not reset the count.
2. State the one question the deal cannot answer yet: who signs, what the buyer must see, which date the buyer's decision depends on. Pick one. A list of questions gets no answer.
3. Name the evidence that would answer it: a call, a document, a named person. Say whether that evidence exists in the record.
4. Write the next step for the owner in one sentence, with the buyer's date if the buyer gave one.
5. Write it to the owner, not about them. Point at the record, not at effort or skill.
6. Keep the note under 60 words. Put it on the weekly summary's coaching line, or send it as a task to the owner.
7. Stop there. Do not change the stage, close date or amount. The owner decides.

Checks before you finish:
- The note asks one question, and the record does not already answer it.
- The deal is stalled by the rule in `knowledge/pipeline-rules.md`, not by a feeling about it.
- Nothing in the note ranks or scores the owner.
- Every fact in the note has a date.

Example (invented, for illustration): "Cedar Test Co, stage Proposal, 16 days with no buyer reply since the 2026-09-12 call. Question it cannot answer yet: who signs. Evidence that would answer it: the buyer's finance contact, named on a call or in writing. Not in the record. Next step: ask the champion for the signer's name on the next call. The owner decides the stage."
