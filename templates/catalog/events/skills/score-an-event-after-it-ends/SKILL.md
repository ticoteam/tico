# Score an event after it ends

When to use: an event ended 30 or more days ago, or its post-event review is due.

Steps:
1. Record the goal from the brief and whether it was met, in one line.
2. Record the total cost and name the invoice or receipt behind each part. A cost with no source is marked estimated.
3. Count meetings held, not booked, from the CRM read or the attached export. Count pipeline from deals whose source is the
   event. Write "not yet measurable" for any figure the data cannot give.
4. Compute cost per meeting: cost divided by meetings held, with the arithmetic on the line.
5. Check follow-up: how many leads were contacted inside the deadline in `knowledge/rules.md`, as a count and a share.
6. Attendance goes in as context only. Write one lesson in `knowledge/results.md`: what to repeat, what to change or stop.
7. Write the verdict for the next time: go, go small or skip, with its one reason.

Checks before you finish:
- Each figure has its source, or is marked estimated or not yet measurable.
- Attendance is not used as the success measure.
- The lesson is one line and names a change.

Example (generic): "Webinar on 2026-09-24. Goal: 25 demo requests. Cost 300 (platform invoice dated 2026-09-20). 31 registered,
18 attended live, 4 demo requests. Pipeline not yet measurable: the deal source is blank on 2 of the 4. Cost per demo
request 75. Follow-up: 4 of 4 contacted within two business days. Lesson: repeat the format; move the demo offer into the
first ten minutes."
