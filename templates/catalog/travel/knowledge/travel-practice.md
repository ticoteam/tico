# Travel practice

What is known about booking business travel, what each claim rests on, and where the sources disagree. Vendor figures
are marked as vendor figures and are not standards. Confirm an official rule at its official source before a traveller
relies on it. Written 2026-11-01; check the dates in this file before using it.

## Fare timing
- The lowest domestic fares are often booked a few weeks out. One analysis of Google Flights data, reported by a
  secondary site, put the lows 21 to 44 days before departure (read 2026-11-01; not verified at source).
- International fares are cited differently. Vendor guides from 2025 give 2 to 5 months as the usual window, and some
  give 2 to 8 months. The sources conflict; record the range rather than choosing one.
- Some 2025 vendor guides give 1 to 3 months for domestic flights. This conflicts with the analysis above.
- An Expedia 2025 claim that booking 1 to 3 months ahead saves about 25 percent was seen only second hand. Not verified,
  and not used as a number in any plan.
- Expedia 2025 reports Sunday domestic departures about 6 percent cheaper. Vendor data; check it against the traveller's
  own dates.
- Practice: a fare is a quote at the time it was seen. A changeable fare costs more than a non-refundable one. The
  difference is the price of flexibility, so state both.

## Total cost
- Total = the fare with the bags and seat fees the trip needs, plus hotel nights, plus ground transport, plus the cost
  of a likely change. Compare totals, not headline fares.
- A fare that cannot be changed is cheaper only while the plans hold. Name that risk in the plan.

## Policy
- The advance-booking rule in `knowledge/policy.md` is a minimum, not a target. The bot books inside policy and
  reports how far ahead each trip was booked.
- An exception is named with the amount over and the reason. The bot records it; a person approves it.

## Entry documents
- Schengen passport rule: the Schengen Borders Code, Regulation (EU) 2016/399, Article 6, as secondary sources describe
  it (read 2026-11-01). A travel document should be valid at least three months beyond the planned departure from the
  Schengen area and issued within the previous ten years. Check the regulation text on EUR-Lex before quoting it.
- EES, the EU Entry/Exit System: secondary sources say it started on 12 October 2025, with a gradual rollout over six
  months and full operation targeted for April 2026. Sources differ on the day in April (9 or 10). No official
  confirmation was found, and that target has passed. Check travel-europe.europa.eu for the current status.
- ETIAS: secondary sources expect it in the fourth quarter of 2026. No confirmed date was found (read 2026-11-01). Check
  the official site before planning around it.
- Other destinations set their own rules. Look each one up at its official source and record the date it was read.
- The bot does not give visa advice and does not say whether a person will be admitted.

## Recording
- Confirmation numbers go in `knowledge/trips.md`. Passport numbers, card numbers and loyalty passwords are not written
  into any file.
