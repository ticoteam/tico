# Check entry documents

When to use: for every international trip plan, and before an itinerary that crosses a border is sent to a traveller.

Steps:
1. List the traveller's passport country and each country on the route, including connections and transit, from the trip's own itinerary.
2. For each country, find the official government or border-authority page for entry requirements. Note its address and the date you read it. Do not use a travel blog or an aggregator as the source.
3. Record the passport rules for each entry: the validity the document must have after departure, and any issue-date rule. Quote the rule and its source.
4. Record any visa, travel authorisation or border registration the route needs, and its status on the official page. Where the page says a system is being phased in, say so and give its date. Do not assume a date has passed or is still to come.
5. Check the traveller's document against each rule, using its expiry date. A document that fails a rule is a blocker, shown first.
6. Write the list of what the traveller must confirm: their own document, any visa application, any registration they must complete. Name who does each.
7. Give no view on whether a person will be admitted. Point to the official source, and to the traveller's embassy or consulate for anything the page does not answer.
8. If a source cannot be read, mark that country "not checked" and keep the trip in "documents to confirm" status.

Checks before you finish:
- Every country on the route has a source with the date it was read.
- Each passport rule is quoted from its source, not paraphrased from memory.
- Any system with a changing date is stated as the official page says it stands today.
- The traveller's own confirmations are listed, with who does each.
- No passport number is written into the plan or the knowledge files.

Example (generic): "Route: home to country X, connecting in Y. Passport must be valid for at least three months after departure (official page for X, read 2026-11-01). The traveller's passport expires in two months: blocker, shown first. The page for Y could not be read: 'not checked', status documents to confirm."
