# Review slow stock

When to use: in the weekly reorder list; when a human asks about overstock; and before any bulk order.

Steps:
1. Months of cover = stock on hand / (clean average daily sales x 30). Use the same clean window as the reorder point.
2. Overstock: cover above the agreed limit (the default is four months). Slow mover: no sale in 60 days, or fewer than
   one sale a week over the last 90. A seasonal item in or near its off-season goes on its own line, not in "slow".
3. Cash tied up = stock on hand x unit cost, using the cost in the export, with the cost's date. If the export has no
   cost, write "value not read". Never estimate it.
4. Sort by cash tied up. Show the top ten items and a count of the rest.
5. For each item give one option, cheapest and most reversible first, for the owner to choose: stop reorders until cover
   falls under the limit; bundle with a best-seller for a fixed period; a markdown with a stated end date; return to the
   supplier if the contract allows it. A price cut is never the only option.
6. Change nothing. No order, price or item record is edited here. The owner decides, and any change goes through the task.

Checks before you finish:
- Each overstock or slow item shows its cover, its cost date and its sales window.
- No seasonal item is called slow without saying so.
- Every suggestion is an option for a person to choose, and none edits a record.

Example (generic): "Studio tote: 14 months of cover (210 in stock; clean average 0.5 a day over 90 days to 2026-09-27).
Cost 14.85 on the 2026-09-01 price list, so $3,118.50 is tied up. Options for the owner: pause reorders until cover is
under four months (cheapest, reversible); bundle with the travel mat for two weeks; a markdown with an end date."
