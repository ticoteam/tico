# Build a target list

When to use: when a person asks for a list, or when the source plan names a public directory or a set of company sites to build one from.

Steps:
1. Write the fit rule first, in one sentence, from `knowledge/qualified.md` and the buyer profile. Example: "logistics firms with a fleet of 50 trucks or more, stated on their own site."
2. Name the source: a directory, an exhibitor list or a company's own public page. Record its address and the date you read it.
3. Check each candidate against the fit rule. Record the fact that makes it a fit, from the source, with the page it came from.
4. Record the company, the role of the person the list is for if the source names one, and the company's public business page. Do not record a personal email address, a home address or a personal phone number.
5. Mark every row "to verify". A row is not a contact until a person or the SDR has checked the right person holds the role.
6. Drop any company that has asked not to be contacted, or that is a customer, a competitor or on the do-not-contact list in `knowledge/do-not-contact.md`.
7. Save the list as `knowledge/lists/<list>.md` with its fit rule, its sources and the date of the last check at the top.

Checks before you finish:
- Each row has a fit reason with its dated source.
- No row holds a private person's contact details.
- Every row is marked "to verify", and the count of rows verified is zero until a person verifies one.
- The list says how many candidates were checked and how many were dropped, and why.

Example (invented, for illustration): "Regional fleets, fit rule: 50 trucks or more, stated on the company's own site. Source: association member directory, read 2026-11-02. Checked 60, kept 11, dropped 49 (fleet under 50, or no fleet size stated). Each kept row: company, stated fleet size, page and date. All marked to verify."
