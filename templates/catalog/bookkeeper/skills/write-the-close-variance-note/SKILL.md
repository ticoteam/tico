# Write the close variance note

When to use: the note in a month-ready-to-close task, and the budget section of the monthly report.

Steps:
1. Read `hub finance report budget_vs_actual` for the month. Its amounts are minor units: 315000 is 3,150.00, so divide
   by 100 before you write any figure. Put the basis (cash or accrual) and the as-of time at the top of the note.
2. Test each line against the threshold. Use the one the close checklist or the owner names, for example a dollar amount
   and a percentage together. If none is set, ask the owner once for one and, until then, take the three largest lines
   by dollars. A line that passes both tests of the threshold is explained. A line that passes one is listed.
3. Say whether the budget changed during the month. A changed budget explains some variance by itself.
4. For each explained line, give its cause in one line and the evidence that shows it: the vendor, the entry or the
   bill. A cause is something you found, not something that is likely.
5. Call a difference timing only when you found the item that reverses it (an accrual, a bill dated next month). Anything
   else is "unexplained".
6. Keep the note to about 120 words: at most three explained lines, then the unexplained lines.

Checks before you finish:
- The basis, the as-of time and the threshold are stated.
- Every explanation names its evidence.
- Every unexplained line is named as unexplained, not as timing.
- The note matches the report's numbers.

Example (invented, for illustration): "Basis: accrual, as of 2026-10-31 09:00. Threshold: $2,000 and 10%, set by the
owner. Software +$3,150 (+42%): an annual licence is posted monthly (entry 4471). Travel -$2,400 (-35%): two September
trips expensed in September (bills 330 and 331). Contractor +$2,600 (+18%): an October invoice accrued before its
approval (entry 4502). Unexplained: Office supplies +$2,050 (+61%), no entry or bill found yet."
