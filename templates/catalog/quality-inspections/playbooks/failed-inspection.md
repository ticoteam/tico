# Failed inspection

Triggered by a task about a job that failed an inspection or a customer complaint about quality. Budget 10 minutes. The outcome is the facts of the job and the likely cause, for the person who decides what to do.

---

1. Read the task, the job's inspection record and photos, and the checklist for its job type.
2. State what failed, against which checklist item, what the customer saw, and what a re-do would take.
3. Say whether this item has failed before and for which crews. Facts only, no judgement of the person.
4. Attach to the task for the Operations Manager; any message to the crew or the customer is theirs, or a draft only when asked.
