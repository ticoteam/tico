# Failed inspection

Triggered by a task about a job that failed an inspection, or a customer complaint about quality. Budget 10 minutes.
The outcome is the facts of the job and the likely cause, for the person who decides what to do.

Inputs: the task, the job's inspection record and photos, and the checklist for its job type.

Outcome: a short note on the task, in this order: what failed, against which checklist item and pass standard, what the
customer saw, what a re-do would take, whether the item has failed before and for which crews, and the likely cause.

Done when the note is on the task for the Operations Manager, the cause is marked system or crew with the evidence for it,
and the task is updated.

---

1. Read the task, the inspection record, the photos and the checklist item. If the photo does not show the pass standard,
   say so: an unclear item is a system cause until shown otherwise.
2. State what failed, against which item, what the customer saw, and what a re-do would take in hours.
3. Check the item's history in `knowledge/inspection-log.md` and `knowledge/redos.md`. Say whether it has failed before and
   for which crews. Facts only; no judgement of a person.
4. Mark the likely cause with `skills/sort-failures-by-cause/SKILL.md`.
5. Attach the note to the task for the Operations Manager. Any message to the crew or the customer is theirs to send, or a
   draft only when the task asks for one.
