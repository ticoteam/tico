# Collections practice

The standing reference behind `knowledge/ladder.md` and the skills in `skills/`. It holds the measures this team reports and the dated research
behind them. A figure is quoted with its source and year. Benchmarks describe other companies; they are context, not a target.

## Measures

- **DSO (days sales outstanding)** = accounts receivable at the date / credit sales over the period x days in the period. Use 90 days of
  credit sales. Report the formula with the figure, and the same method every week so the trend means something.
- **Share over 30 days** = receivables more than 30 days past due / total receivables. Simpler than DSO and needs no sales figure.
- **Collection effectiveness** is the share of collectible receivables paid in a period. It needs a clear definition of "collectible"; set
  one in `knowledge/ladder.md` before it is reported.

## Why invoices are late (dated research)

- Atradius, US barometer (2025, 240 companies, read 2026-11-01 through a secondary summary): customers' own cash problems were cited by 45%,
  delays in the customer's payment process by 33%, supply chain disruption by 26%, and invoice disputes by 23%. Responses were multiple, so the
  shares do not sum to 100. Check the Atradius report before quoting.
- PYMNTS and American Express, "From Friction to Flow" tracker (June 2025, read 2026-11-01 through a secondary summary): 27% of finance respondents
  said at least half their receivables team's time goes to resolving invoice disputes.
- Reading across: a reminder fixes a missed payment. It does not fix a customer who lacks cash or a disputed invoice. Those need a person, a plan
  the owner approves, or a credit note. That is why disputes stop the ladder.

## Reminders and payment routes

- Make paying easy. The reminder carries the route printed on the invoice; a payment link already on the invoice is the cheapest route.
- Automated retries for failed card payments are a platform feature (payment processors describe scheduled retries in their documentation).
  Vendor claims about recovery rates are not sourced here and are not used.
- No independent study found by the team on the timing of reminder emails. Keep the ladder's early steps friendly, and change the timing only
  on this team's own data: the share paid after each step, recorded in the weekly pack.

## Escalation

- A human takes over at 45 days, or above the personal-touch amount, or when a customer replies with a question about the amount. The bot never
  chases a dispute.
- Any late fee, interest or statutory interest is the owner's decision and wording. The bot quotes it only when the owner gives the exact term.
