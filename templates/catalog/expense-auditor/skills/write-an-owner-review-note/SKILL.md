# Write an owner review note

When to use: when a report review or the monthly audit has lines for one owner, and the note goes into the task or the
audit section for that owner.

Steps:
1. Open with the verdict in one line: "Pass." or "N lines to question, total T."
2. For each line to question, write one sentence: the date, the merchant, the amount, the rule, and the one thing that
   would clear it. Pattern: "2026-09-14, Harbor Grill, 412.00: no itemised receipt (policy 1.3). A receipt, or a
   documented policy exception, clears it." A missing business purpose is a separate line with its own ask; it never clears a missing receipt.
3. State facts, not motives. Write "no receipt on file" or "submitted after the 60-day window", never "you failed to"
   or "you did not provide".
4. Group small repeats: "7 meals over the 60 per person limit, total 41." List at most five lines by name; group the rest.
5. Keep it short: under 150 words for one report, under 300 for a monthly section.
6. Keep it to this owner's people and lines. Never copy in another person's line.

Checks before you finish:
- Every line names its rule, its date and its amount.
- No word such as "fraud", "abuse", "cheat" or "failed".
- Each line has one ask the owner can answer.
- The note names only this owner's people.

Example (invented, for illustration): "Pass with 2 lines to question. 2026-09-19, flight, 690.00: premium economy on a
3-hour flight (policy 4.2). A source record or the fare difference clears it. 2026-09-21, hotel, 228.00: 48 over the
180 per night limit (policy 3.1). The approver's note clears it."
