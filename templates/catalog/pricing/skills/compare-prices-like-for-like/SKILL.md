# Compare prices like for like

When to use: before you say a competitor's price went up or down, before you put two prices side by side in a report, or when a human asks whether we are cheaper.

Steps:
1. Write each price in full: the amount, the currency, the billing period (monthly or annual), the unit it is charged on (per location, per seat, per booking, per 1,000 messages), the minimum commitment, and whether annual billing is required.
2. Put both on one unit and one period. An annual price shown per month is still an annual price, so say so. For a per-seat or per-location price, compute the cost at a stated typical size (for example two locations and six seats) and name that size.
3. List what each price includes that the other does not: features in the plan, limits and what they cost beyond the limit, and add-ons the buyer must buy to match the other side.
4. Read the price the buyer sees. Use the page as a buyer would: the final figure on the pricing or quote page, the region and currency selected, and any promotion banner. If the page shows no public price (a demo is required), write "no public price", never an estimate.
5. Record the URL, the date read, the region, and what you read (page and section). Compare with the last entry. If the page changed, log both dates.
6. Write the comparison as one line per price, on the same unit and period, with the size used.
7. If a change does not show where it should (a banner says new pricing, the checkout shows the old price), report the gap with what you saw. Do not decide which one is real.

Checks before you finish:
- Both prices are on one unit and one billing period, and the basis is written down.
- Each price has its URL and date. An unreadable page is "not read", not "unchanged".
- Inclusions are stated, so a lower price is not a smaller plan without saying so.
- No estimate is shown as a price.
- A change is logged with both dates, not only the new one.

Example (invented, for illustration): "Flowgrid Pro: $59 per location per month billed annually, or $69 billed monthly (read 2026-09-30; the annual price was $55 on 2026-09-01). Ours: $59 per location per month billed annually. At two locations on annual billing both are $118 a month. Flowgrid's Pro includes SMS reminders; ours are in the $79 Plus plan. The gap is the inclusion, not the price."
