# Check a job post before it goes out

When to use: before you hand over any job post draft, board listing, careers-page copy or repost, and whenever a role's pay,
location or hiring tools change.

Steps:
1. Pay. Read the pay rule in `knowledge/wording.md`. Where the role's location requires a pay range in the posting (see
   `knowledge/hiring-practice.md`), show a range, not a single figure. Show base pay and commission as separate lines, not
   commission-only or "OTE" alone. Where a board hides posts without a range, show the range there too. A missing figure is
   a marked gap, never a guess.
2. Location. Name the place of work or the remote rule. For a remote role, name the states or countries the team hires in,
   and check the pay rule for each of them.
3. Requirements. Each required item must be needed in the first six months. Replace a degree or a years-of-experience
   count that stands in for a skill with what the person must be able to do.
4. Wording. Remove gender-coded words ("ninja", "rockstar", "dominant"), age-coded words ("digital native", "recent
   graduate", "young and energetic"), and physical or other requirements that exclude people without a reason tied to the
   work. Read the post once as someone who meets seven of ten criteria.
5. Automated tools. If the team uses a tool that scores or filters applicants, the post or the candidate notice says so
   where the candidate's location requires it. If the team has not decided, leave that as a marked gap.
6. Board limits. Check each board's free-listing rules before you plan a repost or a second listing, and write the date you
   checked.
7. Count the words. A post is under 400 words.

Checks before you finish:
- Pay, location and the jurisdiction's rule are shown or marked as gaps.
- Every requirement is needed for the work.
- No gender-coded or age-coded words remain.
- Each board rule you relied on has a date.

Example (generic, invented): Role: Sales Representative, remote in the United States. Pay: base $70,000 to $90,000, plus
commission, with the commission plan on a separate line. Requirement "five years in software sales" changed to "has run a
sales process from first call to signed contract". Board rule checked on 2026-11-01. The AI-screening notice is a marked gap
until the hiring manager confirms whether a tool is used.
