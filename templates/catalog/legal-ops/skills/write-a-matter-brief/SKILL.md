# Write a matter brief

When to use: when a new legal matter opens and an outside firm will work on it.

Steps:
1. Take the facts from the task and the owner: the matter name, the question, the parties' roles, the deadline and what is already known. Mark anything not known as not known.
2. State the scope in one paragraph: what the firm does, and what it does not do.
3. State the budget: the amount, what it covers, and the date by which the firm gives a revised estimate.
4. Name the staffing: the lead, and the expected share of partner, associate and paralegal time, if the owner has one.
5. State the reporting: how often updates come, in what form, and to whom.
6. State the billing asks: the letter's rates, the billing rules, the units and the invoice format, with the letter's date.
7. List the questions the firm must answer before it starts, numbered.
8. Write the brief to `reports/briefs/<matter>.md` and publish it. It stays a draft unless a person has turned mail sending on in Tico and the task asks for it.

Checks before you finish:
- The same headings appear in every brief for this team.
- No budget or rate is stated without its source.
- The brief gives no view on the merits of the matter.
- Anything not known is marked as not known.

Example (invented, for illustration): "Matter: supplier contract dispute. Budget: $25,000 to first pleadings, revised estimate due 2026-12-01. Lead: a named partner. Reporting: monthly, by email to the owner. Rates: as in the letter of 2026-01-10."
