# Deal practice

Standing method for an account executive. The team's own stages and rules in `sales-process.md` win where they differ.
This file holds what is true of most B2B deals: what must be on the record before a stage moves, what counts as a held
demo, and how show-up is measured.

## Stage evidence

A stage describes the buyer, not our activity. A sent deck, a booked call or a friendly reply does not move a deal. Each
stage needs one piece of evidence the buyer gave, with its date:

- **Discovery done:** the buyer's problem in their words, and the person who feels it.
- **Evaluation:** the buyer's own people have looked at what matters to them, and a named person will judge it.
- **Proposal:** the buyer asked for an option, and a person has approved its price and terms.
- **Paper:** the buyer's legal or procurement team has the document, and a signer is named.
- **Won:** signed. **Lost:** a reason from the team's list, dated.

A deal with no dated buyer step is open but unproven. Say so in the review; do not drop its stage.

## Held demo

- A demo is held when the buyer and the seller talk about the product in the booked slot. Keep the evidence: the recording,
  the notes or the seller's dated note.
- A different call held in place of the booked meeting is a call. It is not a held demo, even when the conversation was real.
- A booked slot that has not passed is pending. It is not lost.

## Show-up measurement

- The unit is one booked demo: its booked-at time, slot time, who booked it and through which channel.
- Group bookings by the week they were made. Close each one after a fixed window (for example, the slot plus two business
  days) and record one disposition: held, rescheduled then held, cancelled by the buyer, cancelled by us, no-show, or
  ghosted (no reply after booking, once the window has closed).
- Do not infer a disposition from silence while the window is open.
- Cut the bookings by hours from booking to slot (same day, one to two days, three to six, seven or more). This is the
  first cut to make; the team's own cohort decides whether it matters.
- A small group is inconclusive. Write the group size next to every rate.

## Benchmarks

No reliable public benchmark for demo show-up was found in a 2026 search. Vendor figures disagree, from about 6.5% to about
25% no-show, and none publishes its data. Use the team's own cohort as the baseline. Do not set a target from a vendor number.

## Recording

Record a call only with the consent that the buyer's location requires. Several US states and a number of EU countries require
every party to agree. This file does not set that rule: check it with counsel for each market.
