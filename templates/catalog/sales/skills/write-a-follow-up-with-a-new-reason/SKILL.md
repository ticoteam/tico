# Write a follow-up with a new reason

When to use: after a deal has been quiet past the at-risk threshold, after a missed next step or a no-show, or whenever a message is not the recap of a call.

Steps:
1. Name the reason the buyer would answer: an answer to a question they asked, a fact they need for their decision, a smaller next step, or a date they gave.
2. Check that the reason is new since our last message. If it is not, do not send a follow-up yet; say what would be new.
3. Write it in the seller's voice from `knowledge/voice.md`, under 120 words. Open with the reason, not an apology or a check-in.
4. Make one ask: a date, or a choice between two times.
5. Say what happens if there is no reply (for example, the review pauses on a stated date) only if the seller has agreed to that.
6. Put the recipient, subject and body on the task. It stays a draft unless the task asks, mail sending is on in Tico and a granted Tool allows it.

Checks before you finish:
- No "just checking in", "circling back" or "following up on my last email".
- The new reason is named and dated.
- Under 120 words, with one ask.
- No price, term, date or feature the team has not stated.
- Any buyer words quoted are exact, with the date they were said.

Example (invented, for illustration): "Hi Sam, you asked on 22 September whether reminders can be sent to rebooked students. They can, and the setting is in the attached one-page note. Could we confirm the rebooking flow on a 15-minute call on 2 October?"
