# Check a deal stage against evidence

When to use: before you report a deal's stage in a review, propose a stage or close date change to the owner, or place a deal in a forecast category.

Steps:
1. Write the stage as the record shows it, with the date it was set.
2. Name the exit evidence for that stage in `knowledge/deal-practice.md` and `knowledge/sales-process.md`.
3. Look for that evidence in the record: a dated buyer message, a call note with the buyer's words, a document the buyer returned, a named signer. Our own activity (a sent deck, a booked call, a reply we wrote) is not evidence.
4. Mark each item: confirmed (with its dated source), missing, or contradicted (the record says otherwise).
5. If the stage is ahead of the evidence, propose the stage the evidence supports and the reason. Change nothing. The owner decides.
6. For a close date, compare it with the buyer's own date and their decision process. A close date with no buyer date behind it is "unconfirmed".
7. For a forecast category: commit needs a buyer step with a date and a named signer; best case is evidence that is real but not yet confirmed by the buyer.

Checks before you finish:
- Each stage claim has a dated source.
- Our activity alone was not counted as evidence.
- A contradiction is shown, not averaged or dropped.
- A deal that is open is not used as a stalled example unless its missing evidence is named.
- The recommendation names the person who decides.

Example (invented, for illustration): "Birch Hill Studio, stage Evaluation since 2026-09-10. Exit evidence: two of the buyer's instructors tested the class schedule and replied on 2026-09-18 (quoted). Confirmed. Named person to judge the test: missing. Named signer: not yet needed (it belongs to Paper). Proposed stage: Evaluation holds until a named person judges the test; Proposal follows once the buyer asks for a priced option and a person approves its price and terms. The owner decides."
