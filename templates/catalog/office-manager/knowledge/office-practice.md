# Office practice

Reference facts behind the checks in this bot's skills. The team's own rules, in `knowledge/office.md` and
`knowledge/visitors.md`, apply where they differ. Each fact names its source.

## Fire extinguishers

- A portable extinguisher gets a visual check at least every 30 days (NFPA 10, portable fire extinguishers; secondary
  summaries read 2026-11-01, primary text not read).
- Maintenance is annual, and it is done by a qualified service provider. Local fire authorities can require a licensed
  provider; check the local rule before booking one.
- A missed check or an expired tag is a safety item, so it goes to the Operations Manager at once (`triage-an-office-request`).

## Visitors

- Visit records are kept until 30 days after the visit, then deleted. This is the team's rule (`knowledge/visitors.md`),
  not an external requirement. No external source was read for the number.
- Access is the admin's to grant. Visitors meet a host or reception; no badge, key or code is given by this bot.

## Supplies

- Par levels come from usage. A par set without a usage figure is an estimate and is marked so until a month of counts
  exists (team rule, no external source).

## Sources

- 2026-11-01: NFPA 10 (portable fire extinguishers, current edition), via secondary summaries; primary text not read.
  Check the edition the local authority uses.
- Team rules for visitors and supplies: from the setup answers, no external source.
