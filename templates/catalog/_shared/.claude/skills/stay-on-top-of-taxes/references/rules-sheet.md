# Rules sheet (read 2026-11-18)

A starting list, not a source. Each line says where it was read and when. Copy the lines the company uses into
`knowledge/taxes.md`, re-read each at its source before filing on it, and replace the date. "Secondary" means the
figure came from a summary and was not confirmed on the authority's own page.

## Federal income tax

| Rule | Value | Read at |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Form 1120 due | 15th day of the 4th month after the tax year ends | IRS Publication 509 (2026) |
| Forms 1120-S and 1065 due | 15th day of the 3rd month after the tax year ends | IRS Publication 509 (2026) |
| Extension | Form 7004, automatic 6 months to file; tax is still due on the original date | IRS Publication 509 (2026) |
| Corporate estimates | 15th day of the 4th, 6th, 9th and 12th months; required when the corporation expects to owe 500 dollars or more | IRS estimated taxes page, updated 2026-09-25 |
| Individual estimates (owners) | April 15, June 15, September 15, January 15; required when the person expects to owe 1,000 dollars or more | IRS estimated taxes page, updated 2026-09-25 |
| Individual safe harbour | Paying at least 90 percent of this year's tax or 100 percent of last year's avoids the penalty; a higher percentage can apply to higher incomes, which was not on the page read | IRS estimated taxes page, updated 2026-09-25 |
| Corporate underpayment rules | Form 2220 and the Form 1120 instructions | Not read; the CPA sets the method |

## Information returns for 2026 payments

| Rule | Value | Read at |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1099-NEC threshold | 2,000 dollars for payments in tax years beginning after 2025; may be adjusted for inflation from 2027 | Instructions for Forms 1099-MISC and 1099-NEC (Rev. 12/2026) |
| 1099-MISC thresholds | Rents and most other boxes 2,000 dollars; royalties 10 dollars; gross proceeds to an attorney 600 dollars | Same instructions |
| 1099-NEC due | To the recipient and the IRS by January 31 | Same instructions |
| 1099-MISC due | Recipients January 31; IRS February 28 on paper, March 31 e-filed | Same instructions |
| Corporations | Generally not reported, except legal services | Same instructions |
| Card and network payments | Reported by the payment settlement entity on Form 1099-K, not on 1099-NEC or 1099-MISC | Same instructions |
| E-filing | Required at 10 or more information returns of all types combined | Same instructions (T.D. 9972) |
| Backup withholding | 24 percent | IRS Topic 307, updated 2026-09-24 |
| 1099-K for payment apps | Over 20,000 dollars and more than 200 transactions; no threshold for card transactions | IRS "Understanding your Form 1099-K", updated 2026-11-17 |
| IRIS | The IRS system for e-filing information returns; needs a Transmitter Control Code; application can take up to 45 days; the only intake system for the 2027 filing season as FIRE retires | IRS e-file information returns page, updated 2026-10-26 |
| Weekend rule | A due date on a Saturday, Sunday or legal holiday moves to the next business day | IRS Publication 509 (2026) |

## Sales tax thresholds (examples only)

From the Sales Tax Institute economic nexus chart as of 2026-08-01, read 2026-11-18. Secondary. Never act on these:
read the state's revenue department page.

| State | Threshold and window as charted |
| --- | --- |
| Most states | 100,000 dollars of sales in the state's window |
| California | 500,000 dollars, prior or current calendar year |
| Texas | 500,000 dollars, preceding twelve months |
| New York | 500,000 dollars and more than 100 sales, preceding four sales tax quarters |
| Florida | 100,000 dollars of taxable sales, prior calendar year |
| Illinois | 100,000 dollars; its 200-transaction test was removed on 2026-01-01 |
| Kentucky | Its 200-transaction test ended on 2026-08-01 |
| Alabama, Mississippi | 250,000 dollars |
| Connecticut | 100,000 dollars and 200 transactions, twelve months ending September 30 |

On that date 16 jurisdictions still counted transactions as well as dollars. The list changes every year.

## Delaware corporations

| Rule | Value | Read at |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Annual report and franchise tax due | On or before March 1, filed online | Delaware Division of Corporations, "Annual Report and Tax Instructions" (page copyright 2026) |
| Late filing | 200 dollar penalty plus 1.5 percent interest a month | Same page |
| Minimum tax | 175 dollars on the Authorized Shares Method; 400 dollars on the Assumed Par Value Capital Method | Same page |
| Two methods | The page describes both. Work out both and read the page for which the company may use; a company with many authorised shares and few assets often sees a very different figure under each | Same page |
| LLCs and partnerships | Not read. Read the Division's page before adding the line | - |
