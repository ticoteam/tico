# Sources

What this skill drew from. Ideas only; all wording is original. Checked 2026-11-18.

## Tico

- `docs/goals-and-kpis.md`: KPI fields, immutable readings, weeks ending Sunday, quality (measured, estimate, partial),
  freshness (fresh, stale, missing), improvement and maintenance targets, the automatic per-bot KPIs, proposals.
- `docs/strategy.md`: bets, what they rule out, wrong-if, bet health from KPI-backed goals only.
- `docs/tasks.md`, `docs/pages.md`: tasks, labels, asks and OKs, publishing pages with a cadence.
- `docs/finance-tools.md`: `tico finance metric` and `tico finance report`.
- Catalog skills folded in or kept as sub-steps: see `../../MERGES.md`.

## Operating rhythm

- Gino Wickman, *Traction* (2011), the Entrepreneurial Operating System: a weekly scorecard of 5 to 15 measurables each
  with one owner and a goal; a weekly meeting built around an issues list; identify, discuss, solve; to-dos scored done
  or not done. Ideas used: the scorecard size, the issues list with three endings, binary scoring of commitments. EOS
  vocabulary and meeting format are not used.
- Andy Grove, *High Output Management* (1983): leading indicators paired with a counter-measure. Idea used: guardrail
  numbers beside every quantity target.
- borghei/Claude-Skills (chief-of-staff, operations skills) and lenny-skills (planning, running effective meetings):
  ideas only. No wording used.
- Anthropic knowledge-work plugins, operations and productivity (Apache 2.0): the status report built from records.

## Meetings

- Patrick Lencioni, *Death by Meeting* (2004); the GitLab handbook on asynchronous work; Jason Fried and David
  Heinemeier Hansson's writing at Basecamp. Cited in `../../DISAGREEMENTS.md` from general knowledge; not re-read.

## Goals

- John Doerr, *Measure What Matters* (2018), and Google re:Work, "Set goals with OKRs": objectives with measurable key
  results; committed versus aspirational; grading 0 to 1; about 0.7 as a good grade on a stretch result; goals kept
  apart from performance reviews. Ideas used: all of those.

## Decisions

- Jeff Bezos, Amazon shareholder letters of 2015 and 2016: reversible (two-way door) versus irreversible (one-way
  door) decisions; deciding with about 70% of the information wanted; disagree and commit. Ideas used: the sorting
  question and who decides.
- Gary Klein, "Performing a Project Premortem", *Harvard Business Review*, September 2007: imagine the failure and list
  its causes. Idea used: the pre-mortem section of the decision page.
- Tico catalog skill `product-manager/frame-a-decision-for-the-owner`: the default if nobody decides, and what would
  reopen the decision.

## SOPs

- Atul Gawande, *The Checklist Manifesto* (2009): short, tested checklists at pause points. Idea used: test the SOP on
  someone who has not done the work; keep it to a page.
- obra/superpowers, writing-skills (MIT): testing a written procedure against a fresh agent before relying on it. Idea
  adapted for the cold test.

## Not verified

Book details are from memory of well-known works and were not re-read. No numbers in this skill depend on them except
the scorecard size (5 to 15) and the stretch grade (about 0.7), both of which the owner can change in the settings.
