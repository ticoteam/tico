# Notices and renewals

## Why this is strict

Courts generally enforce option deadlines as written. In the New York cases summarised by Smith, Gambrell &
Russell ("A Legal 'Minefield': Options to Renew in Leases", 2017-12-01, read 2026-11-18): time is of the essence
for an option; notice must follow the lease's terms, including the delivery method; an option can require that the
tenant is not in default both when notice is given and when the new term starts; the landlord has no duty to remind
the tenant; and staying on and paying rent after expiry generally creates a month-to-month tenancy, not a renewal.
Some states will occasionally forgive a late notice; never plan on it. Other states differ in detail, so the
lawyer confirms the rule where the space is.

## The renewal decision

Start 12 months before the last notice date (18 for a warehouse, a lab or anything hard to move).

1. **Rerun the need test** (`space-decision.md`) with a year of real usage.
2. **Read the option clause** and the abstract: window, method, conditions, how renewal rent is set. If rent is "fair
   market value", read the process and its timetable; it may need to start before the notice.
3. **Check the conditions now.** Is the company in default of anything, even a small thing (a late payment, a
   missing insurance certificate, an unapproved alteration)? Cure it months before notice.
4. **Get the market.** The landlord's proposal, and at least two real alternatives priced on the same basis. A
   tenant-side broker can do this; ask how the broker is paid.
5. **Lay out the choices** on the decision page: exercise the option as written; negotiate a new deal with the
   landlord (a renewal outside the option, often with fresh concessions); move; shrink; sublet part; give the space
   up. Show whole-term cost and exit cost for each.
6. **Ask for the OK** on the choice at least 60 days before the last safe date.
7. **If negotiating instead of exercising**, keep the option alive: unless a signed amendment is in hand before the
   last safe date, either send the option notice or get the owner's written decision to let it go.

## Giving a notice

1. Read the **notice clause** and the clause the notice is given under. Write down: who must give it, to whom, at
   which addresses (often the landlord and its lender or lawyer too), by which methods, and when a notice counts as
   given (on sending, on receipt, or a set number of days after posting).
2. Count the last day with `count-a-notice-deadline`. Set the **safe date** 30 days before it.
3. Draft the notice. Keep it short and unconditional:

```
[Company legal name]
[Date]

By [method required by clause __] to:
[Landlord's legal name and notice address from clause __]
[Copy addresses required by clause __]

Re: Lease dated [date] between [Landlord] and [Tenant] for [premises] (the "Lease")

Under clause [__] of the Lease, Tenant exercises its option to renew the Lease for the [first] renewal term of
[__] years, beginning [date] and ending [date].

[Signature of an officer authorised to sign]
[Name, title]
```

   For a termination or non-renewal notice, the sentence changes; the rest does not. Do not add conditions,
   questions, complaints or "subject to" wording: a conditional notice may not count.
4. Check every quoted clause number, date and name against the signed lease (`check-quotes-against-the-current-text`).
5. Ask for the OK on the exact notice. The owner or an authorised officer signs.
6. Send it by every method the clause requires, and by email as well for speed. Email alone counts only if the
   clause says so.
7. Save proof: the mailing receipt, tracking showing delivery, the signed copy. Ask the landlord to countersign or
   confirm receipt in writing, and chase until they do.
8. Update the abstract and the calendar for the new term, and tell `close-the-month` and
   `forecast-cash-and-budget`.

## Notices coming in

Any letter from a landlord, its lawyer or its lender is read the day it arrives. Write on the task what it is, the
clause it cites, and the date by which something must happen. A default notice, a demand, an estoppel or
subordination request is a hard stop under the skill's Gates. A rent increase or operating expense statement is
checked against the abstract before it is paid.

## Letting a lease end

Not renewing is also a decision with dates.

- Check whether the lease or membership renews automatically unless notice is given. Many flexible-space agreements
  do.
- Record the decision and the owner's OK on the abstract.
- Open the move-out task (`sublease-and-exit.md`) at least 90 days before expiry.
- Be out by the expiry date. Holdover rent applies from the next day.
