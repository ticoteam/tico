# Sources

All read on 2026-11-18 unless a line says otherwise.

## Authorities

- US Department of Justice, ADA Title III Technical Assistance Manual, III-1.2000: landlord and tenant are both
  public accommodations; a lease allocation works only between the parties. https://archive.ada.gov/taman3.html
- US Small Business Administration, Pick your business location: zoning, location-driven costs and taxes,
  registering and licensing where located.
  https://www.sba.gov/business-guide/launch-your-business/pick-your-business-location

## Practitioner guides (secondary; state law varies)

- Smith, Gambrell & Russell, "A Legal 'Minefield': Options to Renew in Leases" (2017-12-01; New York cases): strict
  compliance, time of the essence, conditions on default, no duty to remind, holding over is not renewal.
  https://www.sgrlaw.com/newsroom/publications/a-legal-minefield-options-to-renew-in-leases
- Troutman Pepper (Stefanie Brennan), on rentable and usable area and load factors (2013-05-31, via JD Supra):
  compare on usable area, name the measurement standard, a remeasurement right; its worked example gives a load
  factor of about 1.14. https://www.jdsupra.com/legalnews/getting-the-most-bang-for-your-buck-co-16976/
- FindLaw, Commercial lease negotiation checklist (updated 2016-06-20): rent and increases, shared costs, buildouts,
  sublease, short term with options, default and notice, verifying area.
  https://www.findlaw.com/legal/practice/how-to-start-a-law-firm/commercial-lease-negotiation-checklist.html
- Operating expenses (audit rights exist by contract, caps on controllable expenses of 3 to 5 percent as a
  negotiating benchmark, gross-up of the base year, exclusions): law-firm and broker articles found by search.
- Holdover rent of 150 percent, sometimes 175 to 200 percent: vendor and broker articles found by search. A market
  observation; the lease decides.
- Lease accounting (US standard ASC 842: leases longer than 12 months go on the balance sheet; an election for
  short-term leases): KPMG handbook and CPA firm summaries found by search. The accounting itself belongs to
  `close-the-month`.
