# Sources

Read 2026-11-18 unless a line says otherwise. All text in this skill is original. Where a licence limits reuse, only the
idea was taken.

## Tico's own skills and files (folded in)

- `templates/catalog/design-owner/skills/build-the-brand-book`: the staged method, the taste and compliance split,
  claims with IDs and Catch phrases, the rival swap, the voice test, real pieces marked us or not us, the rule pack,
  and the Brand card proposal shape.
- `templates/catalog/design-owner/skills/review-copy-against-the-brand-book`: the review steps (rule or suggestion,
  smallest fix, verdict first).
- `templates/catalog/design-owner/knowledge/brand-practice.md`: contrast minimums and the four tone scales.
- `docs/strategy.md`: what the Brand card holds and what `tico brand check` does and does not catch.

## Outside sources

- Mailchimp Content Style Guide, "Voice and Tone" (styleguide.mailchimp.com/voice-and-tone). Licence: CC BY-NC 4.0, so
  ideas only, no wording. Idea taken: the voice stays constant while tone shifts with the reader's state of mind, and
  humour is optional (stay straight when unsure).
- Anthropic, `knowledge-work-plugins`, marketing `brand-review` skill (Apache-2.0). Idea taken: a compliance pass that
  runs on every review even when no guide exists (unsupported claims, risky comparisons, unattributed testimonials,
  missing disclosures), and findings ranked with a fix each.
- alirezarezvani, `claude-skills`, `brand-guidelines` (MIT). Ideas taken: a tone matrix (traits by context with sample
  wording) and writing the guide so an outside contractor can work from it.
- Anthropic, `skills`, `brand-guidelines`. Idea taken: visuals as tokens (hex values, type by role, fallback fonts).
- W3C, Web Content Accessibility Guidelines 2.2, Success Criteria 1.4.3 and 1.4.11, and the relative-luminance and
  contrast-ratio definitions. The numbers 4.5:1 and 3:1 and the formula come from here.
- Nielsen Norman Group, "The Four Dimensions of Tone of Voice". The four scales used as a cross-check. Article date
  not confirmed.

## Not found in any source (written for this skill)

- The method for deriving a voice from a company's best existing copy (`derive-the-voice.md`). No public skill or guide
  we read gives a method; the Mailchimp guide shows the result only.
- Governance: owner, version, review date and change log.
