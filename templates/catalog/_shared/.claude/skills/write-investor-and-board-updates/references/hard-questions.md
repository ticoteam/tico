# The hard questions

Write these before the prose. An update that answers them before they are asked builds trust; one that avoids them
gets them asked in a worse setting. Pick the five that this period's numbers make most pointed, and answer each from
the records in two or three sentences.

## Money

1. How many months of runway are there on current burn, and on the plan? What changed since last time?
2. Why did burn move? Which lines?
3. When does the company need to raise, or reach break-even, and what has to be true by then?
4. Is any of the cash restricted, borrowed or already owed?
5. Is anything in revenue one-off, pulled forward, or from a related party?

## Growth

6. Where did new customers come from? How much was paid for, and what did each cost?
7. Who left, how much did they pay, and what reason did they give in their own words?
8. How concentrated is revenue? What share comes from the largest customer and the largest five?
9. Is the growth rate rising or falling over the last six periods?
10. What does a customer do in the first week, and what share get there?

## Plan

11. What did the last update say would happen that did not? Why?
12. What is the one thing most likely to make the company miss its plan?
13. What was tried and stopped this period? What did it cost?
14. Which strategy bet looks weakest on the evidence? What would prove it wrong?

## Team and risk

15. Who joined or left, people and bots, and why does it matter?
16. Is there any dispute, claim, incident or regulatory contact? (If yes: facts only here, details through counsel.)
17. What depends on a single supplier, platform or person?
18. What is the team not sure about?

## Using the answers

- If an answer is bad news, it is a lowlight. Write it there.
- If an answer is "we do not know", say so, with how and when the team will find out. Then make that a task.
- If an answer needs a number that would not tie, the figure is "not supplied" and the answer says so.
- If the owner's judgment is needed (questions 12, 14, 18), ask them once, with your draft answer as the default.
  Do not invent the owner's view.
- Keep the full list of questions and answers on the page under the figure sheet. For a board pack, they become the
  owner's preparation notes.

## Words to cut

Softening words hide the size of a problem and readers notice. Replace each with the number.

| Instead of | Write |
|------------|-------|
| "slightly below plan" | "8% below plan" |
| "some churn" | "3 customers, $9,400 a month" |
| "headwinds", "challenging quarter" | what happened |
| "we are excited to" | the fact |
| "on track" (with no number) | the reading against the target |
| "significant progress" | what is done and what is left |
