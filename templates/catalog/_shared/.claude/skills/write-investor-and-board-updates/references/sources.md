## Investor updates

- Aaron Harris (Y Combinator), "How to write the perfect investor update", as summarised by Visible.vc and others:
  lead with the key numbers and growth rates, keep the metrics the same every month, keep it short, put asks where
  they cannot be missed, include what is going badly. Ideas used: numbers first, fixed table, asks high, lowlights.
- Common practice across investor-update guides (Visible.vc, Carta, founder templates): monthly at the earliest
  stages, quarterly later. See `../../DISAGREEMENTS.md` for where they differ.
- lenny-skills (fundraising, communicating with stakeholders) and borghei/Claude-Skills (investor relations, board
  deck builder): ideas only (writing the hard questions first; scoring last period's promises). No wording used.
- Fred Wilson (AVC blog) on "no surprises" between founders and boards. Cited in `../../DISAGREEMENTS.md` from
  general knowledge; not re-read.

## Board packs

- Brad Feld and Mahendra Ramsinghani, *Startup Boards* (2013; second edition 2022): send materials days ahead, spend
  the meeting on discussion, separate formal items from substance. Ideas used: decisions first, pre-read, closed
  session.
- Sequoia Capital, "Preparing a Board Deck": a short structure that leads with the big picture and what has changed.
  Idea used: say what changed since last time; keep the same KPIs.
- National Venture Capital Association model Investor Rights Agreement: the usual shape of information rights
  (annual and quarterly financial statements to larger investors within set numbers of days; a budget). Used only to
  tell the bot to read the company's own agreement; no day counts are relied on.

## Law (United States; dated 2026-11-18; from general knowledge, not re-read at source)

- Securities Exchange Act section 10(b) and SEC Rule 10b-5: liability for material misstatements or omissions in
  connection with buying or selling securities applies to private companies too.
- Regulation D, Rule 506(b) and 506(c): limits on general solicitation in some private offerings. The skill only says
  "check before sending raise news to non-investors".
- State corporate law on stockholder inspection rights (in Delaware, General Corporation Law section 220, amended in
  2025). Not relied on for any step.
- Attorney-client privilege: sharing legal advice widely can lose its protection, which is why legal matters travel
  separately through counsel.
