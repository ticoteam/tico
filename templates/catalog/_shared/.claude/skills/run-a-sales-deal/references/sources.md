## Why one method, and why this one

Qualification methods overlap heavily. BANT starts from budget and authority; MEDDICC adds metrics, a named
champion, decision criteria and paper process and suits large deals with many stakeholders; SPIN is a questioning
technique rather than a qualification checklist. Using several at once doubles the form-filling without adding
judgment. SPICED was chosen as the default because its five facts are the same ones the proposal, the mutual action
plan and the handoff need, and because it fits the short-cycle deals most small teams run. A team selling large,
multi-stakeholder contracts may reasonably pick MEDDICC at setup; the skill then uses that alone. This choice is
logged for the owner in `skills/customers/DISAGREEMENTS.md`.

## Law to check locally (outreach)

Checked 2026-11-18 against the Tico catalog's outbound notes and public summaries. These change; read the current
text. A reply to someone who contacted the team is not cold outreach, but the same honesty rules apply.

- United States, email: the CAN-SPAM Act. No false headers or misleading subject lines, a valid postal address, a
  clear way to opt out, and opt-outs honoured within 10 business days. It applies to business-to-business email too.
- United States, calls and texts: the Telephone Consumer Protection Act and the Do Not Call rules restrict
  autodialled or prerecorded calls and texts and calls to numbers on the registry; several states add their own
  rules. Do not call or text a prospect without checking.
- United Kingdom and European Union: electronic marketing is covered by PECR in the UK and national ePrivacy laws in
  the EU, alongside UK GDPR and GDPR. Rules for emailing people at companies differ by country, and some require
  prior consent. Check the recipient's country before any cold email.
- Canada: CASL generally requires consent before commercial electronic messages.

## Numbers in this skill

The one-hour reply, the 120-word limit, the follow-up spacing (2, 5, 7 and 10 working days), the 14-day "stuck"
line, the 30-day quote validity and the two-to-four-week pilot are starting defaults chosen for this library. None
is a benchmark. The team's own values in `knowledge/sales.md` replace them.
