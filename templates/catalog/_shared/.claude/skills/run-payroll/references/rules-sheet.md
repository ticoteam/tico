# Rules sheet (read 2026-11-18)

A starting list, not a source. The provider applies these; the bot uses them to check the provider and to explain a
number. Re-read each at its source before relying on it. "Secondary" means not confirmed on the authority's page.

## Federal payroll taxes

| Rule | Value | Read at |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Social Security tax | 6.2 percent each for employer and employee, on wages up to 184,500 dollars for 2026 | IRS Publication 15 (2026) |
| Social Security wage base for 2027 | 192,300 dollars, announced by SSA on 2026-10-14 | Secondary (Journal of Accountancy, payroll.org) |
| Medicare tax | 1.45 percent each, no wage limit; an additional employee-only tax applies to high wages, not read today | IRS Publication 15 (2026) |
| Federal unemployment tax (FUTA) | 6.0 percent on the first 7,000 dollars of each employee's wages; a credit of up to 5.4 percent for state unemployment tax paid brings it to 0.6 percent; smaller credit in "credit reduction" states named each year | IRS Topic 759, updated 2026-11-09 |
| Deposit schedule | Set by the lookback period (the 12 months starting July 1 of the second preceding year): 50,000 dollars or less of tax means monthly, more means semiweekly | IRS Topic 757, updated 2026-11-10 |
| Monthly depositor | Deposit by the 15th of the following month | IRS employment tax due dates page, updated 2026-04-21 |
| Semiweekly depositor | Paydays Wednesday to Friday: by the following Wednesday. Paydays Saturday to Tuesday: by the following Friday | Same page |
| 100,000 dollar rule | 100,000 dollars or more of tax on any day is due the next business day | Same page |
| Small liability | Under 2,500 dollars for the quarter may be paid with the return | IRS Topic 757 |
| FUTA deposit | When the cumulative amount owed goes over 500 dollars | IRS employment tax due dates page |
| How to deposit | Electronically: IRS business tax account, Direct Pay for businesses, or EFTPS (EFTPS by 8 p.m. Eastern the day before) | IRS Topic 757 |
| Form 941 due | April 30, July 31, October 31, January 31; 10 more days if every deposit was on time | IRS employment tax due dates page |
| Form 940 due | January 31; February 10 if every deposit was on time | Same page |
| Forms W-2 and W-3 due | To employees and to SSA by January 31 | Same page |
| Records | Keep employment tax records at least 4 years | IRS Publication 15 (2026) |
| Personal liability | People responsible for withheld taxes that go unpaid can be held personally liable (trust fund recovery penalty) | IRS Publication 15 (2026) |
