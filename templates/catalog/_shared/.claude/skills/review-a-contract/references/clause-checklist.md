# Missing-clause checklist

A contract is also judged by what it leaves out. For the type under review, find each item below by meaning, not by
heading. Give every item a row in the review: the section number, or "not found in the text".

When an item is not found, check three places before saying so: the definitions, the exhibits and order form, and any
terms pulled in by a link. Then rate the row against the playbook line in the last column.

## Every contract

| # | The contract should say | Playbook line |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Who the parties are: full legal names, entity types and addresses, matching the signature block | Header |
| 2 | What is being provided, in enough detail to tell whether it was delivered | none: flag if vague |
| 3 | The price, when it is due, and what happens to the price at renewal | B12 / S12 |
| 4 | When it starts, how long it lasts, and how it renews | B7 / S7 |
| 5 | How each side can end it, and what happens to money already paid | B7 / S7 |
| 6 | A limit on liability, and what sits outside the limit | B1 / S1 |
| 7 | Who defends third-party claims | B2 / S2 |
| 8 | Confidentiality | B5 / S5 |
| 9 | Which law applies and where disputes go | B8 / S8 |
| 10 | Whether it can be assigned, and what a change of ownership does | B10 / S10 |
| 11 | How notices must be given, and to whom | none: record it for `count-a-notice-deadline` |
| 12 | Which document wins when the agreement, order form and exhibits conflict | none: flag if absent and they conflict |
| 13 | That the written contract is the whole agreement, and how it can be changed (in writing, by both) | none: flag a right for one side to change it alone |
| 14 | A signature block for a person with authority, for each party | Header |

## Non-disclosure agreement

| # | The agreement should say | Playbook line |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Who discloses and who receives, or that it is mutual | N1 |
| 2 | The purpose for which information may be used | N3 |
| 3 | What counts as confidential, and the five exclusions | N2 |
| 4 | Who else may see it, and that the recipient answers for them | N3 |
| 5 | How long the agreement runs and how long the duty lasts; trade secrets treated separately | N4 |
| 6 | Disclosure required by law: allowed, with notice where lawful | N2 |
| 7 | Return or destruction on request | N7 |
| 8 | That no licence is granted and neither side must go on to a deal | N6 |
| 9 | Governing law and forum | N8 |
| 10 | For an individual: the trade-secret whistleblower notice | N9 |

Also search for what should not be there: residuals, non-solicit, non-compete, exclusivity, standstill, a licence,
audit rights, indemnity, liquidated damages (N5, N6, N7).

## Services or software agreement, the company buying

| # | The agreement should say | Playbook line |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | What service levels apply (uptime, response times) and the remedy when they are missed | none: flag if the service is critical and nothing is promised |
| 2 | That the company owns its data and can export it during the term and for a period after | B3, B7 |
| 3 | What the vendor may do with the company's data, and that it may do nothing else | B4 |
| 4 | Security measures, breach notice and subprocessors; a data processing agreement where personal data is handled | B4 |
| 5 | A warranty that the service matches its documentation, with a remedy | B6 |
| 6 | The vendor's indemnity for intellectual property claims | B2 |
| 7 | A refund of prepaid fees if the company ends for the vendor's breach | B7 |
| 8 | Notice before any price increase, and a cap | B12 |
| 9 | Notice before the vendor suspends service | B12 |
| 10 | The vendor's insurance | B9 |
| 11 | Help with moving out at the end (export format, period, cost) | B7 |
| 12 | For work made for the company: who owns the deliverables | B3 |

## Services or software agreement, the company selling

| # | The agreement should say | Playbook line |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A cap on the company's liability and an exclusion of indirect loss | S1 |
| 2 | A disclaimer of warranties beyond the stated one | S6 |
| 3 | The exclusions from the company's intellectual property indemnity | S2 |
| 4 | That the company keeps the service and its improvements, and may use feedback | S3 |
| 5 | The customer's duties: lawful use, its own data, keeping logins secure | S2 |
| 6 | The right to suspend for non-payment or misuse, with notice | none: flag if absent |
| 7 | Payment terms and interest on late amounts | S12 |
| 8 | Renewal and the notice period | S7 |
| 9 | That service levels, if any, give credits as the only remedy | none: flag service levels with open-ended remedies |
| 10 | Permission, or not, to name the customer publicly | none: follow the customer's wording; never assume permission |

## Statement of work or order form

| # | It should say | Check |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Which agreement governs it, by name and date | The named agreement exists and is signed |
| 2 | Deliverables, dates and acceptance: how and by when the customer accepts or rejects | Flag "deemed accepted" periods under 5 business days when buying |
| 3 | Fees, invoicing points and expenses | Against B12 / S12 |
| 4 | Assumptions and what each side must supply | Flag dependencies on the company with no dates |
| 5 | How changes are agreed | In writing by both |
| 6 | Nothing that quietly changes the governing agreement | Compare liability, intellectual property, payment and term |
