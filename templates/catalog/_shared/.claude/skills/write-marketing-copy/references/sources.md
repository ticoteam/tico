## Law and rules referred to (US default)

- FTC Endorsement Guides, 16 CFR Part 255: material connections are disclosed; a testimonial's result should be what
  customers generally get, or say that it is not.
- FTC Trade Regulation Rule on the Use of Consumer Reviews and Testimonials, 16 CFR Part 465, effective 2024-10-21:
  no fake or bought reviews or testimonials, no insider reviews without disclosure.
- Restore Online Shoppers' Confidence Act (ROSCA), 15 U.S.C. 8401 to 8405, and state auto-renewal laws: clear terms
  and consent before a recurring charge, and a simple way to cancel. State rules differ and change: check with the
  legal owner before changing how renewal or cancellation is worded.
- Outside the US, comparative advertising, pricing display and testimonial rules differ. Check locally.
