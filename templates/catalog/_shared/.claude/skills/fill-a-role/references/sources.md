## Role definition and hiring method

- Geoff Smart and Randy Street, *Who* (2008): the scorecard of mission, outcomes and competencies, written before the
  search. Idea used: define the role by outcomes; the scorecard is the standard for the review.
- Google re:Work, "Use structured interviewing" guide (rework.withgoogle.com): same questions, a rubric, independent
  scoring. Idea used: the three parts of "structured".
- Paul Sackett, Charlene Zhang, Christopher Berry and Filip Lievens, "Revisiting meta-analytic estimates of validity in
  personnel selection", *Journal of Applied Psychology*, 2022: structured interviews ranked as the strongest single
  predictor in the revised estimates. Read via published summaries; the paper itself was not re-read.
- Frank Schmidt and John Hunter, "The validity and utility of selection methods in personnel psychology",
  *Psychological Bulletin*, 1998; Nathan Kuncel and colleagues, "Mechanical versus clinical data combination in
  selection and admissions decisions", *Journal of Applied Psychology*, 2013; Daniel Kahneman, Olivier Sibony and Cass
  Sunstein, *Noise* (2021). Cited in `../../DISAGREEMENTS.md` from general knowledge; not re-read.
- lenny-skills (hiring and interviewing skills) and borghei/Claude-Skills (HR skills): ideas only (work-sample tasks,
  writing scores before the debrief). No wording used.
- Anthropic knowledge-work plugins, human-resources (Apache 2.0): the shape of a recruiting pipeline and interview
  prep as separate steps.

## Law (United States; all dated 2026-11-18)

- Pay-range-in-posting laws: state labour agency pages; summaries from employment law firms and payroll vendors listing
  the states in effect as of May 2026.
- New York City Local Law 144 of 2021 (automated employment decision tools), enforced from 5 July 2023: NYC Department
  of Consumer and Worker Protection.
- Illinois HB 3773 (amending the Illinois Human Rights Act), in effect 1 January 2026.
- California Civil Rights Council regulations on automated-decision systems in employment, in effect 1 October 2025.
- Colorado SB 24-205 and later amendments: status changed during 2025 and 2026; not relied on.
- US Department of Labor, independent contractor rule: 2024 rule, May 2025 non-enforcement, February 2026 proposed
  replacement. Internal Revenue Service common-law test. California Labor Code section 2775 (ABC test).
- Form 1099-NEC reporting threshold: raised to 2,000 US dollars for payments after 31 December 2025 by the 2025 budget
  reconciliation law; IRS instructions for Forms 1099-MISC and 1099-NEC.
- Hiring record retention: 29 CFR 1602.14 (one year); California Government Code section 12946 (four years).
- Form I-9 timing: US Citizenship and Immigration Services, Handbook for Employers M-274.
