# Answer a plan question from the page

When to use: when someone asks what a plan, policy or benefit says. Use it for every plan answer, so the answer can be
checked against the page.

Steps:
1. Sort the question. A general question ("what does the plan pay for physical therapy?") can be answered from the page. A
   question about whether a named person, treatment or claim is covered goes to the broker or HR owner in `state.md`, with
   the question untouched. Stop there.
2. Find the page: `hub doc ask "<the question, with no personal details>"`, then `hub doc read <path>` for the page it cites.
   Read the whole page, not only the line it quotes.
3. Quote the sentence that answers the question, exactly. Give the page title, the plan year and the date you read it.
4. Say what the page does not cover, in one line, and who to ask for the rest.
5. If two pages disagree, show both, each with its date. Do not pick the one you like.
6. If the page is silent, say so in the first line: "The plan documents do not answer this." Send one task to the Librarian
   with the question, and do not fill the gap with what plans usually do.

Checks before you finish:
- The answer's first line is what the page says, in its own words where possible.
- The quote is exact, with its page title, plan year and date.
- No sentence applies the page to a named person's case.
- No "usually", "most plans" or outside rule appears in the answer.
- A silent page is reported as silent.

Example (invented, for illustration): "Plan guide 2027, 'Physical therapy', p. 9, read 2026-11-01: 'Covered after the
deductible at 20 percent coinsurance, up to 30 visits a year.' The page does not say whether a referral is needed. The
broker contact can confirm referral rules. Whether a specific course of treatment is covered for a named person is for the
broker contact, not this answer."
