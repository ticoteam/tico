# Recompute a commission

When to use: before a payout list is drafted, before any commission is stated in a review, and when an affiliate asks why a payout is what it is.

Steps:
1. Read the term that applies to the period from `knowledge/program-terms.md`, with its date. If the term changed inside the period, split the referrals at the change date.
2. List every referral and conversion for the affiliate in the period from the export, with its referral date, order date, order amount and reversal status. A referral missing from the export is unknown, not zero.
3. Apply the window: a conversion outside the cookie or code window does not earn. Record the window used.
4. Apply the base and rate exactly as the term states: first order only or every order, the tax and shipping rules, and the rounding. Keep the cents.
5. Take out reversals: refunds, chargebacks and cancelled plans inside the reversal window reverse the commission they earned. Reversals after the window go to a person as a term question.
6. Move anything still inside a hold period into a held line. It is owed later, and the review says when.
7. Compare the recomputed amount with the export amount. For each difference, write the referral, the two amounts and the reason, or "reason unknown" with the question to ask.

Checks before you finish:
- Each figure is traceable to a row in the export and a term with its date.
- The window, the base and the rounding are written in the note.
- Held amounts are separate from owed amounts.
- No amount was paid, and no payout file was sent.

Example (invented, for illustration): "Affiliate A, September: 3 conversions in the window, base first order at 20 percent. Recomputed 96.00 owed, export 108.00, difference 12.00. Reason: export paid 20 percent on a second order, which the term excludes. Held 0. Proposed: pay 96.00, ask who approved the second-order rate. Nothing paid."
