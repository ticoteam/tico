# Score a lead against the profile

When to use: before you count a lead as qualified, before a hand-off, and before you write a reason a lead is not qualified.

Steps:
1. Read `knowledge/lead-method.md` for the rule and the profile. If the profile is not written yet, stop and ask the person who
   runs sales. Count nothing as qualified until it is.
2. Source: the lead has one source tag. With no tag, it is "no source", not qualified, and listed for marketing-ops.
3. Duplicate: is there an earlier lead for the same person or company that is open or already counted this week? If so, the
   later one is "duplicate".
4. Not blocked: is it already a customer, a competitor, or on the do-not-contact list? Then the reason is "already a customer"
   or "blocked", and it is not handed off.
5. Dated interest: is there a sign of interest in the last 90 days (a request, a form, a trial, a reply, a booked call, a
   referral)? If not, the reason is "no dated interest".
6. Fit: do the segment, size, region and buyer role match the profile? Each match cites a dated public fact or a CRM field. If
   not, the reason is "no match to profile".
7. Qualified only when steps 2 to 6 all pass. Otherwise record the first failing step as the one reason.
8. Write the line: the result, the reason or the matches, and the source of each fact. For example: "Qualified: demo request
   3 Nov (form); 22-person studio (company site, read 3 Nov); buyer role: operations lead (company site)."

Checks before you finish:
- Every qualified lead has a source, a dated interest and a fit line with its source.
- Each not-qualified lead has one reason, the first failing step.
- The reason and the match contain no personal details.
- The qualified and not-qualified totals add up to the arrivals.
