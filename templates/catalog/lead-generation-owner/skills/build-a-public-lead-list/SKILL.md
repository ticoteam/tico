# Build a public lead list

When to use: when you build or add to a list in `knowledge/lists/`, for the outbound sales owner.

Steps:
1. Read the list's brief: segment, size, region, buyer role, the count wanted, the reason and the date needed. If the brief has
   no profile, ask before you search.
2. Check `knowledge/lists/` for an existing list on the same segment. Add to it, do not duplicate it.
3. Build the exclusions first: existing customers, competitors, the do-not-contact list, and companies already in an open
   sequence. Count each one.
4. Find companies from public sources only: company websites, news, job posts, public directories and company registries.
   Do not use pages behind a login. Do not use scraped personal profiles. Respect each site's terms and its robots rules.
5. For each company, record its name, its website, the segment it matches, the dated public fact that shows it, and the buyer
   role title from a public page.
6. Contact details: only the company's general public channel, such as its general address or contact form. Never a personal
   email address or phone number.
7. Score each company against the profile with `skills/score-a-lead-against-the-profile/SKILL.md`, as a target. Keep the matches.
   Count the rest with their reasons.
8. Save the list with its brief, its date, the counts kept and dropped, the exclusions counted, and each company's source and date.

Checks before you finish:
- No personal contact details appear anywhere in the list.
- Every company has its source and date.
- The exclusions are applied and counted.
- The list is handed over as a task to the outbound sales owner, and nothing is sent.
