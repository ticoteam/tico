```markdown
# Quarry Lane lead report, week of 2 to 8 Nov 2026

Sample output for Quarry Lane, a fictional maker of site-survey software. Nothing was sent to any lead.

**Headline: 61 qualified leads this week, up from a four-week average of 54. Inbound sources carry 57 of them. Trial
sign-ups qualify at 21%, down from 30% on average, and that is the one source to look at.**

## Qualified leads by source (arrivals 2 to 8 Nov, CRM export pulled 9 Nov)

| Source | Arrived | Qualified | Rate | Four-week rate | Hand-off to |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Demo request form | 24 | 18 | 75% | 72% | Inbound |
| Trial sign-up | 58 | 12 | 21% | 30% | Inbound |
| Organic search (content pages) | 33 | 14 | 42% | 39% | Inbound |
| Referral partners | 11 | 9 | 82% | 80% | Inbound |
| Partner marketplace listing | 6 | 3 | 50% | 44% | Inbound |
| Event follow-up | 1 | 1 | not rated | not rated | Inbound |
| Replies to outbound sequences | 6 | 4 | 67% | 60% | Outbound |
| **Total** | **139** | **61** | **44%** | **45%** |

## Not qualified: 78, by the first step that failed

- No match to profile: 41. Of these, 22 are companies under the profile's size floor, from their public site, and 19 are in
  regions the team does not sell to.
- No dated interest: 19.
- Duplicate of a lead already counted this week: 11.
- Already a customer: 7, checked against the customer list in the CRM.
- No source: 0.

## Hand-offs

- Inbound sales owner: 57 handed off, each as a task created by Friday 6 Nov, each with its source, reason, dated signal and
  pick-up time. Three were not picked up by their same-business-day time: tasks 2281, 2284 and 2290, all from Friday
  evening. Named here for the owner.
- Outbound sales owner: 4 handed off. One reply, from Thursday 5 Nov, is past its two-business-day time at Monday 09:00.
  Named here for the owner.

## Targets (not leads, not in the 61)

- One list built on 5 Nov: plumbing trade software, 120 companies, from public sources only. Each company carries its source,
  the date it was read and the rule it matched. Handed to the outbound sales owner. No contact details beyond each company's
  general public channel. Nothing was sent.

## The source to look at (one proposal)

Trial sign-ups: 12 of 58 qualify (21%) against 30% over the last four weeks. The sign-up form asks for a name and an email
address only. Proposal, as a task for the person who runs sales and not a change: ask the product owner whether company size
and role can be asked at sign-up. Check: the same rate, on the same definition, is read in the report of 16 Nov.

## Could not count

- Spend per source: no spend export was attached, so cost per qualified lead is not measured.
- Marketplace listings before 2 Nov: their source tag was added on 2 Nov, so earlier weeks are not comparable.
- Event follow-ups: one arrival, too few to rate.

## Sources

- CRM export of leads with source tag, stage, owner and first-touch time, 2 to 8 Nov 2026, pulled 9 Nov.
- Hand-off tasks created 2 to 8 Nov, from the task list.
- `knowledge/lead-method.md`, read 9 Nov.
- Source tag convention, from the marketing-ops owner, read 3 Nov.
```
