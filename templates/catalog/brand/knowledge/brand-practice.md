# Brand practice

The standing method behind `playbooks/monthly-brand-audit.md` and `playbooks/review-an-asset.md`. `brand.md` holds
the team's own rules. This file holds what is true about brand work in general, so each run does not re-derive it.
Every figure carries its source and the year it was read.

## Voice and tone

- Voice is the stable personality, three to five traits. Tone is how that voice adapts to a moment: a launch, an
  apology, a price change, an error message. Keep the voice constant and let the tone move.
- Nielsen Norman Group scores tone on four scales: funny to serious, formal to casual, respectful to irreverent, and
  enthusiastic to matter-of-fact. Pick a few tone words and pair them with words to avoid. A long list is not usable.
- Test tone on real readers. A reviewer's reading of tone differs from a reader's. NN/G's experiments found that
  changing only the tone of identical copy moved how people rated a brand's friendliness and trustworthiness.
- Humour is the riskiest tone. Error messages skip it: people meet them under stress, often more than once.

## Colour and contrast

- WCAG 2.2 (W3C Recommendation, 2023), Success Criterion 1.4.3, Level AA: text needs a contrast ratio of at least
  4.5:1 against its background. Large text (18 point, or 14 point bold) needs 3:1.
- WCAG 2.2, Success Criterion 1.4.11, Level AA: the visual cues that show a control or its state, and graphics needed
  to understand the content, need 3:1 against the adjacent colour. Purely decorative graphics are exempt.
- The ratios are not rounded. A computed 4.49:1 fails 4.5:1. Text inside a logo follows the logo exemption, not the
  text rule, and the book should say which logo versions are approved.

## Trademark first-pass searches

- The USPTO retired TESS on 30 November 2023 and replaced it with a cloud-based trademark search system (USPTO, 2023).
  The new system uses different syntax: the combined-field tag became `CM:`. Its exact-match, single-term "Wordmark"
  search is the basic knockout. The newest release note found was from May 2024, so check the current interface
  before writing steps for it.
- EUIPO eSearch plus is free and offers basic and advanced search, image search and similarity reports. TMview is
  free and covers the EU national offices, the EUIPO and many offices outside the EU (EUIPO help pages, read
  2026-11-01; some pages show older dates).
- A warning letter from a rights holder is not a finding. The EUIPO notes that only a final decision in opposition or
  cancellation proceedings settles a conflict. Record a letter as a claim, with its date.
- A register search shows what is filed or registered. It does not show what is in use. Use app stores, company
  registries and the web for use. This split is practice, not a published figure.

## Monthly audit sample

- Twelve to fifteen items, spread across the surfaces the book names, weighted toward what most people see. This is a
  working size that fits in an afternoon. It is not a statistical sample, so report counts and do not generalise a
  percentage from it.
