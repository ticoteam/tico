# Content practice

The standing method behind `playbooks/draft-a-post.md` and `playbooks/weekly-content-plan.md`. `voice.md` holds the team's
own voice and boundaries. This file holds what is true about publishing in general. Each source carries the year it was read.

## Helpful and people-first

- Google's Search Essentials ask for helpful, reliable, people-first content. They say AI use is not banned, and that
  content made mainly to manipulate rankings is spam (Google Search Central, "Creating helpful, reliable, people-first
  content" and the 2023 blog post on AI content; read 2026-11-01 through search excerpts).
- Google's May 2026 guide on optimising for generative AI features tells publishers to avoid content that only restates
  what a model could easily produce. It treats a separate page for every query variation, made mainly to manipulate
  rankings or AI answers, as scaled content abuse (Google Search Central, read 2026-11-01 through search excerpts).
- The Search Central updates page lists an October update to the generative AI content guidance. The change was not read
  for this file. Check it before you quote the guidance.

## AI-text detectors

- OpenAI launched an AI text classifier in January 2023 and withdrew it on 20 July 2023 for a low rate of accuracy. In its
  own evaluation it flagged about 26% of AI-written text and wrongly flagged about 9% of human text. It was unreliable on
  text under 1,000 characters (OpenAI's withdrawal note and press coverage, 2023; read 2026-11-01 through search excerpts).
- Practice: a detector score is a noisy signal. Use it to find generic sentences and missing facts, never to tune rhythm
  or vocabulary. Record the score, its label and the number of rewrites beside the draft.

## Claims

- A claim keeps its basis. A number carries its source and date. A price carries its unit and any add-on that changes it
  (practice).
- A promise in an ad appears on the page it links to. If the page does not say it, the ad does not say it (practice).
- A guarantee keeps its terms beside it. Any legal wording goes to the owner or counsel (practice).

## Originality

- Original means a fact, a case or a number only the team has, with its source. A post made of generic tips could be
  written by any competitor (practice).
- Ideas drawn from documentation or product notes are not reader demand. Label them so the plan does not read them as
  questions readers asked (practice).

## Channel versions

- One piece becomes separate versions when the caption, the ask and the proof change for each channel. The claims stay
  the same and each cut is checked again.
- Platform ranking rules change. Check each platform's current documentation before you write a channel rule, and date
  the rule (practice).
