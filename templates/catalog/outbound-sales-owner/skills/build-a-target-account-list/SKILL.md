# Build a target account list

When to use: when a new target list is needed, when a contact must be replaced after a wrong-person reply, and before any
contact enters a sequence.

Steps:
1. Start from the ideal customer profile in `knowledge/icp.md`: the firmographics, the rules that rule accounts out, and the
   roles to reach. Do not start from a bought or scraped list. If one is handed to you, record its source and its lawful basis
   first, or do not use it.
2. Build candidate accounts from public sources: the sector, the size band, the location, and any stack the profile names.
   Record where each came from and the date you read it.
3. Score fit against the profile, one criterion at a time, each met, not met or unknown. An unknown is not a fit.
4. Add a dated signal to each account that scores on fit: a hire, a launch, a new location, a change of tool, a published
   problem. The signal must be within the last 90 days. An older signal is context, not a reason to write.
5. Choose one contact per account by role, from a current public source: the team page, a dated profile, the company's own
   post. Use two sources where you can. Use only business details; never a private person's.
6. Check each contact before it is listed as ready. Look at the do-not-contact list, the customer list and any open deal with
   another seller; a match means out. Confirm the channel is a business address for the purpose, and that the consent basis for
   the market is recorded in `knowledge/outbound-method.md`.
7. Rank the accounts, cap the week to what can be researched and checked, and write the list into
   `knowledge/target-accounts.md` with the date of each check.

Checks before you finish:
- Each account has a fit verdict, a dated source, and a contact chosen by role from a current source.
- Every contact has passed the suppression, customer and open-deal checks, and each check is dated.
- No contact came from a list without a recorded source and lawful basis.
- The list is short enough that every account on it was looked at this week.

Example (invented, for illustration): "Account: a regional bakery group with six sites. Fit: met (size and sector in the
profile; its locations page read 2026-11-02). Signal: opened a shared commissary kitchen, its news page, dated 2026-10-20.
Contact: operations manager, from the team page read 2026-11-02. Checks: not on the do-not-contact list; no customer match; no
open deal. Ready."
