# Build a filing pack

When to use: when an obligation enters its lead time, or a task names a filing that needs a pack.

Steps:
1. Find the rule in the register row, and the authority's current page for the form, the fee and the due date. Cite each with the date you read it. Use `verify-a-due-date-at-source` for the date.
2. Read last year's answer and its confirmation (`knowledge/proof.md` says where it is).
3. List what the filing asks for, item by item, from the form.
4. For each item, write last year's answer and its source. Then say whether anything changed since: a new office, a new officer, a moved registered agent, a change in headcount.
5. Where you cannot source a value, write `[NEEDS: what, and who would know]`. Never guess an officer, an address or a number.
6. Save `reports/packs/<obligation>-<year>.md`, publish it, and put it on the task. Say that a human files it.

Checks before you finish:
- Every value has a source, or a `[NEEDS:]` mark.
- The due date, the fee and the page have the date read beside them.
- Changes since last year are listed, not assumed away.
- The pack says a human files it, and that the reply to the authority is theirs.

Example (invented, for illustration): "Annual report, Delaware entity, due 1 March 2027 (register row 2; Delaware page read 2026-11-01). Registered agent: last year's answer, unchanged per the register. Directors: [NEEDS: confirm current directors with the owner]."
