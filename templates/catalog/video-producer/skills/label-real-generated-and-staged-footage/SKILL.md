# Label real, generated and staged footage

When to use: when a shot list is written, when a cut is checked, and before any file is published or shown to a viewer.

Steps:
1. Classify each shot in one of five ways:
   - Real: filmed or captured from a real person or place, with their written agreement if they can be identified.
   - Recorded demo: captured from the demo workspace or demo data, with the run's date.
   - Compressed or sample: sped up, cut from a longer run, or using sample numbers.
   - Generated: made by an image, voice or video model. A generated person is always generated.
   - Drawn: motion graphics or illustration built from the team's own assets.
2. Put the label on the screen where a viewer can see it, for any shot that is not real or that is compressed: a short
   caption such as "Demo data" or "Sped up 4x". A storyboard is labelled "Storyboard, not final" in its sheet.
3. Record the same class on the task and in the shot's sidecar, so the owner can see it before approving.
4. Check voices and faces. A cloned or synthetic voice, or a swapped face, needs the written agreement of the person it
   imitates. Without it, the shot does not ship.
5. Check the platform's disclosure field before upload. Several platforms ask whether a realistic video is synthetic and
   label it when the creator says yes; some label it without a question. Check the platform's current help page, and
   answer truthfully every time.
6. Check that no label is removed in the final cut, and that a compressed or generated shot is not presented as real
   footage elsewhere in the campaign.

Checks before you finish:
- Every shot has one of the five classes, on the task and in its sidecar.
- Every on-screen label that the class needs is visible in the final frame, not only in the sheet.
- Each cloned voice or swapped face has a written agreement on file, or it is cut.
- The platform's disclosure answer matches the classes in the cut.

Example (invented, for illustration): "Shot 5: generated presenter, labelled 'AI presenter' in the lower third and in the
sidecar. Shot 6: recorded demo, 2026-10-28, labelled 'Demo data'. Shot 7: sped up 4x, labelled. Upload: the platform's
synthetic-content answer is yes, because shot 5 is a realistic generated person. The voice is generated, so the agreement
line is 'not applicable: no real person's voice'."
