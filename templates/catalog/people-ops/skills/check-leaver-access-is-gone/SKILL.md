# Check leaver access is gone

When to use: the day after a leaver's last day, and whenever a checklist item says access, an account or a device must be removed.

Steps:
1. List the access items for the leaver from the checklist, privileged first: admin, finance, production and owner roles, then
   single sign-on and mail, then the other tools.
2. For each item, read its task or its owner's reply. Accept only a dated confirmation from the owner that says the access is
   removed or changed. "Done" with no date, or a note from the leaver, is not a confirmation.
3. Mark each item confirmed, open, or late. Open means the owner has not answered yet. Late means the last day has passed with no
   confirmation.
4. Chase each open or late item once, on its task, with the leaver's reference and the item.
5. Send privileged access that is still open a day after the last day straight to the HR owner, in the first line of the page.
   Do not wait for the next weekly check.
6. Update `knowledge/leavers.md` with each item's status and the date of its confirmation.

Checks before you finish:
- Every access item is confirmed, open or late, and none is marked done without a dated confirmation.
- Privileged items were checked within 24 hours of the last day.
- No reason for leaving appears on the item or in the file.

Example (generic, invented): A Finance leaver's last day is 2026-09-25. On 2026-09-26 the IT admin confirms the admin role on
the billing tool is removed (dated). The mail item has no reply, so it is chased once. Because the mail item is not privileged,
it stays late on the page until the owner answers.
