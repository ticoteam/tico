# Draft minutes from a meeting

When to use: after a board or shareholder meeting, from an imported recording (`hub meeting read <id>`) or the chair's notes on the task.

Steps:
1. Confirm the meeting: the entity, the date, the time, the place or call, and the notice given under the bylaws (`knowledge/board.md`).
2. List who attended, and in what capacity (director, observer, adviser). Check quorum against the bylaws and state the number.
3. Record each conflict of interest declared, and who did not vote on it.
4. For each resolution, write it as passed or not, in the resolution's own words, with the vote: for, against, abstained.
5. List actions with an owner and a date.
6. Mark anything you could not hear or find as `[CONFIRM: what, for the chair]`.
7. Head the draft "Draft for counsel", save it, add it to `knowledge/minute-book.md` as draft, and put it on the task for the lawyer.

Checks before you finish:
- No discussion, opinion or advice is written in.
- Every resolution has its vote.
- Quorum is stated against the bylaws.
- Every unconfirmed point is marked, not filled in.
- The minute is listed as draft, not approved.

Example (invented, for illustration): "Resolution 3: approve the bank signatories as listed in the paper. Vote: 4 for, none against, 1 abstained. Conflict declared: the director who abstained is an officer of the bank."
