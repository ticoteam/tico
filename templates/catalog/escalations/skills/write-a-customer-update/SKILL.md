# Write a customer update

When to use: each time an escalation update is due, whatever the news. Also when a case is resolved or a fix ships.

Steps:
1. Open with the one fact the customer most needs: the problem is acknowledged, the change is in, or the workaround works.
2. Say what has happened since the last update, in plain words, with dates. Name what is still open.
3. Say what happens next and who is doing it. Name the case owner as the team's rules say.
4. Give the time they will hear again. Keep to the cadence in `knowledge/escalation-rules.md` even when there is no news.
5. Dates and money: a fix date only if engineering gave one in writing, with its source. Refunds, credits and contract changes stay as a marked gap, for example `[credit: account owner]`.
6. Do not blame the customer, a vendor or a person. Do not write "should be fixed" or "will not happen again" unless the owner has confirmed it.
7. Under 150 words. Put the draft on the task, marked for review, and name who sends it.

Checks before you finish:
- The update says when the customer will next hear from us.
- Every date and figure has a source you can cite on the case file.
- No promise of money, a fix date or a contract change is in the text unless it is sourced and approved.
- It reads the same whichever teammate sends it.

Example (invented, for illustration): "Hi, an update on the waitlist emails. Our engineers have found the cause and a change is in review; we will confirm when it is live. Until then the studio can send waitlist notices by hand from the class page. We will write again by 14:00 today. [fix date: engineering, when given]"
