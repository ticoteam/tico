# Billing practice

The standing reference behind the checks in `skills/`. It holds what makes invoices get paid, the rules that change by country, and the dated sources. A
rule that varies by country is a question for the Tax Specialist or the company's accountant, and the register records which rule applies to each customer.

## Why invoices come back

- Missing or expired PO numbers, the wrong legal entity, and the wrong billing contact are the most common reasons an accounts team returns an invoice.
  Checking them before issue costs minutes; a returned invoice restarts the customer's approval.
- Invoice errors feed late payment and dispute time. PYMNTS and American Express, "From Friction to Flow" (June 2025, read 2026-11-01 through a secondary
  summary): 27% of finance respondents said at least half their receivables team's time goes to resolving invoice disputes. Figures to confirm against the
  report before quoting.

## Fields a valid invoice carries

A general list; the rules for each country override it. Check the local rules before any new customer or any new country.
- Seller and buyer legal names and addresses, and the tax identifiers each jurisdiction requires.
- Invoice number in sequence, issue date, and the date of supply or the period it covers.
- Description, quantity, unit price, net amount, tax rate and tax amount, total, currency.
- Payment terms, the payment route, and the PO number where the customer requires one.

## Electronic invoicing (verify before you rely on it)

- EU e-invoicing is phasing in by country. Most sources read in 2026 put France's structured e-invoicing start for large companies at 1 September 2026, with
  smaller companies from September 2027, and every company required to receive e-invoices. Confirm against the French tax administration (DGFiP) before use.
- Germany: most sources say businesses must be able to receive e-invoices from 1 January 2025. Issuing obligations are phased for 2027 and 2028 in some
  sources and not in others. Confirm against the Federal Ministry of Finance.
- Belgium: one source says B2B e-invoicing became mandatory on 1 January 2026. Confirm against the Belgian authority.
- The EU's ViDA framework covers cross-border B2B e-invoicing and digital reporting. Confirm its dates against the Official Journal.
- The team's register states the format each customer needs. The bot never chooses a format.

Sources for this section were read through secondary summaries, 2026-11-01. None was read in full.

## Credit notes and corrections

- A sent invoice is never edited. The correction is a credit note that cites the original number, and a new invoice.
- A goodwill discount is the seller's decision. Prepare it only when the seller gives the amount.
