# Check a trademark deadline

When to use: before any deadline for a mark goes on the register, into the watch, or into a note to an owner.

Steps:
1. Take the registration date from the certificate or the office's record, not from memory. Name the office.
2. Work out each window in that office's rule. For a US registration: the declaration of use is due between the fifth and sixth anniversaries, and renewal between the ninth and tenth, and every ten years after. Write the arithmetic.
3. Work out the grace period: six months after the window closes, with a surcharge, for a US registration.
4. Check the mark's status on the office's public record, and record the date read.
5. Mark the window as open, not yet open, or closed. If it has closed without a filing, flag it for the decision-maker at once. Do not file anything.
6. Put the next deadline on `knowledge/register.md` with its rule and its citation.

Checks before you finish:
- The registration date comes from the record.
- Each window and grace end shows its arithmetic.
- The status is checked on the public record, with the date read.
- Nothing was filed, paid or renewed.

Example (invented, for illustration): "LARCHMONT FLOW, US, registered 2021-03-16. Declaration window 2026-03-16 to 2027-03-16, open now. Grace ends 2027-09-16 with a surcharge. Status on the USPTO record read 2026-10-01: live."
