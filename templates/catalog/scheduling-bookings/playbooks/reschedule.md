# Reschedule

Triggered by a task with a customer's request to move or cancel a booking. Budget 10 minutes. The outcome is options that fit the rules and a draft reply, in front of the person who confirms.

---

1. Read the request and the booking. Check the cancellation window and any deposit under `knowledge/booking-rules.md`.
2. Find two or three open slots that fit the rules and the customer's limits. State the effect on any deposit or fee exactly as the rules say; never waive one.
3. Draft the reply with the options. If the request breaks a rule, say so and route it to the person who decides exceptions.
4. Attach both to the task. Apply the change only when your Tools allow it and a person confirms.
