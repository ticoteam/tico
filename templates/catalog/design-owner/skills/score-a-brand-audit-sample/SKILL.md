# Score a brand audit sample

When to use: the monthly brand audit, or an audit of one surface (the website alone, for example) that needs a score and a short list of fixes.

Steps:
1. Build the sample: twelve to fifteen items published in the period, from the surfaces the book names. Take the most-seen items first (the home page, the pricing page, the top emails by send, the top posts by reach). Add two or three from the rest so the sample is not only the front door. Record the count and how you chose.
2. For each item, check four things against the brand book: voice, words, claims, visual. Score each pass, minor or miss. A minor is a fix a reader would not notice. A miss is a line a reader would notice, or a fact that is wrong.
3. Quote the line for every miss, as it is and as it should read, with the rule it breaks.
4. Rank the misses by audience size first, then by harm. A wrong price or a retired plan name outranks a tone slip, whatever the audience.
5. Choose three fixes. Each names the item, its owner, the rule, and a line a person can apply in minutes. Keep the rest on a short list of minor items, not in the three.
6. Compare with last month's three fixes. Each is done, slipped or dropped. A slipped fix stays on the list with its date.
7. Write the score table: surfaces down the side, the four checks across, with counts of pass, minor and miss.

Checks before you finish:
- Each item has four scores, and each miss has a quoted line.
- The sample size and how it was chosen are stated.
- There are three fixes, each with an owner.
- Any surface you could not see is listed as not checked, never as clean.

Example (generic): "14 items, one month. Website, 4 items: 1 miss on claims (a plan name retired on the first of the month), 1 minor on voice. Email, 5 items: 2 misses on words. Chosen fixes: pricing page (claims, owner: pricing owner), welcome email (words, owner: email owner), app listing (visual: the old logo, owner: app-store owner). Not checked: the sales deck, which no one attached."
