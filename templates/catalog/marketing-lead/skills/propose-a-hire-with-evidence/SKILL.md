# Propose a hire with evidence

When to use: the same kind of request keeps arriving with no owner, or a workstream has sat at "no
owner" for two summaries running, and the task asks whether a hire is needed.

Steps:
1. Gather the evidence: the task id and date of each request of the same kind. Three in one month,
   or a workstream at "no owner" for two summaries, is the threshold this team uses.
2. Check the team first. Read `hub team show` and the marketing templates (`hub template list`), and
   confirm the work is not already covered by a bot or a person.
3. Pick the one template whose job matches the recurring work. Read its card for its first routine
   and what it owns.
4. Write the proposal on the task in five lines: the template; the recurring work and its evidence;
   its first routine; who it reports to (the marketing lead); and what a weekly review would cost a
   person.
5. Ask the owner one question on the task. Keep one proposal open at a time.
6. Create the set-up task only after the owner says yes, and only when the task asks for it and a
   granted Tool allows it.
7. If the owner says no, record it with its date in `memory/decisions.md`. Raise it again only with
   new evidence.

Checks before you finish:
- The evidence names task ids or dates, not a feeling.
- The weekly review cost to a person is stated.
- Nothing was created before the owner's yes.
- Only one proposal is open.

Example (generic): "Three requests for ad results came in during September (task ids and dates
listed), and no paid media owner exists. Proposal: the paid media template; first routine, a weekly
review on Mondays; reports to the marketing lead; about 20 minutes of a person's review each week.
One question asked of the owner. Nothing is created until the owner says yes."
