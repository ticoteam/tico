# Build a filing calendar line

When to use: when you add a recurring filing or payment to `knowledge/tax-calendar.md`, when a notice adds a deadline, or when a date changes.

Steps:
1. Name the filing, the jurisdiction, the period it covers, and who files it (an outside accountant, the payroll provider, or a person).
2. Take the due date from the authority's own calendar or the notice itself. Note the page and the date you read it. Apply the
   authority's weekend and holiday rule and write the adjusted date.
3. List the inputs the filer needs: sales by place for the period, payroll filings, the trial balance, the contractor list. For
   each one, name its owner.
4. Set the lead time (the team's default is 15 days, from onboarding) and the date the inputs are due to the filer: the due date
   minus the lead time.
5. Give the line a status: ready, due now (inside the lead time with an input open), or late. Only "due now" and "late" lines go
   to the top of the report.
6. Ask each owner of an open input on their task, one question per task.

Checks before you finish:
- The due date has a source and the date it was read.
- Every input has an owner and a status.
- The filer receives the inputs before the lead date, not on the due date.
- A recurring line says what happens next period, so it does not depend on memory.

Example (invented, for illustration): "Quarterly sales tax return, State B. Due 2026-10-31 (authority calendar, read 2026-10-29).
Filer: outside accountant. Inputs due to the filer 2026-10-16 (15-day lead time): sales by place for July to September (owner:
Billing Specialist, ready); payroll filings (owner: Payroll Specialist, open). Status: due now."
