# Listening practice

The standing method behind `playbooks/weekday-sweep.md`, `playbooks/look-up-a-topic.md` and `playbooks/plan-the-social-week.md`.
`watchlist.md` and `sources.md` hold the team's own queries and sources. This file holds what is true about listening in
general. Sources were read on 2026-11-01 through search excerpts unless marked otherwise.

## Query precision

- Build one query for each job: a reply, a competitor move, or a content idea. A hit proves only what its query was built
  to show (practice).
- Shape: a main term, a context term the post must contain, and a short exclusion list. A long exclusion list means the
  main term is weak (practice).
- Prove an exclusion by flipping it to AND for one pass and reading what it would drop. A common word can sit inside a
  real mention (practice).
- Precision is the share of items pushed to a person that the person acts on. Measure it on a fixed sample drawn the same
  way each week. The sample sets the error: for a true 5 percent rate, the 95 percent error is about 4 points at 100
  items and about 3 points at 200. For a rate near 10 percent, it is about 6 points at 100 items (normal approximation
  to the binomial, arithmetic only). Report the count with the rate when the sample is small.
- A forced single label gives a poor read on a post that is honestly two things. Keep an "ambiguous" label (practice).

## Alerts and feeds

- An alert interrupts a person. A feed collects for a digest. Keep them apart. Judge an alert by what people act on; judge a
  feed by coverage and categories (practice).
- Urgency wears out. A queue people stop reading has a precision problem, whatever its point estimate (practice).

## Competitor moves

- A page diff, a sitemap date, a headline, a job post or a complaint is a signal, not a move. A move changes a price, a
  package, ownership, a product, a market, a service that closes, or who is in court (practice).
- Confirm on the company's own page, on a second load. A/B tests, prices shown by location or currency, staged rollouts,
  seasonal banners and date-only updates all create false diffs (practice).
- One complaint is a complaint. A pattern needs several independent sources on the same issue in the same window
  (practice).

## Public data, platforms and personal details

- Public is still personal data. Someone who collects personal details from public profiles becomes a controller, with a
  lawful basis, privacy information and duties to keep it short. The ICO says a scraper takes on controller duties; its
  generative AI pages are about training and their dates vary (ICO, "Political campaigning in the online world", read
  2026-11-01 via search excerpts).
- Most large platforms prohibit automated collection without permission in their terms. Read each platform's current
  terms before a source is added. This pass could not re-verify the platform terms (the platform domains were not
  readable from the search tool).
- Redact before anything is saved: home addresses, unit numbers, personal phone numbers and emails, door codes, guest
  names, and anything about children. A phone number in a public post is not consent to call or text that person
  (practice; check local law).
- Keep a saved item short: the link, the minimum visible text needed to decide, and the reason it matters.

## Disclosure before a human replies

- A reply that names the company, links it, compares it favourably or recommends it is an endorsement. A material
  connection (a job, a founder role, a free service, a payment) is said in plain words in the same message (FTC,
  Guides Concerning the Use of Endorsements and Testimonials in Advertising, 16 CFR Part 255, revised 2023; read
  2026-11-01 via search excerpts).
- On interactive platforms, the disclosure has to be unavoidable. A label that appears only on a profile is not enough
  (FTC 2023 guides and FTC staff guidance, via search excerpts).
- Paying for a review that must be positive is not allowed, even with a disclosure. The FTC Consumer Reviews and
  Testimonials Rule (16 CFR Part 465) took effect on 21 October 2024, and civil penalties apply to knowing violations (FTC,
  "The Consumer Reviews and Testimonials Rule: Questions and Answers", via search excerpts).

## Coverage

- Each source is returned, blocked or not read. Blocked means unknown coverage, never nothing found (practice).
- Read only the sources the team has authorised. Never use another identity or a signed-in session that belongs to someone
  else (practice).
