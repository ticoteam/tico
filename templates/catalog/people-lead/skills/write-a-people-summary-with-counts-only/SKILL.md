# Write a people summary with counts only

When to use: each time you write the weekly summary, or any count, table or headline that goes to its readers.

Steps:
1. Start from the sources, not from memory: the headcount plan, the people calendar, the roster from `hub team show`, the open
   people tasks, and each people bot's newest report. Write the source and its date beside each count.
2. Mark what you could not read. A role with no source is "status unknown", never "on track". A bot you could not read is
   named, and any count that depends on it says so.
3. Check each group before you show it. If a group has fewer than five people, merge it into a neighbouring group or report it
   only as part of a total. Check the group next to it too, since a total can reveal a hidden one.
4. Remove personal detail. Keep first names or references, roles, teams and dates. Leave out salary, health, family,
   immigration status, conduct and performance. If a task carried one of these, do not copy it, and write one line saying
   that an item was left out because it was personal.
5. Write the headline first: hires against plan in one number, and the count of deadlines in the next 14 days.
6. Trace every line. Each line names its source and date, and a figure with no source is left out.

Checks before you finish:
- Every count has a source and a date.
- No group of fewer than five people is shown by name or by a rare attribute.
- No salary, health, family, immigration, conduct or performance detail appears.
- Unknown and unread items are labelled as such, never as zero.

Example (generic, invented): "Hires against plan: 3 of 5 planned for Q4 filled or in offer (source: headcount plan 2026-09-28;
recruiting pipeline report 2026-09-25). One role has status unknown: no hiring task found. Leavers in the next 30 days: 2
(source: people-ops tracker 2026-09-28). Both are in teams under five, so they appear only in the company total."
