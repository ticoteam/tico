# Verify a prospect contact

When to use: before a contact goes on a list, into a sequence, onto a calendar invitation or into a brief as the buyer.

Steps:
1. Read the contact's record and its source. A row in a source list is not proof that the person exists or holds the role.
2. Match on two independent fields, such as name plus organization, or email plus owner name. A single match is not enough when another field disagrees: skip the contact and record why as ambiguous.
3. Apply the outreach flag on every row you read. A filter can miss a restricted record, so check each one.
4. Check the role from a current, dated public source. An older source may name a former role.
5. Check the do-not-contact list, the customer list and any open deal with another seller before adding the contact to anything.
6. Check the channel. Use a business email or phone for the purpose. Do not text anyone without an opt-in path.
7. Record what you checked, and when. A contact with a failed or unfinished check is not verified.

Checks before you finish:
- The match uses two fields, or the contact is marked ambiguous.
- Each row carries its outreach flag and its check date.
- Role and organization come from a dated public source.
- Do-not-contact, customer and open-deal checks are recorded.

Example (invented, for illustration): "Contact: operations lead at a regional accounting firm. Owner name matches the source row; email domain matches the firm's site (read 2026-10-14). Role confirmed on the firm's leadership page dated 2026-09. Outreach flag: allowed. Not on the do-not-contact list. Verified for email only; no phone on a dated source."
