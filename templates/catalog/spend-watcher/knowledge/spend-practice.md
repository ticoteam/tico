# Spend practice

Standing reference for software and cloud spend: the weekly report, budgets and renewals. The team's own vendors, owners and
thresholds live in `vendors.md`, `thresholds.md` and `renewals.md`. This file holds general practice, each item with its source
and the date it was read. Where a source disagrees with the team's record, the team's record wins for the team's facts.

## Cloud cost measurement

- Short lookback windows (7, 30 or 60 days) describe the past and do not forecast the next period. Secondary FinOps guides, read 2026-11-01.
- Size commitments to the steady baseline, not the peak. Secondary guides, read 2026-11-01.
- Rightsize before committing. A commitment on oversized resources locks in the waste. Team practice, supported by secondary guides.
- A peak-CPU downsize rule (for example, under 60 percent) appears in a vendor's calculator, not in a standards body's guidance. Use the team's own
  threshold, written in `thresholds.md`, and verify any vendor figure before quoting it.
- Coverage targets for commitments vary between sources. No single figure is quoted here.

## Budgets and variances

- Compare complete periods. A month still open is preliminary. Team practice.
- Explain each material variance with its cause and an owner. A variance with no cause is a question, not a guess. Team practice.

## SaaS and renewals

- Notice periods differ by contract. Read the clause; do not rely on memory or on a vendor's dashboard alone. Team practice.
- A seat is unused only with a dated usage record. Team practice, and the rule in AGENT.md.

## Anomalies

- Processing payments usually settle within a few business days. Treat one as a finding only after about a week unsettled. Team practice from
  operating experience.
