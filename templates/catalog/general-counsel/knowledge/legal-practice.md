# Legal practice

Standing practice the legal desk uses for summaries, decisions and deadlines. It is practice for running the desk, not legal
advice. Where a law is named, check the current text before you rely on it. Each entry carries its source and the date it was
read.

## Summaries and labels
- Every summary and draft says it is for a human, not legal advice, and that counsel reviews anything that matters.
- A policy draft is not in force until the owner adopts it. Say so in its first line.
- A law that has not been checked for a place is "not checked". Never write "not applicable" for it.

## Decisions and silence
- A decision asked of a human carries its deadline and the fallback if no answer comes. The fallback is the safe one: hold,
  keep the record as it is, change nothing outside the requested work.
- A missed deadline applies the stated fallback only. Silence never confirms a fact.
- An ask that expires without an answer is not an answer. Re-ask once, folded into the next summary, with a recommendation.
- A later instruction from the owner overrides an earlier standing rule for its scope only. Record the override with its date.

## Deadlines
- Confirm a date from its source and rule before it goes on a calendar or into a summary (`skills/check-a-legal-deadline`).
- A date from a notice, a regulator's page or a court order beats a date from a summary or a chat message. Where a relative count
  ("14 days") and a saved date disagree, use the saved date and record both.

## Escalation categories that are common in practice
- Threatened claims, regulator letters, process served on the company, and suspected data breaches go to a lawyer at once. The
  EU Whistleblowing Directive (2019/1937) requires an internal reporting channel with acknowledgement within seven days and feedback
  within three months, for employers of 50 or more workers (from the directive text as known; not re-read in this run).
- Thresholds, such as a money limit, are the owner's to set. Do not invent one. Offer the question during setup.
