# Qualify an inbound lead

When to use: for each inbound lead, before a reply is drafted or a person is asked to act on it.

Steps:
1. Write down what the lead asked for, in their words, with the arrival time and the source. A blank form field is
   "unknown", not "no".
2. Score the team's criteria one at a time, each as met, not met or unknown, with its evidence: the sentence they wrote,
   the field they filled, or a public source with its date. Default criteria: problem (they describe one the product
   solves), fit (their size, sector and location against the ideal customer profile), timing (a decision in the next 90
   days, or a named trigger), buyer (the person can join a call or name who can), budget (a range given, or not asked yet).
3. Check the exclusions before the criteria: a customer, a competitor, a current opportunity with another seller, someone
   on the do-not-contact list, a student or job seeker, a vendor selling to the team. Each excluded lead gets its named
   reason and its route.
4. Pick one outcome: meeting (problem and fit met, timing or buyer met, and they want to talk); question for a person (they
   ask for a price, terms, security, legal, or a custom build); not a fit (a criterion is clearly not met, named); or stop
   (they asked not to be contacted, or they unsubscribed).
5. Write one line for the log: the outcome, the criteria that decided it, and the open question if there is one.

Checks before you finish:
- Each criterion is met, not met or unknown, with its evidence. "Unknown" never becomes "not met" without a stated reason.
- A fit lead with no timing signal is a meeting only when the buyer criterion is met. Otherwise the open timing question
  goes into the reply.
- A stop is recorded before anything else is done with the lead.
- The outcome is one of the four named.

Example (invented, for illustration): "Demo form, arrived 09:14. Problem: met (they wrote that scheduling takes two staff
a day). Fit: met (eight sites, a sector in the profile). Timing: unknown (no date given). Buyer: met (the operations lead
wrote and can join). Budget: unknown. Outcome: meeting. Open question for the reply: their timing."
