# Read why an inbound lead was lost

When to use: for each closed-lost inbound lead in the read-out's cohort, before any reason is counted.

Steps:
1. Start from the reason the seller gave, in the CRM or the note of whoever took the call, and record it as "given". Do not stop there.
2. Read the buyer's last messages, not the seller's notes. Look for what the buyer said about the decision: a cost, a
   date, a person, a missing feature, a silence.
3. Choose one primary reason from the fixed list in `knowledge/inbound-method.md`. Add a secondary reason only when the
   buyer said two things, each with its own evidence.
4. Attach the evidence: a short quote from the buyer, or a dated note, with its date. "No reason given" is allowed, with
   the last contact date, and is counted as its own reason.
5. Where the given reason and your reading differ, record both and say which one the evidence supports. Do not average
   them, and do not change the seller's entry.
6. Count reasons over the cohort only. A lead lost before the window is not in this read-out.

Checks before you finish:
- Each lost lead has one primary reason from the list, with evidence and a date.
- The buyer's words were read before the seller's note was trusted.
- Given and read reasons are both on the record where they differ.
- The count matches the number of closed-lost leads in the cohort.

Example (invented, for illustration): "Closed lost 2026-10-14. Given: price. Read: the buyer wrote on 2026-10-09 that their
current tool renews in March, not that the price was too high. Primary reason: timing (quote: 'we are locked in until
March'). Given and read differ; the evidence supports timing."
