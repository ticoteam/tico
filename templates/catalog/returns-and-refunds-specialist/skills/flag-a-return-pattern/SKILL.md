# Flag a return pattern

When to use: when a customer, a product or an order shows a pattern a human should look at: many returns, a weight or item mismatch, or the same product coming back for the same reason.

Steps:
1. Count from the ledger over a stated period (for example 60 days): returns, refunds, and the items involved.
2. Write the facts only: the count, the dates, the item and any recorded difference, for example weight on receipt against weight shipped, from the carrier or warehouse record.
3. Name what you cannot see: the reason is the customer's words or none; a warehouse check may be missing; a carrier record may be late.
4. Give one question for the human to answer, for example: "Does the warehouse weigh-in show the same difference?"
5. Send it to the owner in the task. Do not contact the customer, do not refuse the next return on the pattern alone, and do not use the words "fraud" or "abuse" in any customer-facing text.
6. For a product problem, count returns per units shipped in the same period. Report a rate only with about 30 or more units shipped; otherwise report the count.

Checks before you finish:
- The flag states facts, a period and a count.
- No accusation is in the text, and nothing is sent to the customer.
- A rate appears only with about 30 or more units, otherwise a count.
- The flag names the human who decides.

Example (invented, for illustration): "Customer ref C-88: 5 returns in 60 days (2026-08-01 to 2026-09-29), 4 with a recorded weight on receipt 300 g below the weight shipped. Not readable: warehouse weigh-in for 2 of the 4. Question for the owner: does the warehouse weigh-in show the same difference? No action taken on the customer."
