# Filing practice

Standing practice for keeping a compliance calendar. It is practice for the desk, not legal or tax advice. Every date here is a starting point to confirm on the authority's own page, with the date read, before it goes on the calendar.

## Recurring filings (confirm each on its page)
- US federal income tax return for a C corporation (Form 1120): due the 15th day of the fourth month after the tax year ends, which is 15 April for a calendar-year company. An extension to file (Form 7004) gives six more months to file, not to pay. From memory; not re-read in this run.
- Delaware annual franchise tax report and tax: due 1 March each year. From memory; verify on the Delaware Division of Corporations page.
- California Statement of Information: the frequency and window depend on the entity type. Secondary sources seen earlier in this build conflict. Verify on bizfile for each entity, and do not rely on a date from this file.
- UK confirmation statement (CS01): filed at least once in every 12 months. From memory; verify on Companies House.

## Beneficial ownership reporting (US)
- Secondary alerts seen in search earlier in this build (Ballard Spahr and Morrison Foerster, August 2026) say a 2026 final rule exempts US-organized entities from the FinCEN beneficial ownership report. Check FinCEN's own page before you rely on this, and record the date read.

## Lead times
- The defaults are 60, 14 and 3 days before a due date (`playbooks/onboarding.md`). A filing with a fee, an inspection or a broker questionnaire needs more lead time. Write the longer lead time in its register row.
