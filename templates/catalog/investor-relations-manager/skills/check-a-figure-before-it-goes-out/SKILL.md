# Check a figure before it goes out

When to use: for every figure in an investor update, an answer to an investor question, or a board pre-read. The check takes a few minutes and prevents the
correction email that follows a wrong number.

Steps:
1. Read the figure from its source, not from a summary of it. Note the source and the date of the reading.
2. Tie it to a second source where one exists, for the same definition and period. Revenue from the KPI reading should match the billing export for that period once deferred and accrued amounts are reconciled (advance billing and revenue recognition often explain a gap). A cash figure should match the
   bank statement or the finance input. A difference the reconciliation does not explain is not averaged: both values and both sources go to the owner, and the figure is held.
3. Compare with last month, on the same definition. If the definition changed, say so and restate the prior month on the new definition, or leave the
   comparison out.
4. For a move over 20% month on month, find the reason in a dated source (a meeting, a weekly update, a billing change). If none is found, write "reason not
   found" and name the question to ask the owner.
5. Check the arithmetic in the table: totals, percentages and the difference against plan. A percentage is computed from the two numbers shown, not from
   a rounded one.
6. Check the figure against the exclusions list: no customer name, deal or person attached to it.
7. Write the figure with its unit, its period and its source in the update. A figure that failed a step is "not supplied" in the draft, with the reason.

Checks before you finish:
- Every figure has a source and a reading date.
- No figure is estimated, rounded up to a round number, or carried from an older update without a date.
- Each move over 20% has a reason with its source, or "reason not found".
- The draft is marked incomplete at the top if any figure is "not supplied".

Example (invented, for illustration): "Cash 412k at 2026-08-31, from the owner's bank statement (2026-09-02). It does not tie to the finance input of 2026-08-31 (409k). Both values and both sources go to the owner; the line reads 'not supplied' until one is confirmed."
