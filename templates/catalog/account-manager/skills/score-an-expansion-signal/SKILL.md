# Score an expansion signal

When to use: when you review a customer's usage against their contract, read a call for stated goals, or are asked whether an
account should be offered more.

Steps:
1. Name the candidate in one phrase: more seats, a higher usage tier, a module, a second location, a new team.
2. Gather evidence. Usage against the entitlement from the contract, over at least four weeks, from an export or a dated read.
   A stated goal from a call or a written message, with its date and who said it. Note both if you have them.
3. Check the entitlement. What the contract allows, and whether the extra usage is inside a fair-use or overage clause.
4. Rate the signal:
   - strong: usage above entitlement for at least four weeks, plus a stated goal that needs it;
   - medium: one of the two, with the number and date;
   - weak: a feeling, a single spike, or usage that the customer has said is temporary.
   Propose only strong and medium signals. Weak ones are noted, not offered.
5. Check health. If customer-success has the account as red, the signal waits. It goes to the Customer Success Manager first.
6. Write the opportunity in one line: the account, the evidence with number and date, the option (for example "true-up to 45 seats
   at renewal"), and the rating. No evidence, no line.
7. Attach the price, if any, only from the rules or the request. Otherwise it is a marked gap.

Checks before you finish:
- Every number has its source and date.
- No opportunity is proposed on a hunch, a single spike or a red account.
- The customer's own words are quoted exactly where a goal is cited.
- The option matches the contract's terms (seats, tiers and modules as written).
- The rating is stated, with the reason.

Example (invented, for illustration): "Harbour Studio: 42 active instructor seats on a 30-seat plan in each of the last six weeks
(usage export 2026-09-27). The 2026-09-22 call names a second class schedule from January. Rating: strong. Option: true-up to 45
seats at renewal, price `[price: pricing owner]`. Health: green per the 2026-09-25 note."
