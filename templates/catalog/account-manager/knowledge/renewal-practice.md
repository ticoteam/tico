# Renewal practice

Standing reference for renewals, notice and expansion. Facts about this team's own contracts live in
`renewal-rules.md`; this file holds general practice and dated benchmarks, each with its source and year. Where the two
disagree, the team's own signed terms win.

## The deadline is the notice date

- The date that matters is the last day a notice can be given, not the renewal date. Read the notice clause in the signed
  contract: how many days, in writing, to whom. Count back from the anchor the clause names (the renewal date or the term end date) and record both dates.
- Start the renewal conversation early enough that the customer's procurement and signature fit before the notice date.
- Check the notice clause for auto-renewal terms. Some contracts renew unless cancelled before the notice date; the CRM
  field may say "auto-renew" without the notice days.

## Timing

- The team's start point is set at setup (default: 120 days out). It is a planning default for the calendar stages, not an
  industry benchmark.
- A renewal first looked at in its last month has little room for a change of terms. The stages in the weekly review exist
  for that reason.

## Retention context (dated benchmark)

- SaaS Capital's 2025 B2B SaaS retention benchmarks (published September 2025) give a median net revenue retention of 102%
  for companies with annual contract values of $25,000 to $50,000. The highest-value companies show the highest gross
  retention. Read the full report for the other bands before you quote any number.
- Use benchmarks as context for an account plan. They are not a target for one customer.

## Automatic renewal law (check before you rely on it)

- California's automatic renewal law (Business and Professions Code section 17600 and following) was amended for agreements
  formed, altered or extended on or after 1 July 2025. Law-firm summaries from 2024 and 2025 describe express consent,
  yearly reminders, clear notice of material price or service changes, and cancellation through the channel used to sign up.
  The law protects consumers, meaning individuals buying for personal, family or household purposes. Business customers are
  generally outside it, but their contracts still follow the notice clause. The statute text was not read for this file.
- The FTC's click-to-cancel rule was vacated by the Eighth Circuit in July 2025 (Custom Communications, Inc. v. FTC). The FTC
  can still act on unfair or deceptive renewal practices under Section 5 and the Restore Online Shoppers' Confidence Act
  (law-firm summaries, 2025). Check the current status before you rely on it.

## Expansion

- Usage above the contract is the strongest expansion evidence. A goal the customer stated in a call, with its date, is
  next. A feeling that they could buy more is not evidence.
- An expansion proposed without a usage number tends to read as a sales push. Quote the number and its source in the option.

## Ownership

- Name one owner per renewal in the calendar. A renewal with two names, or none, is the usual way one slips past its notice
  date. Team practice.
