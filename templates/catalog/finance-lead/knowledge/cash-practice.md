# Cash practice

Standing reference for cash forecasting and the finance calendar. The team's own numbers, banks and owners live in
`team.md`, `thresholds.md` and `cash-forecast.md`. This file holds general practice, each item with its source and the date
it was read. Where a source disagrees with the team's record, the team's record wins for the team's facts.

## Forecasting method

- The 13-week forecast is a direct forecast: cash in and cash out by week, line by line. A forecast built from profit is for
  planning, not for weekly cash.
- Roll it forward every week and compare last week's forecast with actual cash, line by line. Secondary guides suggest near
  weeks come from documents and later weeks are honest estimates. Team practice applies this.
- Keep a variance log: each miss with its cause, owner and date. Team practice.

## Cash definitions

- Say which cash a figure is: bank available (spendable today) or books cash after the cut-off. They differ by timing,
  unposted items and holds. Team practice: every summary states which one it uses.
- A balance feed is a snapshot. A reconciliation matches entries across sources. Team practice.

## Collections and timing

- Forecast collections from payment history, not from invoice terms. Secondary guides recommend a customer's average days to
  pay as the basis. Read 2026-11-01.
- A payment can take about one business day to post on both sides, and some take several days to settle. Team practice from
  operating experience.

## Finance calendar

- Each close, payroll, tax and filing date has one owner and one source. A date with an input still open a week before is at risk.
- Payroll follows actual pay dates. Tax payments follow the statutory due date for each jurisdiction the team pays in. Verify
  the dates for those jurisdictions when they are set up.
