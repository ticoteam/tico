# Score a request

Use this for every new request or idea that reaches the product team, before anything is proposed to build.

## Steps
1. Restate the request as the outcome a customer or the business gets, in one sentence. If you cannot, ask the requester once.
2. Score each dimension on the evidence, not on taste:
   - **Reach**: how many customers or segments it touches, from the Feature Owner of that segment (their count, with its date).
   - **Impact**: what changes for them, and which goal in Tico it serves.
   - **Confidence**: how much evidence supports the other two: interviews, usage, support tickets. Say what it is built on.
   - **Effort**: the estimate from the Engineering Manager. If none, say "not estimated" and ask for one; do not guess a number.
3. Compare with the roadmap. Say what it would displace, and what it would wait behind.
4. Route it: a spec or discovery to the segment's Feature Owner; a build plan to the Engineering Manager.
5. Write the score in one line per dimension, with its evidence and its date, in `knowledge/prioritisation-practice.md`'s format.

## Never
- Score a dimension with no evidence as anything but "unknown".
- Promise a date for the request.
