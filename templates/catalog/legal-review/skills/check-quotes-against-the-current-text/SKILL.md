# Check quotes against the current text

When to use: before any clause, date, number or name from a contract goes into a summary, a letter, a note or a task, and
before you reuse a clause citation from an earlier document.

Steps:
1. Name the version you quote: the exact file under review, its date and its page, and whether it is proposed (awaiting signature) or executed (signed). A CRM summary or an old letter is not the text.
2. Copy the words exactly, in quotation marks, with the section number. Do not paraphrase inside the quotes.
3. Open that version again and confirm the number points to the words you quoted. Section numbers move between versions.
4. If the words come from an earlier letter or template, check every clause it cites against the version under review. An old letter
   can carry a wrong number, or words that version does not have.
5. Mark any quote you could not check against that version as "unverified" in the output.
6. Record the date of the check beside the quote.

Checks before you finish:
- Every quote names its version, page and date.
- The words are exact, and the section number was checked again.
- Reused citations were checked clause by clause.
- Unverified quotes are marked, not left looking verified.

Example (invented, for illustration): "Clause 16.2: 'the Customer shall indemnify the Supplier' (signed agreement, p.7, checked
2026-11-01). An earlier letter cited 16.1 for the same words. The current text has them at 16.2, so the citation is corrected
here and flagged to the owner."
