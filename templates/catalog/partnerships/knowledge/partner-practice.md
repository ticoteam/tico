# Partner practice

Standing method for a partner channel. The team's own agreements and rules of engagement (`rules-of-engagement.md`) win
where they differ.

## Registrations

- A registration is valid when its required fields are present, the prospect is in scope and the partner is eligible.
  The protection clock starts only when the registration is complete.
- Conflicts resolve in the order the rules set. The common rule is that the first complete registration wins, unless our
  own seller already has an open, dated opportunity with the prospect. Name the rule each time.
- Protection has an end date. Tell the partner before it ends, with the next step.

## Tiers and fit

- A tier is a set of duties and benefits written into the agreement, not a title. Review tiers on a dated schedule, when
  volume or fit changes.
- Fit is who the partner serves, what they do with us and how big they are. Write one sourced sentence per candidate.

## Referral and affiliate terms

- Get legal review of the terms and the outreach before the first send. The reviewed text is the text that ships; a
  change to it goes back to review.
- Disclose the incentive where the audience would expect it. The FTC's Guides Concerning the Use of Endorsements and
  Testimonials in Advertising (16 CFR Part 255, revised 2023) cover endorsements and paid or incentivised promotion. Check
  the current text before you rely on it.
- A number cleared for one segment or one page is cleared only there. Use the same cleared wording everywhere it appears.

## Partner health

- A partner with no registered or won deal in 90 days gets a dated next step, or its agreement is allowed to lapse. A
  person decides which.
- A registered deal that goes quiet past its threshold gets one update with a new reason, not a reminder.

## Terms before resale or integration

- Read the terms of every layer: the product, the provider behind it, and any intermediary. A permitted layer does not
  clear the layer above it.
- Automated or non-human access is often restricted separately from the product itself. Check it before any test.
