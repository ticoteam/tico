# Check a partner claim

When to use: before any partner pitch, referral email, co-marketing text or partner page uses a number, a promise, an endorsement or a comparison.

Steps:
1. Find the claim's exact wording in the copy and quote it.
2. Find the cleared text for it: the legal or compliance review on the task, with its date. If none exists, the claim is not cleared.
3. Compare the wording. A changed word, a dropped qualifier or a new number is a new claim that needs its own review.
4. Check the scope of the clearance: which audience, page or segment it covered. A claim cleared for one segment is not cleared for another.
5. Label what is known: stated by the partner or by us (with the page and date), or tested by us (with the test and date). A claim with neither label is marked "unverified".
6. Use the same cleared wording everywhere the claim appears. If two cleared versions differ, use the newer one and say so.
7. When you rewrite a block of agreed text, list each old line as kept or dropped. A dropped line that was cleared goes back for review.

Checks before you finish:
- Every quoted claim has a cleared source and date, or is marked unverified.
- No claim reaches a wider audience than its clearance covers.
- Each dropped line from a cleared block is named.
- An incentive or endorsement is disclosed where the audience would expect it.

Example (invented, for illustration): "Partner email, line 3: 'Most partners cut onboarding time by a third.' No cleared text for this line. Marked unverified; replaced with the dated figure from the partner survey, or dropped. Line 4 uses the cleared range of 20 to 35 per cent, with the same wording as the partner page."
