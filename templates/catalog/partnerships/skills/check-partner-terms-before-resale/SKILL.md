# Check partner terms before resale

When to use: before a partner or integration resells access, routes requests through an intermediary, or runs automated or agent access to a product.

Steps:
1. List every layer: the product's owner, the provider behind it if different, and any intermediary or marketplace between them and the customer.
2. Read each layer's current public terms, and record the date read. Note the clause on each of: resale or sublicensing, sharing access with customers, automated or non-human access, and use of outputs.
3. Check the direction. A permission at one layer does not clear the layer above it, and an intermediary passes upstream terms on to its customers.
4. Where a term is unclear or prohibits the route, mark the route blocked. Ask the owner for written clarification before any test.
5. Check whether the access is self-serve or needs a grant. Do not call a route testable until the grant exists.
6. Label each capability as stated by the provider or tested by us, with the URL and date.
7. Do not sign up, accept terms or spend on a provider's account without a human's written yes.

Checks before you finish:
- Every layer has its terms and read date recorded.
- Each restriction that applies has its clause quoted.
- A blocked route is named as blocked, not as pending.
- No account, acceptance of terms or spend happened without a written yes.

Example (invented, for illustration): "Partner X resells a model through intermediary Y. Layer 1, provider terms read 2026-10-20, section 4: sublicensing prohibited. Layer 2, intermediary, section 5: upstream terms pass to customers. Resale route blocked. A direct, granted integration is the only route to test."
