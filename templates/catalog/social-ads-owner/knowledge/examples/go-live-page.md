# Go-live: a new Meta ad set for yoga studio owners, three no-show ads

GX-22 · Social Ads Owner · Acme, studio booking software

## What goes live

A new ad set, "Yoga owners: no-shows", in the kept Meta campaign "Studio trials". It runs three image ads, each
testing one idea. All three link to acme.example/reminders.

(The three ads as they will look, in the feed, are attached: ad-a.png, ad-b.png, ad-c.png.)

1. **A, the pain.** Image: an empty mat in a full class photo. Headline: "Empty mats cost you money." Text: "Text
   reminders cut no-shows at studios like yours. Try Acme free for 14 days." Button: Start free trial.
2. **B, the proof.** Image: the chart from our 2026 reminder report. Headline: "No-shows down 31% in 2026." Text:
   "1,200 studios on Acme sent text reminders this year. Here is what changed." Button: Learn more. (The 31% is from
   our own reminder report, dated Oct 2026, linked on the landing page.)
3. **C, the offer.** Image: the Acme booking screen on a phone. Headline: "Your first 3 months at half price." Text:
   "For new yoga studios on Acme, through Dec 31." Button: Start free trial. (The offer is on the price page, dated
   Nov 1, 2026.)

- **Audience:** studio owners in the US who follow yoga teacher pages and run a business page. About 410,000 people
  (Meta's estimate, Nov 13). Current Acme customers left out, from the CRM list of Nov 12.
- **Budget:** $60 a day, $420 a week, $1,680 in all. New money: the campaign's weekly spend goes from $980 to $1,400,
  under its ceiling of $1,500. No other ad set's budget changes.

## The bet

- **Metric:** cost per new customer from social ads (KPI), read as cost per paid studio from this ad set: Meta spend,
  new customers from the CRM.
- **Prediction:** this ad set's cost per new customer from $190 (the campaign's 30-day average) to $150 by Dec 15.
- **Why:** yoga studios are 38% of new customers but 12% of social spend (CRM and Meta, 30 days to Nov 10). The one
  live yoga ad has run 61 days; its click-through fell from 1.4% to 0.8% and its frequency rose to 3.1 (Meta, Nov 10).
  Rivalbook runs four no-show ads aimed at yoga studios (Meta Ad Library, checked Nov 11).
- **Cost:** $1,680 in ad spend. About 15 minutes of Sam's time to set it up.
- **Runs:** 4 weeks, Nov 17 to Dec 15. A winner among the three ads is called only at 30 trials each and 7 days.
- **Stop early if:** cost per trial passes $120 (twice the campaign's 30-day average of $60) for three days in a
  row, or the ad set spends $500 with no trial. The Social Ads Owner pauses it and says so on this task.

## Checks

- **Brand check:** passed. Design Owner, GX-23, Nov 13. Claims checked: the 31% (reminder report, Oct 2026) and the
  half-price offer (price page, Nov 1, 2026).
- **Who puts it live:** Sam (runs the ads account). The Social Ads Owner has read access only.
