# Prepare a growth call

## Steps
1. Read `tico growth setup`, `tico growth numbers --months 3` and `tico growth channel list`.
2. Write the brief in the chat, five lines at most:
   - What Growth shows now, in one sentence.
   - The three questions to ask, most valuable first (the order in `skills/growth-interview/SKILL.md`).
   - Who else could answer each, if this person can't.
3. On the call, ask them one at a time. After it, run `skills/numbers-from-chat/SKILL.md` on the
   transcript and post what you saved in the chat.
