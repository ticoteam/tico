# Pick what to try next

When to use: before `playbooks/propose-ideas.md` step 3, when fewer than three ideas are in Ideas,
or when someone asks what to try next.

Steps:
1. Read the position: `tico growth numbers`, `tico growth channel list`, `memory/learnings.md`.
   Write down the stage (no customers yet, first customers, a channel that works, several that work),
   the monthly budget, and who has time (from `tico team show`).
2. Pick two or three families from the table for that stage and budget. Prefer one the team has
   not tried and one that builds on what already works (`skills/what-already-works/SKILL.md`).

   | Stage | Free or cheap | With budget |
   |---|---|---|
   | No customers yet | waitlist with refer-a-friend, founder posts where buyers already talk, early-access price | small search ads on the highest-intent terms |
   | First customers | founder-led outreach, comparison pages against named alternatives, a free tool or calculator, customer-language copy | search ads, sponsoring one newsletter the buyers read |
   | One channel works | case studies from the best customers, referral programme, integration or partner listings, review sites | the next paid channel, webinars or events with a partner |
   | Several work | programmatic pages, community, annual report or benchmark, awards | conference talks, brand campaigns, new countries |

3. Match the timeline: ads, email and posts read in weeks; content, search and community in
   months; brand and partnerships later. Say which an idea is.
4. For each family, find a signal on this team (a customer quote from `knowledge/customers.md`, a
   competitor move from `tico market delta`, a number). No signal, no idea.
5. File the ideas with `playbooks/propose-ideas.md` step 3, each with the channel Owner whose
   number it moves.

Checks before you finish:
- No idea repeats a test in `memory/learnings.md`; a follow-up tests the same belief another way.
- At least one idea costs nothing but time.
- Each idea says when it can be read (weeks or months).
