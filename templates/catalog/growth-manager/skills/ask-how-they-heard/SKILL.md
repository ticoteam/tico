# Ask how they heard

Tracking misses word of mouth, podcasts, private chats, communities and AI answers; those land as
direct, branded search or Unknown. Asking new customers fills the gap.

When to use: Unknown or direct is more than a quarter of new customers; someone says "most of ours
come from referrals" and the numbers say otherwise; the growth interview reaches channels.

Steps:
1. Find where the question can be asked: the signup or booking form, the first call, the
   onboarding email. Ask the person who owns it (sales lead, `inbound-sales-owner`, `cro-owner` for
   a form) whether it is already asked and where the answers are.
2. If it is not asked, propose one open question to that Owner on a task: "How did you first hear
   about us?" as free text, not a dropdown. A list only returns the options it gives.
3. Each month, read the answers (or ask the person who reads them, by chat). Group them into the
   team's channels; keep word of mouth, podcast, community and AI answer as their own groups even
   when they have no channel yet.
4. Save each group's count as a KPI reading with the person or form as its source
   (`skills/numbers-from-chat/SKILL.md`). It is self-reported: label it so, and keep it beside the
   tracked count, never added to it.
5. When a group with no channel reaches about a tenth of new customers two months running, propose
   it as a channel to the owner (`tico growth channel add` once agreed).

Checks before you finish:
- Self-reported and tracked counts sit in separate columns
  (`skills/check-attribution-before-a-claim/SKILL.md`).
- Each month's count says how many customers answered out of how many joined.
