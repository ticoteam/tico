# Dispatch practice

The standing method behind `playbooks/tomorrows-dispatch-plan.md`. `crews.md` and `job-types.md` hold this team's facts.
This file holds what is true about field dispatch in general. Each figure carries its source and the year, and is
checked at its source before it is quoted in a plan.

## First visit

- The share of jobs fixed on the first visit is the usual measure of planning quality. A 2025 benchmark (Aquant, as
  summarised by secondary sources; the primary report was not read) puts the median near 75 percent over a 30-day
  window, the top fifth near 86 percent and the bottom fifth near 53 percent.
- Measure over 30 days. A 7- or 14-day window splits one repeat visit into two tickets and flatters the result (same
  secondary source).
- Older surveys agree on the level: Aberdeen Group, 2013, about 75 percent; The Service Council, 2016 data, 76 percent.
- A first visit most often fails on parts or access that were not checked before the day. Check those in the plan
  first. Skills come second.

## Arrival windows

- No controlled study of arrival windows or no-shows was found when this file was written (2026-11-01). The window the
  team promises is its own decision. Set it from observed drive times and job lengths.
- Track the real arrival against the window for every job, in the close-out. That is this team's own measure. Do not
  compare it with an industry figure you have not read.
- A vendor claim that real-time schedule changes lift on-time arrival from 67 to over 90 percent has no source. Do not use it.

## Job length

- Planned length is the median of completed jobs, not the booking default. The 80th percentile sets the buffer before a
  fixed-time job. See `skills/estimate-a-job-length-from-history/SKILL.md`.

## Close-out

- A job is not complete until its completion note, required photos and any sign-off are on the record. Open close-outs
  age quickly, so the next plan lists them by technician.

## Sources

- Aberdeen Group, field service survey, 2013, as cited in a secondary PDF, read 2026-11-01.
- The Service Council, Field Service Business Assessment, 2017 summary of 2016 data, read 2026-11-01.
