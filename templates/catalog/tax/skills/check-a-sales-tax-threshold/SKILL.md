# Check a sales tax threshold

When to use: when you update the threshold watch, when sales into a place grow, or when someone asks whether the team must register somewhere.

Steps:
1. Name the place and the rule as it stands today, from its revenue department's page. Record the page and the date you read it.
   Rules differ: some use revenue alone, some also count transactions, some treat marketplace sales differently, and some have
   changed recently. Do not rely on a remembered rule.
2. Find the test window first. Some rules look at the current or prior calendar year, others at the trailing twelve months.
   Write which one applies, then take sales for that window only.
3. Take sales for that window from the sales export, on the basis the rule uses (gross or taxable sales, sales shipped to
   that place, marketplace sales if the rule counts them).
4. Count transactions for the same window, only where the rule still counts them. A place that has dropped the transaction
   test is measured on revenue alone.
5. Compare the amount with the threshold and state the percent. Mark places at 80 percent or more for escalation.
6. Record the official source, the date checked, the window and any change since the last check in `knowledge/thresholds.md`.
   A place with no data is "no data", never zero.

Checks before you finish:
- Each threshold cites its official source and the date checked.
- The basis (revenue, transactions, marketplace) and the window are written next to the figure.
- Places at or over 80 percent are at the top of the calendar.
- No registration is recommended as a conclusion. Write "over threshold; accountant to confirm".

Example (invented, for illustration): "State A: 94,300 of a 100,000 revenue threshold, trailing twelve months, marketplace
sales excluded (source: revenue department page, checked 2026-10-29). At 94 percent, so escalated. Accountant to confirm the basis."
