# Tax practice

Standing reference for the tax calendar, registration thresholds and contractor forms. This team's own registrations,
deadlines and filers live in `tax-calendar.md`, `thresholds.md` and `contractors.md`. This file holds general practice, each
item with its source and the date it was read. It is not tax advice; the accountant decides any treatment.

## Economic nexus for sales tax

- Most US states with a sales tax have an economic nexus rule: a sales threshold, sometimes with a transaction count. Secondary
  summaries read in 2026 counted about 45 states plus DC with such a rule. Verify each state's rule on its revenue page before
  registering anywhere.
- A common revenue threshold is $100,000. Some states set other figures. New York uses $500,000 together with a transaction
  count (verify).
- Several states have dropped the transaction-count test in recent years. Secondary summaries listed South Dakota and Louisiana
  (2023), North Carolina and Wyoming (2024) and Alaska (January 2025), with more changes in 2025 and 2026. Check each state's
  current position.
- Marketplace sales are treated differently by state. Some count them toward the threshold; others exclude them where the
  marketplace collects the tax. Check the rule for each place.

## Filing calendars

- Due dates come from the authority's own calendar. Weekend and holiday shifts follow that authority's rule.
- Inputs reach the filer before the deadline, by the lead time the team set (default 15 days, from onboarding). Team practice.

## Contractor forms

- Collect each contractor's tax form when the contractor is set up, not in January. Team practice.
- The reporting amount for information returns changes by year. Read the authority's current figure each year. A remembered
  figure is not a source.

## Sources

- Secondary summaries of state economic nexus rules and transaction-count changes, read 2026-11-01. Verify each state's current
  rule on its revenue page before relying on it.
- Team practice items need no external source.
