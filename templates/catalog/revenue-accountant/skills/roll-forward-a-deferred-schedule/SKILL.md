# Roll forward a deferred schedule

When to use: each month at close, and when a contract is added or changed mid-term.

Steps:
1. Take each contract's opening deferred balance from last month's schedule.
2. Add this month's billings for that contract from the billing export, net of credit notes.
3. Subtract this month's release. Release follows the team's policy for the kind of revenue: ratably by day over the service
   period for subscriptions, on delivery for one-off services, and as the policy says for setup fees. Cite the policy section.
4. Write the closing balance. Check that opening plus billings minus release equals closing, for each contract.
5. Add the contract totals and compare them with the deferred revenue account in the ledger after the proposed entries.
6. Any remainder is a listed difference with its cause: a missing invoice, a wrong period, a credit note not yet applied. Never a plug.

Checks before you finish:
- Every contract row ties, line by line.
- The total ties to the ledger, or each difference has a cause.
- Each release cites its policy section.
- Contracts whose schedule lines are estimated are marked "estimated".

Example (invented, for illustration): "Contract C-104, 12,000 annual, billed 2026-01-01. Opening deferred 9,000; billings 0;
release 1,000 (ratable, policy 2.3); closing 8,000. Ties. Total difference against the ledger: 240. Cause: a credit note of 240
posted in the ledger on 10-02, not yet in the billing export."
