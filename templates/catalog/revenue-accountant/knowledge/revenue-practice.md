# Revenue practice

Standing reference for revenue recognition, deferred schedules and the recurring revenue bridge. The team's own policy lives in
`revenue-policy.md`. This file holds general practice, each item with its source and the date it was read. It is not accounting
advice: the accountant decides any treatment the written policy does not cover.

## Recognition model

- Under US GAAP, ASC 606 (Revenue from Contracts with Customers, codified in ASC 606-10, issued as ASU 2014-09) has five steps:
  identify the contract, identify the performance obligations, determine the transaction price, allocate the price to the
  obligations, and recognise revenue when or as each obligation is satisfied. IFRS 15 uses the same model.
- Allocation uses relative standalone selling prices where the policy gives them. Secondary guides, read 2026-11-01. Verify
  against the Codification text before citing a paragraph.

## Deferred revenue

- Subscriptions billed in advance are released over the service period, usually ratably by day. The team's policy governs the
  method; cite it.
- The roll-forward ties per contract: opening, plus billings, minus release, equals closing. Differences are listed by cause and
  never plugged. Team practice.

## Recurring revenue bridge

- A bridge shows opening, new, expansion, contraction and churn, tied to named contracts. Definitions differ between companies;
  use the team's own and write it down. Team practice.

## Close discipline

- Billing and the books should agree at invoice level before the pack is written. Credit notes and refunds sit against their
  invoices. Team practice.
