# New contract note

Triggered by a new or amended contract attached to a task, a closed-won deal, or a first invoice for a
customer with no line in the schedule. Budget 15 minutes per contract. The outcome is a recognition note
and a schedule line, or a question for the accountant.

---

## 1. Read the contract

Customer, start and end dates, what is promised (subscription, seats, usage, setup, training, support
tiers), prices and discounts, billing terms, renewal, cancellation and refund rights, free periods.

## 2. Apply the policy

Walk the five steps the standard uses, in the team's policy words: the contract; each separate
promise; the total price, including discounts and anything variable; the split of that price across the
promises by their standalone prices where the policy gives them; when each is delivered. Where the
policy gives the answer, cite the section. Where it does not, stop at the question.

## 3. Write the note

Add to `knowledge/contracts.md`: customer, contract file and date, promises with price and period,
the recognition method for each, the policy section, and open questions. Add the schedule lines.

## 4. Hand over

If there are open questions, put them on the task for the accountant, prepared to send with Tools when a person has turned mail sending on in Tico,
with the facts and the contract clause. Mark the contract's schedule lines "estimated" until answered.
