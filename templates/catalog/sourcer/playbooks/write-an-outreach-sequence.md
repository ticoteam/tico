# Write an outreach sequence

Triggered by the slate, or by a task that names a person to approach for a role. Budget 10 minutes a person.

Inputs: the person's public profile link and the one piece of their work that matches a required criterion; the role file;
the sender named in `state.md`; and the pay range and location rule from the role file, where the team allows them to be
stated.

Outcome: three short messages for one person. Each one goes out only when the task asks for it, a granted Tool allows it, and
a person has turned mail sending on in Tico. Until then they are drafts, with planned dates in `knowledge/contact-log.md`.

Done when: all three touches are written and checked, the planned dates are logged, and the person is either in the sequence
or left out with the reason in the contact log.

---

## 1. Read the person's public work

The profile link and one piece of their work that matches a required criterion: a project, a talk, a write-up, a repository.
If you cannot find one specific thing, the person does not get a message. A generic note does worse than none.

## 2. Touch one (day 0), under 90 words

1. The specific work, in one line, and why it caught your eye.
2. The role in one line: what the person would do first at {{company_name}}.
3. The range and location or remote rule, where the team allows it.
4. One easy question ("Open to a 20 minute chat next week?").

Sign as the sender named in `state.md`. No flattery, no "exciting opportunity", no pressure.

## 3. Touch two (day 5), under 60 words

Something new: a line from the hiring manager on the problem, a detail about the team, or a link to something the team
published. Never "just bumping this".

## 4. Touch three (day 12), under 40 words

Close the loop politely and leave the door open ("If the timing is wrong, no reply needed"). After this, stop and log it.

## 5. Check and hand over

Read all three as the recipient: accurate, specific, nothing promised. Follow `skills/write-a-three-touch-outreach/SKILL.md`
for the checks. Send touch one only when the task asks for it, a granted Tool allows it, and a person has turned mail sending
on in Tico. Otherwise keep it as a draft on the task. Record the planned dates of the other touches in
`knowledge/contact-log.md`. A "no" at any point ends the sequence the same day.
