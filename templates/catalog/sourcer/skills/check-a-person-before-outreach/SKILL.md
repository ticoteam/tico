# Check a person before outreach

When to use: for each person before they go on a slate, before a first message, and before any follow-up.

Steps:
1. Check `knowledge/do-not-contact.md`. If the person or their organisation is listed, stop. Do not write, do not add to the
   slate, and do not record their details beyond the reason for the listing.
2. Check `knowledge/contact-log.md`. Anyone contacted in the last six months, or who has said no or "not now", is skipped
   unless the "not now" named a month to follow up and that month has come. A planned follow-up of a sequence still open for this role (touch two on day 5, touch three on day 12) is not a new contact: check the log for a reply or a no, and continue only if there is none.
3. Check the person is not a current candidate, a current employee of the team, or a person the Recruiter already holds. Use
   the Recruiter's pipeline line, not memory.
4. Read only the public, work-related source: the profile, the talk, the repository, the write-up. Do not read a private
   profile, and do not open a page that needed a login.
5. Match the required criteria only. Each one is shown (with the public link), partly shown, or not shown. Never record
   "not shown" as "no".
6. Check the person's data for anything you should not hold: a personal email, a phone number or a home address the person did
   not publish for work; a photo used to judge; a school or a name used as a stand-in for background. Leave each one out, and do
   not copy it into a file.
7. Note where the data came from (the page, its date) so the notice can name it, if the law requires one. See
   `knowledge/sourcing-practice.md` for the notice rule.

Checks before you finish:
- The person passed steps 1 to 3.
- Each required criterion has a result with a public link.
- No protected characteristic, proxy for one, or personal contact detail is in the file.
- The source and date are recorded for the notice.

Example (generic, invented): A conference speaker whose talk shows a backfill tool in production (criterion shown, link to the
talk, dated 2026-10-14). Not on the do-not-contact list. Last contact: none. Not a current candidate. The speaker's personal
email appears in the talk's footer; it is left out and not recorded. Ready for a slate with its source line.
