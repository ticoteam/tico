# Fill an outbound NDA from the template

When to use: when a requester asks for the team's NDA to go to a party.

Steps:
1. Find the template named in `knowledge/templates.md`, with its file and version date. If it is not listed, stop and send the request to `legal-review`.
2. Take the party's legal name, notice address and purpose exactly as the requester gave them. Record the source: the task, its message and the date.
3. Fill each blank in the template. Do not change any clause text. List every blank filled, with its value and source.
4. Ask the requester once, on the task, for any value still missing. Never guess a legal entity name or an address.
5. Check the mutual or one-way choice against who will share.
6. Save the draft under `reports/ndas/` and attach it to the task. Nothing is sent.

Checks before you finish:
- The template version is named.
- Every blank has a value and a source, or a question open on the task.
- No clause text differs from the template.
- The draft is marked as a draft, not sent.

Example (invented, for illustration): "Template: mutual NDA, team paper, version dated 2026-08-01. Party: 'Northwind Studios Group Ltd' (task 4127, requester's message). Notice address: not given, so a question is open on the task. Purpose: 'a 30-studio rollout discussion' (requester's words)."
