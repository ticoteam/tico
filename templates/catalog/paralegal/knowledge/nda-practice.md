# NDA practice

Standing practice for checking and preparing NDAs. It is practice for the desk, not legal advice. Each figure names its source and year.

## What NDAs usually say
- Common Paper's Contract Benchmark 2022 found that 52% of the NDAs in its benchmark had a one-year confidentiality period, and 15% had no time limit. 51% had a one-year NDA term. Its standard terms leave out residuals. Use these as a comparison when you write a variations list, not as a target.
- Trade secrets are often protected for as long as they stay secret. That is usually a separate clause from the confidentiality period.
- A mutual NDA fits two parties who both share. A one-way NDA on a two-way discussion is a flag.

## Clauses that need a human
- Residuals (a right to use what people remember): always a flag, unless the variations list allows it.
- Non-solicit and non-compete: flag. Send to `general-counsel` if they reach staff or customers beyond the discussion.
- Unlimited confidentiality for ordinary information: flag.

## Whistleblower immunity notice (DTSA)
- Under 18 U.S.C. 1833(b), an agreement that governs trade secrets or confidential information with an employee, consultant or independent contractor should include the immunity notice. The duty covers agreements made or updated on or after 11 May 2016.
- Commentary says that if the notice is missing, the employer cannot recover exemplary damages or attorney fees under 18 U.S.C. 1836(b)(3) against that person. The statute was not re-read for this file. Check its current text before you draft.
- Pointing to a company policy may not reach a contractor who never received the policy. Put the notice in the agreement.
