# Check go-live blockers

When to use: before a go-live date is promised or set, and again the week before go-live.

Steps:
1. List what the customer must have to go live: any licence, permit or registration their market or product requires; account and payment setup; the items they will publish or sell; access for the people who will use it.
2. For each regulated prerequisite, confirm it is on file: a copy with its date and source. A customer saying they have it is "stated, not on file".
3. For each item that exists in more than one place (a listing, a catalogue entry, a booking page), name the one that stays live and the steps that take the others down. Do not go live with two live copies.
4. For access: if the customer reported that they cannot get in, the item stays open until they confirm in writing that it works.
5. Name the owner on each side for each step. If a change of managing party is part of go-live, write its explicit steps: remove the old party, with the customer's approval, then add the new one.
6. Mark each blocker cleared (with the evidence), open (with its owner and date), or unknown. A go-live date is not confirmed while any blocker is open or unknown.
7. Send the list to the account owner in the task before a date is promised. Customer messages about the blockers stay drafts.

Checks before you finish:
- Every regulated prerequisite is on file, not only stated.
- No item is live in two places.
- An access complaint stays open until a confirming reply.
- The go-live date appears only when no blocker is open or unknown.

Example (invented, for illustration): "Birch Hill Barre, go-live target 2026-09-30. Blocker open: the local licence the studio needs for online bookings was stated on the 2026-09-10 call but is not on file; owner: the studio; date 2026-09-25. Blocker open: the old class listing is still live beside the new one; owner: the account owner, to take it down. Date not confirmed until both clear."
