# Check a handoff before kickoff

When to use: when a deal closes and before the kickoff agenda or the onboarding plan is written.

Steps:
1. Find the deal: the CRM read, the closed-won task, the order form and the sales call notes. Note each source with its date.
2. Go through the six items in `knowledge/onboarding-practice.md`: who signed and who will use it; what they replace and where their data lives; their goal in their words; every promise with its source; the operating fields (day-to-day settings, the schedule or inventory owner, who approves changes); a named human on each side for each milestone.
3. For each item, write found (with its source and date), gap, or conflict (two sources disagree). Do not fill a gap from memory.
4. Check the promises against the standard milestones in `knowledge/milestones.md`. A promise the list lacks gets its own milestone, with its source.
5. Check the dates. A promised go-live or training date earlier than the standard duration is flagged to the owner in the task before the plan is written.
6. Write each gap as a kickoff question, one line, in the customer's terms.

Checks before you finish:
- Each of the six items is found, a gap, or a conflict. None is left blank.
- Every promise carries its source.
- Gaps are questions for the kickoff, not assumptions in the plan.
- The operating fields are checked by name, since handoffs often skip them.

Example (invented, for illustration): "Cedar Barre, closed 2026-09-25. Found: signed by the owner; goal in their words from the 2026-09-18 call ('members booking online before October'). Promise: a data import from their old booking tool (deal note 2026-09-18). Gap: operating fields. No one is named as the weekly schedule owner. First kickoff question: who updates the class schedule each week?"
