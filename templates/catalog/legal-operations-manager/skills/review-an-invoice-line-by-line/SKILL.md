# Review an invoice line by line

When to use: when a law firm invoice arrives on a task, before any spend summary or payment decision is written.

Steps:
1. Find the firm in `knowledge/firms.md` and the matter in `knowledge/matters.md`. If there is no engagement letter on record, say so at the top and review against `knowledge/billing-rules.md` only.
2. For each time entry, write down the date, timekeeper, role, hours, rate, amount and the description as written.
3. Check the rate against the letter for that role and that date.
4. Check the units against the agreed increment, default 0.1 hour.
5. Flag block billing: several tasks in one entry with one total.
6. Check scope: does the work belong to this matter and its agreed scope?
7. Flag non-billable work under the rules: admin, filing, training, fixing the firm's own errors, internal conferences beyond the rule.
8. Flag duplicates: the same work on two dates or by two people, with no reason given.
9. Check each expense against the rules, its receipt and the limit.
10. Recalculate every line (hours times rate) and the total. Record any difference.
11. At the top of the review, write the amount billed, the amount in question, and the main reason.

Checks before you finish:
- Every line was read, not sampled.
- Each questioned line names the rule it breaks and the letter or rule line with its date.
- The amount in question is a separate figure, never deducted from the amount billed.
- No line is judged on the quality of the legal work.

Example (invented, for illustration): "09-19, associate, 1.5 h at $285 = $427.50. The letter sets $250 for associates from 2026-01-10. In question: $52.50, because the rate differs from the letter."
