# Write a pulse readout

When to use: after a pulse survey closes and its groups have passed `skills/check-a-group-before-reporting/SKILL.md`.

Steps:
1. Open with the headline in one or two sentences: participation against the invited count, and the one or two biggest
   changes since last quarter, with their size and their base.
2. Participation: overall and for each group above the threshold, against last quarter. Name any group with low participation.
3. Scores and trends: each question this quarter, last quarter, and the change. Compare identical questions only. Show the two
   largest rises and the two largest falls first. Changes within the noise rule in `knowledge/survey.md` are called noise.
4. Themes from comments: four to seven themes, each with its count and the groups it came from. Quote only comments that identify
   nobody. A comment about harassment, safety, discrimination or someone at risk is not a theme: route it under
   `skills/route-a-serious-comment/SKILL.md`, and say only that it was routed.
5. Actions: two or three, each tied to a finding, with one owner, a date, and one line on what employees will be told.
6. Close the loop: last quarter's actions, each with done, late or dropped, and whether employees were told.
7. Sources and gaps: the export, its date, the groups not shown, and anything you could not read.

Checks before you finish:
- The first line is the answer, not the method.
- Every trend compares identical questions, and any changed question is named.
- Each action has an owner, a date and what employees will be told.
- Every number has its base.
- Nothing shows a group below the threshold, and no comment identifies anyone.

Example (invented, for illustration): "Q3 pulse: 31 of 42 answered (74 percent, up from 66 percent). 'Workload' fell 0.4 points on the
5-point scale, the largest fall; 'I know what good work looks like' rose 0.3, the largest rise. Actions: publish promotion
criteria per level (owner: the people lead, by 2026-11-15, told to employees in the next all-hands). Last quarter: rota fix done
late by one day; no-meeting Wednesdays done and announced."
