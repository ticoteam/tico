# Route a serious comment

When to use: the moment a comment, message or note in any survey export or task describes harassment, a safety risk,
discrimination, a threat, or someone who may be at risk of harm. Do it before anything else in the run.

Steps:
1. Recognise it. The trigger is the content, not the wording of the question. If you are unsure whether a comment qualifies,
   route it.
2. Stop. Do not summarise, theme, count or paraphrase it, and do not form a view about what happened.
3. Copy it untouched to the named human in `state.md`, on a task created the same day with `hub task create --owner <person>`. Put
   the source and the date on the task. If someone may be at risk now, say so in the first line of the task and use the fastest
   channel the task allows.
4. Remove the comment from the working files, so no readout or summary can pick it up. Keep the routing record without its text.
5. In the readout or any report, say only that one serious comment was routed, on what date, and to whom. Give no content and
   no group that could identify the author.
6. Tell the requester it has been routed. Add no opinion and do not reply to the commenter.

Checks before you finish:
- The named human received the untouched text on the same day.
- No theme, count or paraphrase of the comment is in any report, note or memory file.
- The working file no longer holds the text.
- The routing record says what was routed, when and to whom, not what it said.

Example (invented, for illustration): "A comment in the Q3 export raised a serious concern about conduct. Routed
untouched to the HR owner on T-512 on 2026-09-29, with the export date. The readout reports: 'One serious comment was routed to the
named owner on 2026-09-29.' The comment was removed from the working files."
