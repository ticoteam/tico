# Score account health

When to use: each weekly brief, before a renewal stage review, or when a person asks why an account is at risk.

Steps:
1. Confirm the lifecycle stage. An account still in onboarding is scored against its onboarding plan, not against adoption. An account that is paused or cancelling is scored as cancelling and its terms go to the Account Manager.
2. Check for a waiting state. If the account is blocked on a step the customer owns and the request has a date, mark it "waiting on customer" with that date. Do not score it red for that step before the date passes. Step 6 still applies: a hard signal sets red whatever the wait.
3. Read each family in `knowledge/health-practice.md`: adoption, support, relationship, commercial. For each, write green, yellow, red or unknown, with one dated fact.
4. Apply the rules in `knowledge/health-rules.md`. If that file has no rule for the case, use the starting rules in `knowledge/health-practice.md` and say so.
5. Give the status and the two facts that drive it. Name every family that is unknown.
6. Look for a hard signal that overrides the rules: a stated intent to leave, a champion who left with no successor named, or a failed payment unrecovered past the team's limit. A hard signal sets red and goes to the account owner the same day.
7. Record the date and sources in the account note. A change since last week reads "moved from X to Y", with the fact that moved it.

Checks before you finish:
- Each status has two dated facts behind it.
- No family is scored from a source you could not read. Unknown is written as unknown, never as green.
- A usage trend compares windows of the same length.
- Waiting on customer has a date.
- A customer's own words about leaving are quoted, not turned into a metric.

Example (invented, for illustration): "Cedar Barre: yellow. Adoption yellow: weekly active instructors down from 22 to 17 over 30 days against the prior 90 (usage export, 2026-09-27). Support yellow: four open tickets, the oldest 19 days (support queue, 2026-09-28). Relationship unknown: no call since 2026-07-14 and no champion named. Commercial unknown: no billing export. Yellow, not red: two families are yellow and none is red."
