# Write a customer next touch

When to use: for each yellow, red or waiting-on-customer account in the weekly brief, or when a task asks for a message to a customer.

Steps:
1. Name the reason for the touch in one line, from the health note: the fact that moved the status.
2. Choose the sender: the account owner who will send it, named by role in `knowledge/renewals.md`. The draft goes to that person for review, not to the customer.
3. Open with the customer's goal in their words, or with what they asked for. Do not open with "checking in".
4. Make one ask, with a date: a 20-minute call, a question they can answer in a line, or a decision by a date.
5. State only facts from a dated source. Make no promise of a fix, feature, credit or discount unless the team has recorded it.
6. Mark every price, term or date you do not have as a gap: `[price: Account Manager]` or `[date: account owner]`.
7. Count the words. Under 100. Cut anything that repeats the brief.
8. Put the draft on the task with the sender's role, the reason and a send-by date. It leaves only when a person sends it, or when mail sending is on in Tico and the task asks for it.

Checks before you finish:
- Under 100 words, one ask, one date.
- No "checking in", "just following up" or "as you know".
- Every price, term and date is sourced or marked as a gap.
- No promise of a feature, credit, discount or fix.
- No personal detail beyond what the health note already holds.

Example (invented, for illustration): "Hi [contact name], fewer instructors have used the schedule since August, and I wanted to ask how the new team is finding it. Could we find 20 minutes this week to look at the class plan together? [date: account owner]". Sent by the account owner after review.
