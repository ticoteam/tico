# Payroll practice

Standing reference for pay run cut-offs, changes and post-run checks. The team's own pay dates, approvers and baseline live in
`payroll-calendar.md`, `approvals.md` and `baseline.md`. This file holds general practice, each item with its source and the date
it was read. Employment and tax rules differ by place; the team's provider or counsel decides them, not this file.

## Cut-off

- Payroll providers commonly set a cut-off two to five business days before the pay date, so changes can be checked and entered.
  A secondary vendor guide (RosterElf, undated page), read 2026-11-01, gives that range. Verify the team's own cut-off with its provider.
- Lock the pay period at cut-off. Changes after it wait for the next run or an off-cycle run.

## Changes and corrections

- Each correction needs a justification and evidence. Secondary guides name retroactive entries as a common source of payroll errors.
  One vendor survey (about 24 percent of organisations reporting) is quoted in secondary coverage. Treat it as a pointer only.
- Timesheets not approved by their source are held as exceptions, not paid by default. Team practice.
- An off-cycle run needs a named manager's confirmation. Team practice.

## Post-run checks

- Compare the register with the approved summary after every run, person by person. The register is the record the books use.
  Team practice.
- Update the baseline only after the check passes, with the register date. Team practice.
