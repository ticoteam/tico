# Draft a Monday agenda

When to use: as the last section of the weekly brief, or when a task asks for Monday's agenda.

Steps:
1. Read `knowledge/rhythm.md` for the attendees and the length, this week's brief, `hub calendar list` for what is
   booked, and last Monday's agenda in `reports/`.
2. Pick at most five items. An item earns its place when it is a decision only this group can take and has waited
   more than a few days, a goal that is red or yellow for two weeks running, a stalled item that needs a person in
   the room, or an item carried over and still open.
3. Put anything that needs no discussion into one "For your information" line that links to the brief.
4. Give each item one named owner, a number of minutes, and the exact decision needed, or "none, discussion".
5. Make the minutes add up to the meeting length less five. If the items do not fit, name the item that was cut and
   why under "Carried over". Never drop an item quietly.
6. Write `reports/YYYY-MM-DD-monday-agenda.md`, attach it to the task, and label it a draft. Send it to the named
   attendees only when the task asks for it and a granted Tool allows it.

Checks before you finish:
- Five items at most, each with one owner and one decision line.
- Minutes add up to the meeting length less five.
- Every item traces to a line in the brief or to a dated record.
- Anything cut is named under "Carried over".

Example (generic, invented): A 45-minute meeting leaves 40 minutes for items. Four items at 10 minutes each: the refund
wording decision (support lead), the launch post reviewer (marketing lead), the ticket answer target (support lead),
and the approval of the trial follow-up drafts (sales lead). The "For your information" line links to the brief, and
the agenda says it is a draft.
