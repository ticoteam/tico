# Check a grant before it is recorded

## When to use
Before a grant goes into `knowledge/grants.md` or `knowledge/cap-table.md`, when a person asks whether a grant is in order, and in the monthly check for every grant made since the last one.

## Steps
1. Get the grant record: holder, class, number of options, grant date, strike, vesting terms, the board consent that approved it, the 409A used, and the signed grant letter.
2. Board approval. Find the consent or minutes that approved this grant, with its date. The approval date must be on or before the grant date. No approval found: the grant goes on the open list.
3. 409A age. Take the valuation date of the 409A in force on the grant date. Check it is within 12 months of the grant date. Check for a known material event since the valuation date (a financing term sheet, a tender offer, or a major change in the business). If there is a known event, or the 409A is over 12 months old, mark it "for counsel" and escalate.
4. Strike price. Compare it with the 409A fair market value on the grant date. At or above: fine. Below: escalate.
5. Pool. Subtract this grant from the pool left. The result must not be negative. Compare the result with any pool figure promised in an offer. Below the promise: escalate.
6. Signed grant letter. Check it exists, and that its number, strike and vesting match the record. A mismatch goes on the open list with both figures.
7. Write the result as what the documents show: "Approval dated X (minutes, item N). 409A valued X, within 12 months, no event noted. Strike X against 409A X. Pool left after grant X." Then the open items, each with its owner.

## Checks before you finish
- Every step names a document and a date, or says the document is missing.
- You did not call the grant valid, approved or priced in your own words.
- The pool arithmetic is written out, and the result is not negative.
- Any material event or missing approval is on the escalation list.
- You changed no record. The change goes in the log only after a person confirms the source.

## Example (invented)
Grant G-17: 25,000 options to a new hire, grant date 2026-09-08, strike 0.61.

- Approval: board consent dated 2026-09-02, item 4. Before the grant date.
- 409A: valued 2026-05-20. Within 12 months. A note dated 2026-08-30 says no financing talks since. Counsel to confirm before the grant is treated as priced.
- Strike 0.61 against 409A fair market value 0.61: at the value.
- Pool: 412,000 left before the grant, 387,000 after. Above zero. No offer promises a higher figure.
- Open: signed grant letter not on file. Owner: the Corporate Secretary.
