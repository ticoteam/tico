# Price the whole term

When to use: before you state a total cost, compare two quotes, or put a price in a comparison or a recommendation.

Steps:
1. Fix the term, the units and the currency: contract length in months, seats or units, tier. Convert a per-seat monthly
   price and a yearly price to the same basis before you compare them.
2. List each cost line from the quote, the order form and the public price page: subscription, seats above the quoted
   band, setup or onboarding, support tier, usage or overage, and any add-on the need requires. Note the payment terms;
   they change cash timing, not the total.
3. Add what the quote leaves out, and label each line "not in quote": integration work, training, the staff hours to run
   the tool, the renewal uplift (the clause and its percentage), and any minimum commitment.
4. Add the cost of leaving: the export format and whether it is free, the notice period in days, any early-termination
   fee, and deletion of team data on exit.
5. Sum the term. Show the term total and the monthly figure, both with the date each input was read.
6. Compare vendors on the same term and seat count. For each vendor, price its least expensive tier that meets the same
   requirements. Tier names differ between vendors, so match on features, not names. If no tier meets them, or the price
   is not stated, leave the row out and say why.

Checks before you finish:
- Every line has a source and the date it was read.
- All vendors are priced over the same term and seat count, each at its least expensive tier that meets the requirements.
- The uplift and the exit cost appear, or say "not stated".
- No vendor's own statement is labelled "verified".

Example (invented): Vendor A quotes 600 a month for 25 seats on an annual plan (pricing page, read 2026-09-28). Over 24
months that is 14,400. A 5 percent uplift in year two would add 360, if the contract allows it; the contract is not yet
signed, so the line says "not stated". The export fee is not on the pricing page, so it is marked "not in quote". Vendor B
quotes 800 a month. Its quote, dated 2026-09-20, states a 5 percent rise at renewal on page 2, so the 24 months are 9,600 in year one plus 10,080 in year two: 19,680 in total. The comparison
shows both totals and the two lines that neither quote states.
