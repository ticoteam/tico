# Check the cancel path

When to use: before any reply that answers a cancellation or downgrade request goes on the task.

Steps:
1. Name the exact way to cancel: the link, the screen, or the reply that completes it. Where the law of the customer's market requires it, use the same medium they signed up or requested in, or an easier one (see `knowledge/cancellation-practice.md`).
2. Say when billing stops and what happens to the current period: runs to its end, stops now, or is refunded. Use the policy's words. If the policy does not say, mark a gap for the owner.
3. Say whether a refund or proration applies, using the policy and the billing record, with its date.
4. If the customer declined the offer, the reply does not repeat it or make a second one.
5. Remove any guilt line, threat or "are you sure" that adds steps. One confirming question is allowed only where the cancel path itself needs it.
6. Check that someone who has never seen the product could follow it. If a step needs an account login or a support contact, say so.
7. Record the cancel path used on the reasons log line, so the next request is answered the same way.

Checks before you finish:
- The reply names one complete way to cancel.
- The billing stop date and refund status are stated, or marked as a gap.
- No second offer, guilt line or maze step is in the text.
- Where a rule applies to the customer's market, the owner has checked the reply before it is sent.

Example (invented, for illustration): "To finish cancelling, reply 'cancel' to this email, or use Settings, then Plan, then Cancel. Billing stops at the end of the current month, 2026-09-30, and you keep access until then. No refund applies to the current month under our policy."
