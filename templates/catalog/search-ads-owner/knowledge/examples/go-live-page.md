GX-22 · Search Ads Owner · Acme, studio booking software

## The bet

- **Metric:** cost per new customer from search ads (spend from Google Ads, new customers from the CRM).
- **Prediction:** the new ad group brings 8 new customers by Dec 22 at $150 or less each, and the channel's cost per new
  customer goes from $185 to $175 a month by Dec 22.
- **Why:** in the search terms report, 41 yoga scheduling searches matched our general "studio software" keywords in
  October and 6 became customers at $142 each (Google Ads and CRM, Oct 1 to 31). The general ads do not mention yoga.
  Two competitors run yoga ads on these searches (checked Nov 9).
- **Cost:** $40 a day, about $1,680 over 6 weeks. This ad group's own budget: no other campaign's budget is raised. About
  20 minutes of Sam's time to set it up.
- **Runs:** 6 weeks, Nov 17 to Dec 29. Measured on Dec 22, with a week for late signups.
- **Stop early if:** the ad group spends $600 with no new customer, or its cost per new customer passes $300 for two
  weeks in a row. The Search Ads Owner pauses it and measures then.
