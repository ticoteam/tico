# Test ad copy and landing page fit

When to use: before any new ad copy test starts, before a landing page is called a match for its search, and at each review while a test runs.

Steps:
1. Write the question in one sentence that names the measure and one idea: "Does a price-first headline lower the cost per repair against the service-first headline in Commuter repairs?"
2. Name the variants: two or three whole ads, each differing only in the idea under test. Hold the keywords, match types, bidding, budget and landing page the same, or the test is not readable. A landing page test is a separate test.
3. Check every claim in each variant against the Claims section of `knowledge/paid-search-method.md`. A price needs its period and its dated source.
4. Check fit before launch. For each ad group's theme, read the landing page's first screen. It must show the same service, the same price or offer, and the same action the ad promises. If it does not, the fit fails: route the page to `cro-owner` with the search terms and the ad text as evidence. Do not run a copy test to hide a page mismatch.
5. Size the test. The count per variant is the default in `knowledge/targets.md` (30 primary conversions). Estimate the weeks from the last four weeks of conversions for the ad group, split evenly. If that is more than eight weeks, it is not run as a test: make one change, read it at four weeks, and label the evidence as weaker.
6. Set the dates in `knowledge/tests.md`: the earliest read is seven days after launch or the day each variant reaches its count, whichever is later. Do not stop early because one variant looks good. Stop only when a variant is broken (disapproved or not delivering), and say why.
7. At the read, show each variant's conversions and cost per result side by side. Read the gap against its spread, using the Ad copy and landing pages section of the method file: with about 30 conversions in each variant, a gap under about 52 per cent is no winner yet.
8. Record the reading and the decision (keep, replace, or no difference yet) in `knowledge/tests.md` and `knowledge/copy.md`. A winner becomes the control for the next test.

Checks before you finish:
- The question was written before launch and names one idea.
- The count and the earliest read date are stated, and no call is made before either.
- Each variant's count is shown beside its cost, and the gap is read against its spread.
- The landing page was checked for fit, and any mismatch is routed to `cro-owner`.
- The result is in `knowledge/tests.md`, including "no difference yet".

Example (invented, for illustration): "Test: the price-first headline ('Commuter repairs from $49, pickup included') against the service-first headline ('Same-day repairs near you'), Commuter repairs on Google. The price has a basis: the price list dated 2026-10-28. It needs 30 repairs per variant, and the campaign books about 30 a week per variant at its current split. Launched 2026-11-09, so the earliest read is 2026-11-16; the look on 2026-11-12 is not a read. On 2026-11-16: price first 33 repairs at $36.40 each, service first 30 at $42.80 each. The gap is 15 per cent. One standard error for counts this size is about 25 per cent, so this is no difference yet. Keep both running and read again on 2026-11-30."
