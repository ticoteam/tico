# Pause, resume and discard a shared meeting

Tico Recorder on a Mac can share a meeting live to Tico. **Pause sharing** stops the shared part while the recording goes on
on your Mac. **Discard…** ends the shared meeting in Tico without filing it.

Paused speech stays in your local recording. Tico never receives it, in the shared transcript or in later corrections.

## Pause sharing

1. Open the meeting in Tico Recorder.
2. Click **Pause sharing**.
   The button changes to **Resume sharing**. The recording continues on this Mac.
3. Click **Resume sharing** to share speech again.
   The button changes back to **Pause sharing**.

If nothing happens when you click, wait a moment. A slow request to Tico can delay other controls.

## Finish or send after a pause

**Finish meeting** and **Send** refuse while the meeting is paused. Click **Resume sharing** first. Then Tico uploads
the speech you shared before the pause, and the meeting can be filed.

## Send a recording with paused speech

If the recording has paused speech, Tico asks you to review its kept action items before anything is sent.

1. Click **Send**.
2. Click **Continue to Send**.
   Tico then asks you to review each kept action item, with its title and quote.
3. For each item, click **Send reviewed item** to send it. Click **Dismiss** to leave it out.

Tico sends only the items you reviewed. The shared transcript never includes paused words.

## Discard a shared meeting

Discard ends the shared meeting in Tico without filing it. Your recording and transcript stay on this Mac. A discarded
meeting cannot be sent later, and its action items cannot be sent either.

1. Stop the recording.
2. Click **Discard…**.
3. In **Discard shared meeting?**, click **Discard**. To keep the meeting shared, click **Cancel**.
   The meeting shows **Discard pending · saved locally** until Tico confirms that the meeting has ended. Then it shows
   **Discarded · saved locally**.

If the badge still says **Discard pending · saved locally**, Tico has not confirmed the end yet. Tico Recorder keeps the
pending discard and retries it, even after a restart. The recording stays saved on this Mac while it waits.

For what happens to a sent meeting, see [Meetings](meetings.md).
