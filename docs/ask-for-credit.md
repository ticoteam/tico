# Contact support for more credit

**Unreleased:** this help describes the account page update. Deployment is still being verified.

The account page has no credit form. To ask for more credit, contact support.
Your account page links to it from the grace card and the balance card, and the credit emails give the same line.
The link opens [app.tico.dev/support](https://app.tico.dev/support).

1. Open [app.tico.dev](https://app.tico.dev/).
2. Select **Sign in to Tico** and sign in with your Tico account.
3. Select your company from the **Company** menu.
4. Select **Contact support for more credit**, or **Support** in the menu.

The support page lists your company’s support requests.

## If you cannot reach support

If you cannot find the company, check that you signed in with the Tico account that belongs to it.
If you see **No credit yet**, billing is not ready. Use **Support** on the account page if you need help.

After credit is added, check [your balance and server states](account.md).
Models resume within five minutes after enough credit is added. Server restart has separate credit requirements.
