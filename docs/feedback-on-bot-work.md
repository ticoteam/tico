# Rate a bot's work

Rate a bot's message, task or approval with **Good** or **Not good**.
A **Not good** rating can tell the bot what went wrong, or become a rule for that bot.

You can rate:

- Replies in your Assistant chat.
- Messages in threads and direct messages with a bot.
- Comments a bot posts on a task.
- Tasks a bot created.
- Approvals and questions from a bot in **Decide**.

Each person has one rating for each item.

## Rate a message

On a computer, move the pointer over the message. The buttons appear just below the words.
On a phone, press and hold the message to open **Message actions**.

1. Select **Good** (thumbs up) or **Not good** (thumbs down).
2. To take back a **Good**, select it again.

The **Message actions** menu has **Good**, **Not good** and **Copy**.

## Say what was wrong

Selecting **Not good** saves the rating at once. Closing the sheet keeps it. The sheet then asks what was wrong.

1. Type in **What was wrong?**, or select a reason chip to fill it in.
2. Select **Tell the bot**. Tico shows **Sent to bot**.

**Tell the bot** leaves a note for the bot's next run in that conversation.

On a task or a Decide item, the chips start with **Shouldn't exist**, **Badly written** and **Wrong person**.
Your last five distinct reasons follow them. If you have used a reason before, the sheet shows **You've said this before.**

For example, the Support bot's reply quotes last year's refund policy. Dana selects **Not good**, types "Quotes the 2024 refund policy", and selects **Tell the bot**. The Support bot gets that note on its next run in the same conversation.

## Make a rule

Select **Make a rule** on the **Not good** sheet to turn your reason into a rule for the bot. Tico drafts one sentence from the reason.

1. Edit the sentence in **Rule**. It takes up to 1000 characters.
2. To apply the rule to every bot, turn on **All bots**.
3. Select **Save rule**.

If you manage the bot, the rule is active. If you do not, the rule is suggested instead.

## Change or remove a rating

Select **Not good** on a message you already rated **Not good**. The sheet opens and shows your reason, or **No reason**.

- Select **Edit** to change the reason.
- Select **Remove** to delete the rating.

## If the buttons do not show

On a computer, move the pointer over the message. The buttons show when you hover over the message or move keyboard focus into it.
