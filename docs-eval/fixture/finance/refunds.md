# Refund policy

## Who can get a refund

- **Annual plans** can be refunded within 14 days of the payment.
- **Monthly plans** are not refundable. Canceling stops the next charge, and the workspace stays active until the end of the period already paid for.

## How a customer asks

The customer fills in the billing form in the help centre under Billing, Refunds. Support checks the date
and the plan and issues the refund in the billing tool the same business day.

## Approval

Support can approve refunds up to $500. Anything above $500 needs the founder's approval before it is issued.
